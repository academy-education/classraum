## Strand 2: comprehension by the whole-passage-variant method

### Why this method

Batch 2's comprehension options leaked (83.3% options-only, 5/6 unanimous)
against a comprehension-shaped control at 25.0%. **That control is itself
built by the symmetric-worlds method** (cohorts `isee-reading-worlds-s*`).
The SSAT pilots (REGISTER A76-A78) built the five-version whole-passage
method for exactly this leak: version k makes choice k correct on every
question, a hash draw after freezing picks the version students read, so
**over the draw the expected options-only rate is exactly chance whatever the
solver picks.** What the draw cannot protect, and where every SSAT pilot
failed, is naturalness (pilots 1-2) and difficulty (pilot 3: 9/12 easy,
because distractors were absent from the drawn version or flatly dismissed).

### What is built

- `map-wv.mjs`: a MAP copy of `ssat-wv.mjs` resized to MAP's shape. **Four
  versions, four choices, five questions per passage.** Break-tested before
  this commit (`--selftest`: three versions, a fake kill quote, two negations
  in one paragraph, an option absent from one version, a bad mix, three
  choices, grade-12 prose, an off-target kill quote all refuse; bars D and E
  fail at their margins; a flat or incomplete judge is discarded; control
  median 1 is INVALID).
- **Two units** by two fresh Claude authors, brief `MAP-WV-AUTHOR-BRIEF.md`:
  - `map-wv-p01.wv.json`, id `MAPWV-P01`: **literary** (narrative fiction),
    grade 6, RIT 190-199.
  - `map-wv-p02.wv.json`, id `MAPWV-P02`: **informational** (history feature
    about an invented town or person), grade 7, RIT 200-209.
- Pilot 4's difficulty rules (SSAT-READING-WV4-PREREGISTERED.md) are adopted
  from the start, since pilot 3's failure is the closest evidence: every
  option of every non-vocabulary question concerns things present in EVERY
  version (mechanically checked), wrong options are partial readings rather
  than dismissed candidates, at most one negation per paragraph, the kind of
  correct answer varies across questions, attitude words are never named.
- Mix per unit (checked): exactly 1 vocabulary-in-context, exactly 1 theme
  (literary) or central-idea (informational), at most 1 summary, 1-3
  inference, 1-3 point-of-view / attitude / purpose / structure. These are
  MAP's public sub-strands (MAP-RESEARCH §12).
- Shape (checked): 190-320 words per version, longest/shortest <= 1.3, 3-7
  paragraphs, Flesch-Kincaid in [4.0, 9.0) per version (batch 1's E1 band).

**Freeze -> hash -> draw.** The `*.wv.json` files are committed before the
draw; `frozenSha` = sha256 of their bytes in sorted path order;
`k = sha256("map-wv-2026-10-06|" + frozenSha + "|" + passage_id)[0:8] mod 4`.
Never re-rolled. Authors never see `map-wv.mjs`, any checker, any earlier
item, or this file; the orchestrator returns `verify` PROBLEM lines verbatim.

### Instruments

- **A options-only** (shared render with strand 1, below): the 10 drawn
  comprehension items, options only (stems and passages withheld, as batch
  2), three samples of one Claude solver.
- **C + F with-source** (shared render with strand 1): the two graders above,
  batch 2's schema, on the drawn passages.
- **D cross-version validity**: one fresh Claude reviewer on all 4 versions x
  5 questions x 2 passages = **40 question-versions**: `valid` only if this
  version makes the claimed answer the unique best answer and refutes or fails
  to support every other choice; `absent` (choices the version never
  discusses, recorded); one `cross_item` note (recorded).
- **E naturalness, relative**: two fresh Claude judges each see 8 unlabelled
  passages, shuffled by `map-wv.mjs build`: the 2 drawn candidates and the **6
  batch-2 comprehension passages** (`map-pilot-2-rlu.batch.json`, the
  unconstrained grade 5-7 prose the same project wrote with no variant
  constraint, so the bar measures what the method costs). Neutral prompt, given
  verbatim and nothing else:

  > Below are 8 reading passages. For each, rate how natural and coherent the
  > prose reads as a published reading passage for students in grades 5-7, on
  > a scale of 1 (very unnatural or incoherent) to 5 (reads exactly like a
  > well-written published passage). Judge each passage on its own. Give an
  > integer rating and a one- or two-sentence reason for each. Return JSON:
  > {"labels": {"N1": {"rating": <1-5>, "reason": "..."}, ...}}

### Strand 2 bars (passes only if ALL hold)

| bar | threshold | source |
|---|---|---|
| **A** options-only | **SUPPORTS** if mean <= 50% AND unanimous-on-key <= 3 of 10; **FAILS** if mean >= 75% OR unanimous-on-key >= 7 of 10; between is **not passing**. **INVALID** if control R falls outside 10-45% (re-run the screen once). | batch 2's FIXED / NOT FIXED call, scaled from n=6 to n=10 |
| **C** exclusivity | both graders pick the key and neither names a second defensible answer on **>= 9 of 10** | SSAT C (10/12) and batch 2's 20/24, both ~83%, ceil at n=10 |
| **D** cross-version | **>= 36 of 40** question-versions valid | SSAT D (90%) |
| **E** naturalness | pooled candidate median (2 judges x 2) **>=** pooled control median (2 judges x 6); INVALID if the control median is 1; a judge that omits a rating/reason or rates all 8 alike is discarded and replaced | SSAT pilot 3's relative bar |
| **F** difficulty | an item is EASY if either grader rates `grade_fit` too_easy, or both assign a band below its target band; **<= 2 of 10** easy | new: the bar SSAT pilot 3 failed, restated on MAP's grade-fit scale |
| **G** dead distractors | an option named dead by BOTH graders on **<= 1 of 10** items | batch 2 condition 4 as a stratum bar |

*Range checks:* A's control R measured 25.0% in batch 2, inside 10-45% with
room on both sides; A's candidate can land anywhere 0-100% and both bars are
inside. **A is near chance by construction** (expected 25% over the draw), so
it is a sanity check on the build, not evidence the items are good; the exact
null over the 4^2 draws (picks held fixed) is printed beside it. With only two
passages, two draws decide A, so it is noisy at this n (SSAT A76: a 10-item
random-key screen fired by chance 1 time in 16). C, D, F, G span their full
integer ranges. E's control median can be 1-5 and the candidate median can
reach any of those values, so E can pass and fail (floor clause above).

**Reported, not deciding:** batch 2's pilot-pass count (conditions 1-4) on the
10 items, beside batch 2's comprehension 3/6; the `map-wv.mjs` lexical
word-match solver; D's `absent` and `cross_item` notes; free eliminations.

All bars are run (no stop rule): the strands share the options-only and
with-source renders, so stopping saves nothing. No repair round after any
attack.

## What can't be learned here, stated in advance

- Whether any band label is right (MAP-RESEARCH §10): graders are not response
  data.
- Whether students find the items hard: a human sitting decides verbal
  cohorts, and MAP has none.
- Whether a model judge's naturalness rating predicts a human's (A77's caveat).
- Whether the variant method scales past 2 passages: a pass here licenses one
  more pilot at larger n, not a batch.
