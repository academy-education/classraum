**"Can't tell" doesn't mean "I'm not sure."** You're never going to be
sure — the passage and the audio are hidden, so being unsure is the
normal state and isn't a reason to press it. It means something much
narrower: *nothing in these four options points anywhere.*

- "This one's longer / more careful / more hedged than the others."

counting. A hunch you can't justify is still a hunch the options handed
you. Press "Can't tell" only when all four look genuinely equal and
you'd be picking at random.
