## Ceiling check
The same instrument measured the live hard SEC control at 63.6% (n=22 items, 66
picks) on 2026-09-26. A "candidate exceeds control by more than 10" bar can fire only
while the control is at or below 90%. **If the control returns above 90% the blind half
is reported NOT MEASURED, not passed.** If the control returns at or below its own
best-fixed-letter line + 5 points, the instrument is not discriminating either and the
blind half is likewise reported NOT MEASURED.

## Bars
- **Blind (batch level):** candidate minus matched control <= **+10** points. Above
  that, the batch is HELD whole. Where both arms sit far above their letter line the
  instrument is near saturation and the with-source half decides (standing rule); the
  margin is still the comparable number.
- **Unanimity** is a batch-level rate against the control's rate only (A53); never a
  per-item drop on its own.
- **Per-item drop** (gate-verdict.mjs, any one):
  1. key disputed by any grader (Conventions also needs 3/3 key votes at insert)
  2. not exclusive by majority
  3. distractors weak by majority
  4. median of 2+ of 3 distractors free-strikable
  5. explanation path wrong per any grader
  6. a grader-majority resolving word within four words after the blank
- **Difficulty:** panel MEDIAN of the three graders, never the author's label.
  **Panel-median easy is DROPPED, not relabelled** (accepts.mjs does not gate SEC
  difficulty, so this is applied by hand from the verdict output and recorded).
- **Conservative extensions (as v9):** any item a grader explicitly recommends
  dropping is dropped; any item a majority of graders call off the digital SAT
  SEC blueprint is HELD for Andy, not inserted.
- **Cross-item tell:** if a majority of graders name the same batch-level heuristic
  (e.g. "the key always closes the pair", "the key always agrees with the far noun"),
  it is recorded against the batch even if no item drops on it.

## Discard / reporting
- Fewer than 5 hard survivors: insert what passes; the hard route stays at 4 and the
  commission is reported as not having bought a form.
- Zero hard survivors is reported as a failed hard commission regardless of insert
  count.
