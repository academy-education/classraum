### The construction table
Column key:
- **Key** is the verb's number. In a pronoun slot, **Pron** is the key pronoun's number.
- **Near** is the number of the noun immediately before the blank. In an inversion it is the last noun of the
  fronted phrase. In B5 it is "which", read as its antecedent's number. **Near = Key in 9 of 18.**
- **Pos** is where the misleader sits: `adj` immediately before the blank; `early` earlier in the sentence;
  `self` the subject word itself; `after` after the blank.
- **First** is the number of the first noun of the blank's clause against the key: `agr` / `opp`.
- **After** is the first noun within 8 words after the blank: `opp` (the wrong number, never within 4 words if
  it would resolve), `agr`, or `free`.
- **Grid** codes, each a complete 2x2 = 4 options, one lexical verb:

  | code | sg / pl | code | sg / pl |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | i | was / were | b | is / are V-ing |
  | j | is / are | g | was / were V-ing |
  | c | is / are V-en | k | is / are being V-en |
  | h | was / were V-en | l | was / were being V-en |
  | e | has / have been V-en | f | has / have been V-ing |
  | m | does / do | n | has / have |
  | a+p | V-s / V crossed with its / their | | |

  Every grid holds voice constant (c/h, k/l and e/h are passive in all four options) and holds progressive
  constant (b/g, k/l and f/g are progressive in all four).

### Rules for every item (carried from v22 unless marked NEW)
1. **NEW (3): number.** Key number, Near, First, misleader position and After as in the row. Where Near agrees,
   the misleader must still be genuinely tempting, from its own position (early, self or after).
2. **NEW (4): the second dimension sits in the blank's own sentence.**
   - The decider is a finite verb in the blank's own sentence (tense, aspect), or the grammar named in the row
     (auxiliary type from a later verb form; pronoun from its antecedent). It is quoted in the declarations as
     `decider_quote` and in the explanation.
   - **Deletion test:** delete the decider from the blank's sentence. The item must then become undecidable on
     the second dimension: no OTHER sentence may settle it on its own. The author writes the result per item.
   - Banned anywhere in any passage (v22, Amendment 1, plus NEW): "since" in any sense; "ever since"; "to this
     day"; "to the present (day)"; "until now"; "so far"; "thus far"; "to date"; "up to now"; "for the
     past/last N"; "in recent years/decades"; "lately"; "recently"; "nowadays"; "at present"; "currently";
     duration anchors ("for more than / over / nearly a century, decades, years", "over the past/last",
     "throughout", "across those/these/the decades/years"); NEW: "on its/their own", "by itself/themselves".
   - "now", "still", "already", "yet", "once" may not appear in the blank's own sentence.
   - No tense settled by a printed date or year.
3. **NEW (1): grids.** Voice never varies inside an item; progressive never varies alone. Options are exactly
   the assigned complete 2x2. No option contains a word the other three lack (no adverb, no "had", "would",
   "will" or other modal, no odd auxiliary). A word shared by all four options is allowed only in A1, B1 and
   C1 (the next word of the inverted subject), never an adverb: A1 "its", B1 a numeral, C1 an adjective. A1
   must not use "the": live v22 B-05's option set is {does the, do the, has the, have the}, and an identical
   option set is a hold under rule 14. Fixing the word class per slot keeps the three inversions' option sets
   apart without the authors having to coordinate.
4. **Pronoun slots (A2, C3).** Pronoun options are "its" and "their" only, never a person pronoun (singular
   "they" is a banned trap). The antecedent is the only noun of the right kind in the sentence.
5. **Length.** Key length divided by the shortest option's length is < 1.6. C5's key is "are", never "were",
   because the bare "were" against "is" is 2.0. Key uniquely longest at most 2 per author and 6 in the batch;
   uniquely shortest the same. The table's structural count is 4 and 4.
6. **Per-author variety.** Key plural in exactly 3 of the author's 6 agreement items. Key at the row's letter. No
   key form and no grid twice in one author. The blank is not always in the last sentence.
7. **Inversions (A1, B1, C1).** The subject's head noun is at least five words after the blank. In A1 and C1
   the deciding verb form is too.
8. **Boundary slots.** Identical words in all four options. Exactly one option correct. Never both a semicolon
   and a period offered; never both a colon and a single dash offered. No key decided by a printed partner mark.
   The key is the lone strong-mark option only in B7.
9. Every distractor is grammatical in isolation and wrong only in context. No answer-resolving word within
   four words after the blank. Free strikes at most 1 of 3. Explanation path correct.
10. No interrupter mark immediately after the blank, except C5 (the concessive clause's own comma) and B6.
11. At most 3 of 24 items delay the decider with a long fronted phrase. A1, B1 and C1 count. No other slot opens
    with one.
12. No banned trap: literary present, singular "they", "one" as a noun; collective nouns, data/media;
    subjunctive; who/whom or pronoun case; contractions, dictionary variants; "one of the ... that", "none of",
    "neither of", "the number of", "a series/pair of".
13. **No live or held frame reused** (the list above).
14. **Near-duplicates (v22 Amendment 2, now in the body).** Each author runs `sec-near-dup.mjs` on its file
    against ALL live SEC rows. An item is changed before delivery, and HELD if frozen still flagged, when:
    - its passage trigram Jaccard is >= 0.20 with any live R&W row, OR
    - its option set is IDENTICAL as strings to a live item's.
    Skeleton-only matches are recorded, not held.
15. **Declarations.** Each author writes the v17 `template` triple per item in `items.json`, and per item in
    `declarations.json`: frame, true_subject_head, misleader_words, misleader_position, key_number, near_noun,
    first_noun_of_clause, after_noun_within_8, dimension, dimension_key, decider_quote, deletion_test,
    pronoun_antecedent (A2, C3), key_mark (boundary), grid, key_letter, length_ratio, distractor_kills,
    hostile_reader.
    - **Pre-freeze comparison:** I diff each declaration against its row, and the 24 option sets against each
      other. A deviation or a cross-author collision is resolved by ONE replacement item from the author whose
      slot is later in table order, once, recorded. A second is recorded, and both items are frozen as they
      stand.

## Decidable counts
`sec23-brief-counts.mjs` is written before any item exists. It is v22's script with the v23 table, a pronoun
parser, a boundary-mark classifier, and these bars on the frozen file:
- key plural 8-10 of the agreement items;
- **NEW** voice-varying items 0;
- **NEW** simple-vs-progressive items 0;
- past key in 40-60% of time items (tense + aspect);
- each Key x Near cell 3-6 (from the declarations);
- **NEW** nearest noun agrees with the key in 7-11 agreement items (from the declarations);
- **NEW** the declared decider lies inside the blank's own sentence in every agreement item;
- 0 banned adverbials;
- 0 extra-word options;
- **NEW** boundary: 5-7 items, comma key >= 1, no-mark key >= 1, lone-strong-mark key <= 1, 0 twin offers.

Quota flags (exit 1, not bars): per-author key plural 3/6, letters 2/2/2/2, 2 boundary items per author, no key
form or grid twice per author, key form <= 2 and grid <= 3 batch-wide, uniquely longest/shortest <= 2 per author
and <= 6 batch-wide, the length ratio, every table-row field, inversion head 5+ words after the blank, an
interrupter after the blank outside C5/B6, and "agree with the first noun of the clause" <= 12.

Every classified option is printed for a human read. A count missed by the frozen file is RECORDED, not
repaired. It drops nothing on its own. The script refuses (exit 2) on an unreadable or empty batch, a key not
among its choices, a passage without exactly one blank, or declaration ids that do not match the batch. Without
`--decl` the three declaration bars print NOT MEASURED and the run cannot pass.

**No audit-and-repair round, and no repair after freeze.** Anything a stage after the freeze catches, pre-flight
included, is dropped or held, never repaired and re-counted. Every agent is Claude. **The run stops at the first
failed gate** (blind bar, elimination bar).

**Schedule.** This needs the owner's per-batch agent approval under the standing rule; nothing here grants it.
At most 3 subagents at once: authors A+B+C; solvers a+b+c; graders d+e+f phase 1; phase-1 snapshots (sha256 in

subagent idle 6+ minutes is relaunched from its disk state.

**Pre-flight drop (decidable, kept):** the word immediately before the blank is the first word of every option,
or the word immediately after the blank is the last word of every option.

**With-source render:** `BLIND=bare node bank-helper.mjs blind <frozen batch>`, asserted before any grader sees

snapshotted (sha) before the key file exists; phase 2 adds key, key_ok, cold_miss, path_coherent,

## Ceiling check: measurable range, stated before the run
The candidate's options-only rate lives in [0%, 100%]; a complete 2x2 puts no-signal at 25%. Boundary items have
four options too. The +10 bar can fire only if control <= 90%. The control history (63.9-72.2%) leaves 28-36
points of headroom, so both outcomes are reachable.
- **Control above 90%:** the blind half is reported NOT MEASURED, not passed.
- **Control at or below its own best-fixed-letter line + 5:** also NOT MEASURED; the with-source half decides.

The three solvers are one model sampled three times. Margins stand as measured; intervals are about sqrt(3)
wider than printed and are not quoted as verdicts. An options-only pass is reported as "the option set alone
does not decide it", never as "the batch does not leak". The graders' free-strike count is the half that sees
stem-plus-option eliminations.

## Bars (v22's, unchanged)
- **Blind (batch level):** candidate minus matched control <= **+10** points. Above that, the batch is HELD whole.
- **Unanimity** is a batch-level rate against the control's rate only, never a per-item drop on its own.
- **Per-item drop** (sec23-gate.mjs): 1 key disputed by any grader or a cold miss (Conventions also needs 3/3 key
  votes at insert); 2 not exclusive by majority; 3 distractors weak by majority; 4 median n_struck >= 2; 5 path
  wrong per any grader; 6 a majority resolving word within four words after the blank; 7 a majority
  `matches_open_mark: true`; R any recommend_drop; E panel-median easy.
- **Elimination (A23):** candidate minus matched live control eliminable-rate <= +20 points, control <= 80%,
  n >= 12 per arm, run on all 24 authored items.
- **Difficulty:** the panel MEDIAN, never the author's label. A panel-median easy item is DROPPED.
- **Conservative extensions:** an explicit recommend_drop drops; majority off-blueprint is HELD for Andy; a
  frozen item held under rule 14 is HELD.
- **Template pair, minimum cover with a fixed tie-break (v22's rule):**
  - **Edges:** a pair named as one template by 2+ of 3 graders in `summary.near_duplicates` in the phase-1
    snapshot, both endpoints otherwise kept. A group of k contributes all its pairs. Phase-2-only pairs are
    recorded only.
  - **Hold set:** the vertex cover chosen by (1) fewest panel-hard items, (2) fewest items, (3) the
    lexicographically first list in the fixed order.
  - **Fixed order:** sha256 of the id string, ascending, computed now (all prefixed SEC23):

        1 C-08   2 A-06   3 C-02   4 B-02   5 B-08   6 C-04   7 A-08   8 B-05
