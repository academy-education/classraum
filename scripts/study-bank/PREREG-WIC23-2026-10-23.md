## Method and bars: v20's (= v19's), unchanged
Same commission (40 WIC, 32 hard / 8 medium, four authors, 10 items each, 8 hard each), rules R1-R6, pre-freeze probes
(options-only r1, revision round, r2; difficulty probe) with one revision round, freeze, and Stages 1-5 with the SAME
bars as PREREG-WIC20 / PREREG-WIC19 / PREREG-WIC18:

- Stage 1: bank-helper structural check OK; dupscan against the live bank.
- Stage 2: options-only attack, three samples, control = the WHOLE eligible live word-shaped WIC pool (hard + medium,
  now including v19 and v20 rows). PASS if E (candidate margin minus control margin) <= +10, read against the
  measured control's attainable range.
- Stage 3: options-only elimination, margin <= +20.
- Stage 4: three with-source graders, phase 1 cold and sha-recorded before the key is sent; any grader cold-missing
  more than 4 of 40 fails the whole batch. Per-item gate rules as in `wic23-gate.mjs` (gate-verdict rules 1-6, key
  above ceiling per any, same distractor above ceiling per >= 2, antonym pair per >= 2, recommend_drop per any,
  panel-median easy; HELD on majority off-blueprint or dup-scan; rule 8 caps majority key-from-one-sentence at medium).
- Stage 5: tells on the kept set — key spread ok; key strictly longest and strictly shortest each <= 35%; no repeated
  option words.
- The batch key-strictly-longest band (15-35%) is checked by the authors before the r2 probe (v20's addition).
