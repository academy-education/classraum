# sat-math-v20-adv — pre-registration

Written 2026-10-02 before any item exists. Committed before authoring.

## Why this batch
`next-form.mjs` / `form-capacity.mjs` (2026-10-02, after v19 Algebra): SAT Math
is 26 complete forms, bound by Advanced Math 397 at 15 per form; form 27 needs
405, deficit **8**. Algebra 407, Geometry 252/189, PSDA 296/189 — no other
domain is within reach, so 8 Advanced Math survivors buy form 27 and nothing
else is needed. (A55's "aimed at the thinnest domain, bought nothing" check:
the second-thinnest domain already clears 405.)

## Commission
14 items, two halves of 7 by two independent Claude authors (never GPT), max
two subagents at once. Difficulty at roughly the live Advanced Math mix
(114 hard / 274 medium / 9 easy, ~29% hard): each half targets **5 medium +
2 hard**. The yield needed is 8 of 14 (v18 Advanced Math yielded 8 of 14).
All option sets numeric. Disjoint skill lists per half, written in the brief.

Authoring constraints carried from A56-A70 and the skill:
- `distractor_solve` for every distractor; sandbox recompute.
- **Key at an extreme about as often as chance (~50%, gate bar 40%)**, achieved
  by letting named error paths undershoot as well as overshoot — never by
  moving distractors after review.
- No ±pair with the key, no key-as-unique-composite, no closure (key = mean /
  sum / difference of two options), no derivational hub, no singleton parity/
  sign/integrality/roundness tell, no expression options (A67: the factor is
  visible), no reviewer-facing text in `explanation`.
- The discriminant-equals-zero motor at most once in the batch (A67).
- Stems must not state an answer-form constraint that kills options (the
  `df25c0d2` lesson).

## Pipeline
author (two halves) -> merge-halves -> sandbox `verify` (keys + distractors +
key-extremity verdict) -> pre-flights -> INDEPENDENT no-arithmetic with-source
auditor (stem + options, before the gate) -> at most ONE repair round, and
only on items the auditor flags -> pre-flights again -> GATE: fresh blind
options-only solvers (three samples) + three fresh with-source graders.
**No second repair round.** An item repaired once that the gate faults is
dropped, not repaired again (A70: the repair created the gate's defects four
times). An item the auditor finds with >= 2 free strikes or a disputed key is
dropped rather than repaired.

## Pre-flight (all must pass on the merged file before any solver runs)
- `math-bank-helper.mjs verify`: every key and every `distractor_solve` recomputes;
  key-extremity verdict PASS on the gated file (>= 0.8 x derived chance).
- `check-sign-pair.mjs`: zero ±pair sets containing the key.
- `check-key-is-composition.mjs`: zero key-as-unique-composite.
- `check-explanation-hygiene.mjs`: zero reviewer-facing text.
- `merge-halves.mjs`: no duplicate ids/keys/closure relations.
- `check-math-hub.mjs`, `check-symbolic-hub.mjs`, `check-key-singleton.mjs`,
  `check-key-extremity.mjs`: run and recorded.

## Control (blind half) — composition-matched, non-v2
`make-adv20-oo.mjs` (committed with this file): for each band, as many live
Advanced Math items as candidates the author labelled that band; numeric
option sets only (the gate's own parser); excluding `v2` (±pair tell, A66/A68)
and `sat-math-v17-adv, v18-adv, v18-alg, v19-alg, v20-adv`. Eligible pool
2026-10-02: **hard 52, medium 126** (397 live; 206 excluded by cohort; 6
non-numeric). Interleaved in one file, keys dealt flat per arm.

## Ceiling check
Non-`v2` hard Advanced Math measured 26.2% (A67); non-`v2` hard Algebra
21.4-33.3% (A68, A70). The +10 bar can fire in either direction while the
control sits at or below 90%. If the control returns above 90% the blind half
is NOT MEASURED, not passed. A result at the letter line is a measurement.
An options-only result is never reported as "does not leak" — the with-source
half decides stem-plus-options eliminations.

## Bars
- Blind: candidate minus matched control <= +10 points. Unanimity reported only
  as a batch-level rate against the control's (three samples of one solver).
- Per-item drop, any one (three graders):
  1. key disputed by any grader
  2. not exclusive by majority
  3. distractors weak by majority
  4. median >= 2 of 3 distractors free-strikable without arithmetic
  5. explanation path that does not produce its printed value
- EXT-1: any item a grader explicitly recommends dropping is dropped.
- HOLD: any item a grader majority calls off the published SAT Advanced Math
  blueprint is held for Andy, not inserted.
- Difficulty: banked label = three-grader PANEL MEDIAN, never the author's.
  "hard" = belongs on the module-2 hard route (a decision point routine
  procedure does not bypass); "medium" = careful multi-step procedure reaches
  the key; panel-median "easy" is not inserted.
- Key extremity: the gated file must pass the gate bar. The kept file is
  measured and reported; if it has fewer than 10 items the gate prints "no
  measurement" and that is reported as such, not as a pass.

## Need and discard
- >= 8 survivors: inserted as `sat-math-v20-adv`; full forms 26 -> 27 expected,
  confirmed by `form-capacity.mjs` after insert, not inferred.
- 1-7 survivors: inserted (they are sound items); reported as NOT buying form 27.
- Hard yield is reported as the panel-median count, whatever it is; it is not a
  target this batch is judged on.
- If the gate finds a class of free elimination the auditor missed entirely,
  that is recorded as a pipeline failure regardless of item count.
