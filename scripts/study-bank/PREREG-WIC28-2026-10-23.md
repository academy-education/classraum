In the graders' notes, v27 kept-hard items name a strong lure and give each wrong word its own inference
("'Manipulated' is the strong textbook distractor"; "ledger is a near-miss (a record), but its layers record
nothing except years"). Kept-medium items name ONE near-miss and wave the other two through ("haven/gateway fit
the strait setting but not the coal logic"; "insurance/inventory are plausible; loot weak"; "blackened, blistered,
cracked each contradicted by a clause").

**Caveat, as in II23:** `strong` and `n_struck` 0 are the panel describing its own consistency (graders who call an
item hard also call its distractors strong). They say what the panel rewards, which is what the brief and the probe
aim at. They are not independent predictors, and the gate never uses them.

### Grader-named templates
**None. The WIC graders were never asked.** In v25-v27 the GRADER-BRIEF had no batch question (II23 and SEC25
added one; WIC did not), and no WIC grader note names a cross-item pattern. One uniformity is visible by count, and
it is mine, not the graders':
- every v25, v26 and v27 blank sentence is the passage's LAST sentence (32/32, 40/40, 40/40), almost always an
  expert's verdict;
- the intensifier ("above all", "a kind of", "in effect", ...) went 3/32, 9/40, **30/40**. The v27 brief's four
  examples all carried one.

Four-world construction means the frame cannot predict the key, and it does not predict difficulty (v27: with an
intensifier 7 hard / 17 medium / 6 dropped; without 4 / 6 / 0). It is a template, not a tell, and real SAT WIC
items vary the frame. A second candidate pattern, an "unchanged control" sentence ("tide mark and stream unchanged
rule out submerged and tilted"), was checked and does not separate (served v27: 6 hard / 12 medium / 2 dropped
with one, 5 / 11 / 4 without), so it is not acted on.

### 1. The probe's phase 2 (`wic28-wprobe.mjs phase2`)
After all four cold files exist and validate, `phase2` writes their sha256 to `sat-cs-wic-v28-wprobe.phase1.sha256`
and only then writes each render's key letters and explanations (`-r<k>.phase2.json`). Each reader, the same one
that read the render cold, writes `-r<k>.probe2.json` (`key_ok`, `path_coherent`, `note2`). `score` refuses (exit 2)
if a cold file no longer matches the recorded sha, if a probe2 entry lacks a boolean `key_ok` or `path_coherent`,
or if either is false without a `note2`. The reader brief spells out what "path wrong" means using v27's four
drops as the pattern (a thin detail, a narrow reading of the word, a fact that supports the word).

### 2. MUST revise, and what verify checks
| Reader judgement | MUST revise | verify requires |
|---|---|---|
| difficulty easy (v26-v27) | yes | passage changed |
| `n_struck` >= 2 (v27) | yes | passage changed |
| **distractor_quality weak or plausible (new)** | yes | passage changed |
| **phase 2 `key_ok` false (new)** | yes | passage changed |
| **phase 2 `path_coherent` false (new)** | yes | explanation changed |
| key missed, not exclusive, second defensible, one sentence, resolving word, antonym pair, above ceiling, literal fit, presupposes absent | named | nothing (listed) |
| explicit_kills >= 2 | listed only | nothing |

On v27's own probe data this MUST set would have held 93 of 160 worlds, against v27's 13 (dq: 67 strong, 83
plausible, 10 weak). v27 revised 146 worlds anyway, so the load is the revision the authors already did, now
pointed at the panel's weak spot. `verify` still proves an edit, not an improvement. A world's key never changes
and an item never vanishes (both exit 1, as in v27). `render` now refuses to run twice (a second render would reset
what verify compares).

### 3. The author brief (`sat-cs-wic-v28-work/AUTHOR-BRIEF.md`)
v27's brief kept in structure (central rule, self-test, P1-P5, A-E, four-world build steps), with:
- the v27 table of what the panel rewarded, the four explanation drops, and the part-of-speech record;
- **P1 raised**: three near-misses per world;
- **P2** extended to "names the key's function" (v27's two easy drops);
- **P5**: verb or noun sets, verbs preferred (12/44 panel-hard);
- **P6 (new)**: each wrong word rejected on its decisive reason, in its broadest ordinary sense, never with a fact
  that could support it;
- the **frame rule** (below);
- examples: four v27 panel-hard served worlds verbatim (A-05, B-05, B-06, E-06, every grader `strong`), and five
  counter-examples (D-05 and B-03 easy; D-03 and D-07 explanation drops; A-03 kept medium). All their words are v27
  words, excluded already, and are also denylisted on record.

The brief's starred rules are what the self-check enforces. P1, P2, P4 and P6 are not checkable by a script, and
the self-check's `surface_fit`/`inference` fields remain wording discipline, never evidence (v27's finding stands:
the absence-claim pattern is inert as a predictor).

### 4. The frame rule (self-check, per author)
The blank sentence ends the passage in at most **5 of 8** items (an item counts as closing if its blank sentence is
last in ANY of its four worlds), and carries an intensifier in at most **2 of 8**. Break-tested at the margin (5 and
2 pass, 6 and 3 fail) and by deleting each rule (the failing directories then pass). It is a uniformity rule, not a
difficulty rule.

### 5. Exclusion
The self-check refuses any option word that is in `sat-cs-wic-v28.live-words.json` (live WIC, v17 held, v18-v24
frozen, v25/v26/v27 worlds), **or** read directly from the frozen v19/v20/v23/v24 batches and the v25 (32), v26 (40)
and **v27 (40) worlds files**, or a stem or inflection of any of these, or a denylisted word, or a stem used twice in
the batch. `wic28-livewords.mjs` refuses unless the v27 worlds file holds 40 four-world items with sha 78cf5871
(and v26's 003443a6, v25's c7794cc7). The frozen-file net works without the live-words file (break-tested).

## Selection rule (v27's, with v28 constants)
`wic28-select.mjs`:
1. Refuse unless `wic28-selfcheck.mjs --final` is green, each of the 5 authors has exactly 8 files, and
   `wic28-wprobe.mjs verify` is green. **Also refuses duplicate item ids (new).**
2. Write `sat-cs-wic-v28.worlds.json` and record its sha256.
3. Selection seed: **20262890 + int(sha[0:8]), mod 2^32.** mulberry32 draws world index floor(4u) per item, in id
   order.
4. **No re-roll.**
5. Letters from a balanced deck, 10 per letter, rng(seed + 7919).
6. Write `sat-cs-wic-v28.batch.json` (served world only) and `sat-cs-wic-v28.selection.json`.
7. **No post-selection edits.** A served world that fails any gate drops its item WHOLE. An item is never re-served
   from another world. Unserved worlds are never banked or recycled, and their words stay excluded.

## Stages and bars (v27's, unchanged)
The run stops at the first failed stage. The control's attainable range is stated for every bar, and both sides of
each bar are reachable inside it.

| Stage | Bar | Control and its range | Ceiling / floor check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 shape | bank-helper check OK; dupscan vs all live R&W flags 0 (a flag is HELD) | none | not a margin |
| 2 options-only | **E = M_cand - M_ctl <= +10** | the WHOLE eligible live word-shaped WIC pool, dry-rendered at **177** (45 hard, 132 medium); control line **25.4%** (45/44/44/44). MEASURED only in **(30.4%, 90%]**, so M_ctl in (+5.0, +64.6]. Candidate line 25.0% (10/10/10/10), M_cand in [-25, +75] | reject needs M_cand > M_ctl + 10, reachable for every measured control (M_ctl + 10 <= 74.6 < 75); pass reachable always. **Projected control 33.3%, 2.9 points above the floor** (below) |
| 3 elimination | margin <= +20, control <= 80%, n >= 12 | paired live control, `elimination-paired.mjs`'s own draw | reject reachable for any control <= 80%. v25-v27 all read **0.0% vs 0.0%**: every sample marked every item not-certain, so the stage has never measured anything. Kept as pre-flight, not evidence |
| 4 with-source | any grader cold-misses > **4 of 40** -> batch FAIL; per item DROP/HELD/rule 8 exactly v27's | three fresh graders | v25-v27 cold misses were 0-1 per grader; the bar is attainable both ways |
| 5 tells | key spread no FAIL; key strictly longest <= 35% and strictly shortest <= 35% of kept; no repeated option words | chance: a unique longest key ~1 in 4 | 35% sits above chance and below certainty |
| 5 attrition | **E_kept = M_kept - M_ctl <= +10** on the kept candidates (0.1-point rounding); < 8 kept is HELD | stage 2's control. **If the control is NOT MEASURED: M_kept <= +10 on its own (new, v27's stage-2 fallback)** | kept-arm line 25-37.5% for 8-40 kept; reject reachable at every n >= 8 |

**The stage-2 control is approaching its floor.** It holds every live word-shaped WIC row, and four-world rows sit
near chance by construction. From v27's own stage-2 picks: 100 non-four-world control rows read 38.3%, the 43
v25/v26 rows 31.0%, and v27's 34 kept candidates 21.6%. Weighted to the 177-row pool: **33.3%**, against a floor of
30.4%. Those rows push M_ctl down and E up, against a pass, never for one. If the control lands at or below 30.4%,
stage 2 is NOT MEASURED and the batch continues only if M_cand <= +10 (v27's rule), and the attrition check uses the
same fallback. Excluding four-world cohorts from the control would restore headroom but changes the control, so it
is NOT done here; it is listed under "unsettled". As a reported sub-read only, `wic28-gate.mjs` prints the control
split into four-world and other cohorts (from the key file's `cohort`). On v27's real files it reads four-world
31.0% (M +0.8) and other 38.3% (M +10.3).

Unchanged from v27, recorded so nobody re-derives them: the three solvers are one model sampled three times
(margins stand; intervals are about sqrt(3) wider than printed); unanimity is a batch-level comparison; an
options-only pass is "the option set alone does not decide it", never "the batch does not leak".

## Discard and reporting plan
- **Discard (whole batch):** a stage-2 E above +10 with the control measured, or M_cand above +10 with it not
  measured; a stage-3 margin above +20; a grader cold-missing more than 4. A failed attrition check, or fewer than 8
  kept, HOLDS the kept set for the owner; nothing is inserted from it.
- **Per item:** v27's gate rules decide; difficulty is always the panel median; medium survivors are inserted and do
  not move the hard route; an item is never re-served or repaired.
- **Zero hard survivors is a failed hard commission**, whatever the insert count.
- **Reported against the need**, from `form-capacity.mjs`: C&S hard after insert against 104 (form 13) and 112
  (form 14); the R&W route number with its binding domain (SEC will still bind unless SEC26 has landed).
- **Comparisons v28 is built to make** (so the result cannot be read selectively):
  1. kept hard against v27's 11 of 40, and drops by reason against the table above (did P6 and the phase 2 move the
     explanation drops from 4?);
  2. the probe's pre-revision `difficulty`, `distractor_quality` and phase-2 `path_coherent` on the served worlds
     against the panel, in this document's table form (did phase 2 catch the worlds the panel drops on rule 5?);
  3. the share of panel-hard items with all three graders `strong` (v27 7 of 11);
  4. the graders' `batch_note` and the readers' `_batch.tells`, read by name against the frame finding above; a
     pattern named by two or more graders goes into the next brief;
  5. part-of-speech yield against the v25-v27 record.
- Whatever the outcome, the result and any new finding go to REGISTER.md section 5 in the same commit as the insert.

## What the method still cannot fix
Everything in v25-v27's sections of the same name still holds: per-world exclusivity and difficulty are the
author's to get right; only the served world is graded; the four-world construction has with-source tells of its
own; the gate is model-only; three samples are one solver; and the human sitting decides verbal cohorts. New for v28:
- Phase 2 of the probe is one reader per world judging an explanation it can now compare with its own cold answer;
  the gate's rule 5 drops on ANY grader's `path_coherent` false, so a phase-2 pass is weaker than the gate's bar.
- Making distractors below strong a MUST-revise cannot make them strong; verify proves only that the passage changed.
- The frame rule removes one uniformity; whether the batch develops another is what the new batch questions are for,
  and neither of them decides anything this round.
