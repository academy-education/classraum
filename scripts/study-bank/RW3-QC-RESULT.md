This is the kill-span brief working. s2's defect was that a shown
passage did not CONTRADICT its sibling answers, only failed to assert

## The caveat, stated because it limits the number

Only TWO of the three voter answer-strings are distinct — two voters
returned identical picks. So this is closer to two independent readings
than three, and 98.7% should be read with that discount. It does not
change the direction of the result (s2's vote was taken the same way and
came back 43%), but a future run should force voter divergence the way
the blind attack does, by giving each voter a different reading stance.
