# Live-bank mechanism lists

One line per distinct mechanism already in the live bank, per domain, built
by authors READING every kept row rather than grepping for phrases.

They exist because of §13l: on 2026-09-19 two batches lost **25 of 66 items**
to mechanism duplicates of live rows, and every author had grepped the bank
as instructed and reported no siblings. A dependent-system quotient item does
not contain the words "dependent" or "quotient".

Building the list cut the duplicate rate from 14/30 to 4/16 in one batch —
and all four survivors were mechanisms that were ON the list. The list is
necessary and not sufficient: the author must check each FINISHED item back
against it.

These are snapshots, not a registry. They go stale as the bank grows; the
counts in each file's header say what it was built from. Re-read rather than
trust one that predates a batch you care about.
