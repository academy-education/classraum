### What varies, so that no one- or two-feature rule predicts the key
- **Rule pair.** Number x {pronoun number (8), tense (7), aspect (8), auxiliary type (3)}; boundary marks
  decided by a count elsewhere, by a delayed verb, by a later list item, or by where a clause ends; noun
  form decided by a count elsewhere and by whether the next word is a verb.
- **Key role.** Pronoun: matched 4 / mismatched 4, key pronoun sg 4 / pl 4, verb sg 4 / pl 4, near noun and
  first noun each agreeing in 4 (a design found by exhaustive search over all 8-of-16 row sets: every
  single and pair rule over num, near, first, pronoun, match and pronoun-vs-near scores exactly chance,
  0.250). Aspect:
  perfect 4 / past 4. Tense: past 4 / present 3. Auxiliary: have / be / do once each. Boundary keys: no
  mark, comma x2, semicolon x2, period, colon, dash, and a comma at the early or the late position of a
  garden path; in the delayed-verb family the four rows key four different marks; in the restrictiveness
  family three. Noun form: all four classes keyed once (SEC25 keyed only possessives).
- **Letters.** 10 per letter; each author 3/3/2/2 with distinct sequences; at most 2 per letter in each
  agreement stratum and 3 in boundary.

### What the dry run changed in the table before this document
- **The first letter assignment** (a rotating ABCD) made every aligned quad a permutation, as SEC25's did;

- **The first pronoun design** (a 2^(4-1) factorial with first = num x pronoun x near) aliased "first" with

## Cross-item tell check (`sec26-tellsim.mjs`): bars and the result on the table
For each item type (agreement, boundary, noun form) it searches, per stratum (agreement by second
dimension: pronoun, tense, aspect, aux), every single-feature rule (f = v, f != v), every two-feature
conjunction, every odd-one-out rule and the key-letter rule over the surface features a student can read
without doing the grammar: verb number; its relation to the noun before the blank, the first noun, the
noun after; the second dimension's value; "been/being"; for pronoun grids whether the pronoun matches the
verb's number and the near noun; for boundary items the mark, strong/bare, number of marks, a misplaced
mark (garden-path rows read a comma at either position as the same); for noun forms possessive and
plural. The agreement features are also searched pooled over all 26 agreement items. Expected accuracy
of a rule on an item = 1/|picked| if it picks the key, 0 if not, 0.25 if it picks nothing.

|---|---|---|---|

## Bars (SEC25's, unchanged) and the measurable range of each, stated before the run
- **Blind (batch level):** candidate minus matched control <= **+10** points; above that the batch is HELD
  whole. E is compared after rounding to 0.1 point (WIC26's finding: a raw float difference failed an exact
  +10.0). **Ceiling:** the candidate rate can be anywhere in [0%, 100%], and a complete 2x2 puts
  no-signal at 25% (boundary and noun items have four options too). The +10 bar can fire only while the
  control is <= 90%; SEC controls have been 56.9-72.2% (v24 59.7%, v25 58.3%), leaving 28-43 points of
  headroom, so both outcomes are reachable. **Control above 90%: the blind half is NOT MEASURED, not
  passed. Control at or below its own best-fixed-letter line + 5 (30.0% on the dry run): also NOT
  MEASURED; the with-source half decides.**
- **Unanimity** is a batch-level rate against the control's, never a per-item drop on its own. The three
  solvers are one model sampled three times: margins stand, intervals are about sqrt(3) wider than
  printed. An options-only pass is reported as "the option set alone does not decide it", never "the batch
  does not leak".
- **Per-item drop** (`sec26-gate.mjs`): 1 key disputed by any grader, or a cold miss (Conventions also
  needs 3/3 key votes at insert); 2 not exclusive by majority; 3 distractors weak by majority; 4 median
  n_struck >= 2; 5 path wrong per any grader; 6 a majority resolving word within four words after the
  blank; 7 a majority `matches_open_mark: true`; R any recommend_drop; E panel-median easy. These are rules,
  not margins; each reads three graders and fires on a majority or on any one, so no ceiling applies.
- **Elimination (A23):** candidate minus matched live control eliminable-rate <= **+20** points, control
  <= 80%, n >= 12 per arm, on all 40 authored items. **Ceiling:** the bar can fire only while the control is
  <= 80% (the condition exists for that reason); recent SEC elimination margins were -7.6 (v22), 0.0 (v23),
  -4.9 (v24) and -6.3 (v25, control 6.3%), so the control sits far below 80% and a candidate fails only at
  control + 20, which is attainable. Control above 80%: NOT MEASURED.
- **Difficulty:** the panel MEDIAN, never the author's label and never the probe's. A panel-median easy
  item is DROPPED.
- **Conservative extensions:** an explicit recommend_drop drops; majority off-blueprint is HELD for Andy;
  a frozen item still flagged by `sec26-exclusion.mjs` is HELD.
- **Template pair, minimum cover with a fixed tie-break (v22's rule):** edges are pairs named by 2+ of 3
  graders in `summary.near_duplicates` in the phase-1 snapshot, both endpoints otherwise kept; a group of
  k contributes all its pairs; phase-2-only pairs are recorded only. Hold set: the vertex cover with (1)
  fewest panel-hard items, (2) fewest items, (3) the lexicographically first list in the fixed order.
  **Fixed order** (sha256 of the id string, ascending; all prefixed SEC26):

        1 B-10   2 D-02   3 C-09   4 B-03   5 A-03   6 A-06   7 C-05   8 C-02
        9 C-10  10 D-03  11 B-05  12 A-02  13 A-08  14 D-01  15 A-01  16 D-08
       17 A-04  18 D-04  19 B-04  20 C-06  21 D-06  22 C-01  23 A-07  24 D-09
       25 B-06  26 A-10  27 B-01  28 A-09  29 B-09  30 B-08  31 B-07  32 B-02
       33 D-07  34 C-03  35 C-07  36 C-08  37 D-05  38 C-04  39 A-05  40 D-10

  Computed per component by exhaustive enumeration in `sec26-template-cover.mjs`; it refuses a component
  over 20 items and a grader file without `near_duplicates`. **A component over 20 is possible here** (if

- **Cross-item tell (recorded):** a heuristic named by a majority of graders is recorded against the
