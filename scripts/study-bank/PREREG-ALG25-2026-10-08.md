### The kept-set band (unchanged from v24) and the under-10 rule (new)

- Key at an extreme on the KEPT file must be **40-60%**, else nothing is
  inserted and nothing is moved.
- **If the kept file has fewer than 10 scorable items, the band is NOT
  MEASURED** (`key-extremity-gate.mjs` returns no-measurement below 10, and a
  check that cannot measure must not return a pass). Then **nothing is

## Rule 2 — duplicates by mechanism, wording varied by the authors

- **Drop** (pre-freeze): an exact stem duplicate (`stem-duplicates.mjs
  --family sat`), or an option multiset equal to a live Algebra row
  (`check-math-near-dup.mjs`).
- **Drop** (pre-freeze, by hand read): an item that is the SAME MECHANISM WITH
  THE SAME SETUP as a live maths row, as v23's held A1/A2, or as any of v24's
  20 authored items (held, dropped or pre-flight-dropped) — i.e. the same item
  with different numbers. Read from `math-mechanism-dup.mjs` run on the merge
  together with those reference files (every FLAG and the top 5 near per
  candidate read by hand), plus a hand read of the batch against live v23 A6
  (`d1477b94`) and B7. Sharing the family's insight (a linear function on an
  interval is bounded at its endpoints; k need not be an integer) is NOT a
  duplicate: the whole commission shares it by design. The setup triple
  (rule 4) is what distinguishes items.
- **Word-set Jaccard is reported, not a drop.** `check-math-near-dup.mjs`
  (>= 0.60) and `merge-halves.mjs` stem overlap are run and every hit is read
  by hand under the rule above. A84 showed the wording measure condemning
  items whose setups differed.
- **Authors vary the wording and check while authoring.** Each author is told
  not to reuse A6's boilerplate ("In the function f defined above, k is a
  constant, and ... for every value of x with ...") and to use several stem
  registers (a bare function, a table or two given values, a short real-world
  linear model). Before submitting, each author runs on its own file:
  `math-bank-helper.mjs verify`, `check-math-near-dup.mjs --family sat`,
  `math-mechanism-dup.mjs`, `alg25-preflight.mjs --commission`, and
  rewords/re-sets until near-dup reads < 0.60 within its file and against live
  rows, or the author states per remaining hit why the setups differ.

## Rule 3 — the two cross-item tells A84 measured

- **At most 2 non-integer-constant items** in the batch (both in R). Each
  declares `nonint_constant: true` and `integer_k_answer` (the value the
  integer-k reading gives). **They must not all offer that value as an
  option**: R writes one that offers it and one that does not.
  `alg25-preflight.mjs` R4 detects an undeclared item from its stem (a
  question about values of k plus an integer condition) and refuses it.
- **Off by one next to the key on at most a quarter of items**: an option
  whose value is key +- 1, or an author-declared `off_by_one_options` value
  (an endpoint/inclusion error) adjacent to the key in value order. At most
  floor(32/4) = 8 batch-wide (P <= 2, Q <= 2, R <= 2 as commissioned).
  `alg25-preflight.mjs` R5 counts it. Graders name any off-by-one-endpoint
  option adjacent to the key they find; that is recorded, and on the KEPT file
  R5 is reported.

## Rule 4 — shapes, setups, blueprint

- **Shapes:** only those that graded hard — whole-interval bound (v23 A6, v24
  A2, v24 A4) and the non-integer-constant count (v23 B7, v24 B4, B6) —
  **with setups none of those used.** Each item declares a `setup` triple
  (v24's matrix, extended):

      (i)  where k sits: slope, fixed y-intercept | slope, pivot point inside
           the interval | slope, pivot point outside the interval | a function
           VALUE at a stated point | the slope of a combination (f(x) + mx, or
           f(x) - g(x) with g fixed) | k in the BOUNDING line, f fixed (NEW)
           | (R's two B items: v24's half-B (i) list)
      (ii) bound: lower only | upper only | two-sided | between two fixed lines
           | (B items: v24's half-B (ii) list)
      (iii) asked: width of the k-range | sum of greatest and least k |
           greatest or least k | number of integer k | spread of f(t) for t
           outside the interval | greatest or least f(t) | number of integers
           f(t) can equal | sum of greatest and least f(t) | greatest or least
           y-intercept | greatest f(a) - f(b) | other: <named by the author>

  No two items share a full triple; no two share both (i) and (iii); none
  equals a banned triple (v23 A6, v23 B7, v24 A2, A4, B4, B6). `setup` is
  stripped from the frozen file.
- **No three-unknown systems.** At most two variables (x, y) plus exactly one
  named constant k; at most two equations in total, a "lies on" condition
  counting as one. No k^2 / quadratic step in k (v23 grader F's tell). No
  quadratic in x, no absolute value, no divisibility beyond "is an integer".
- **Blueprint:** each grader states `on_blueprint` + `blueprint_reason` per
  item (SAT Algebra: linear equations in one or two variables, linear
  functions, systems of two linear equations in two variables, linear
  inequalities). A grader majority calling an item off-blueprint HOLDS it.
- ASCII hyphen-minus only (R3 refuses U+2010-U+2015, U+2212, U+FE63, U+FF0D).
  `≤` `≥` allowed. Every option numeric, four choices.

## Bars (v24's, rule 7 sharpened)

- **Blind:** candidate minus matched control <= +10 points; unanimity a
  batch-level rate; control above 90% = NOT MEASURED, not passed.
- **Per-item drop**, any one (three graders): 1 key disputed; 2 not exclusive
  by majority; 3 distractors weak by majority; 4 median >= 2 of 3 distractors
  free-strikable without arithmetic, or the key free-strikable by any grader;
  5 a `distractor_solve` / explanation path that does not produce its value;
  6 a majority report ascending (descending for "greatest") plug-back reaches
  the key in <= 2 routine substitutions; 7 a grader majority names the item as
  ONE TEMPLATE with an EARLIER item by id — the later item is dropped.
  **Template, defined for the graders (new):** the same setup and the same
  solution steps with different numbers or a cosmetic change. Sharing the
  family insight or the asked quantity alone is not one template, because the
  commission shares it by design; graders name pairs that share setup AND
  steps, and separately note any cross-item insight.
- **EXT-1:** any item a grader explicitly recommends dropping is dropped.
- **HOLD:** a grader majority off-blueprint -> held, not inserted.
- **Difficulty:** banked label = three-grader PANEL MEDIAN; v18-v24's
  question verbatim ("hard" = a decision point routine procedure, including
  Desmos, does not bypass; "medium" = careful multi-step procedure reaches the
  key). Easy is rejected.
- **Kept-set band:** 40-60% at n >= 10; under 10 -> held (rule 1).
