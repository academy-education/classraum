# ATV2 b1 pre-freeze review — atv2-b1-quads.json (verbatim reviewer report, 2026-08-18)

Reviewer independent of the author. Criteria and severity calibrated to
atv2-prefreeze-review.md (the pilot review): REWRITE = world-decidable
paradigm recognition, a setting that confirms/refutes/near-forces a sibling
pivot's setting, a setting incoherent with an entire sibling slate, or a
thesis stated in two pivots; advisory = lone-family options, offset hedges,
priors that random keying neutralizes, transcript-authoring burdens.

## atv2-b1-p1 (Musicology, Fantin suites)

**REFUTABILITY:** none. Composer, courts (Brenna, Casperi), and the edition
project are fictional. The "were baroque suites danced or listened to"
debate is real, but it is genuinely open in the real literature and none of
the four q3 verdicts is the textbook winner for an invented composer. Ink
analysis of ornament signs (q1) matches real philological practice with no
decidable outcome.

**ASYMMETRY:** q3 has two nuanced settings (s3 divides the repertory, s4
accepts on ledger evidence rather than the scores) against one reject and
one plain accept — offset, same shape the pilot tolerated at p1/q4. q4 s4
("Acquire an amateur copy reported in a private collection, for which talks
are underway") is the most novelistic/specific of its slate; advisory only.

**ORTHOGONALITY:** all six pairs checked. One family echo, not a leak:
q2 s1 ("payment records show the suites were played there") and q3 s4
("fee entries in the court ledger") both reach for court payment archives.
Different courts, different claims, every combination writable — but if
both are drawn the transcript must keep the two document sets distinct.
Advisory. q4 (editorial plan for the missing second-violin book) is
independent of q1–q3 in every combination; q1's ink settings never
constrain q4's options.

**STEMS:** neutral; q4's "missing second-violin book" is lecture premise,
as the pilot allowed for Tell Arvani's abandonment.

**Verdict: CLEAN** (advisories: q2 s1 / q3 s4 shared evidence family;
q4 s4 specificity; and the "Brenna" name collision with b3 — see §4).

## atv2-b1-p6 (Astronomy, disk gap at the Aldan Array)

**REFUTABILITY:** this is the most paradigm-recognizable lecture in b1 —
"Aldan Array" reads as ALMA and the disk-gap debate is HL-Tau-flavored.
It survives the pilot-p2 test, narrowly, because the four q3 causes
(unseen companion / ice condensation front / grain growth to invisibility
/ shadowing from an inner warp) are the REAL literature's still-open
candidate set: recognizing the paradigm hands the solver the debate, not
the answer. Unlike pilot p2, no setting is the real-world-true one.
Prior note: current fashion leans "hidden planet", which random keying
neutralizes.

**ORTHOGONALITY — two leaks, both pilot-p2 classes:**

- p6/q2 s4 ↔ p6/q3 s4. "It extends just partway around the star, an arc
  rather than a full circuit" is, to an astronomy-literate reader, close
  to diagnostic of shadowing: companion-carved, ice-line, and grain-growth
  gaps are azimuthally complete annuli, while a warp shadow is exactly the
  mechanism that produces an arc. If q2 draws s4, q3's slate collapses
  toward s4; if q3's key is s4, q2 s4 is confirmed above chance. One side
  of this pair must change (give the arc a live non-shadow reading in the
  slate, or replace the arc geometry / the shadow cause).
- p6/q3 s1 → p6/q4 s1 (and s4). "A companion too faint to detect yet" makes
  "Ask the array's time committee for a much deeper exposure of the same
  field" the near-forced next step — the pilot-p2 recordings-limitation ↔
  imaging-next echo. It is partially diluted because q4 s4 (wait for the
  receiver upgrade) also serves the faint-companion story, but a solver
  handed q3 = s1 still bets q4 = s1 well above chance, and the entailment
  is the same shape the pilot required rewritten. Break it: decouple q4 s1
  from the detection hunt (a deeper exposure aimed at something the other
  q3 settings also want, stated in the option), or replace q3 s1's "too
  faint to detect yet" clause.

Milder: q2 s2 ("two narrow openings") against q3's and q4's singular "the
gap" stems — writable, transcript must manage the number. Advisory.

**ASYMMETRY:** q4 s2 (release the data to rivals) is the lone
non-observing option in a slate of three observing plans — pilot
lone-logistical class, advisory. q3 s2 is the length outlier of its slate;
the post-freeze length-rank check will see it, advisory here.

**Verdict: NEEDS REWRITE — p6/q2 s4 ↔ q3 s4 (arc/shadow entailment) and
p6/q3 s1 → q4 s1 (faint-companion/deeper-exposure echo); one side of each
pair.**
