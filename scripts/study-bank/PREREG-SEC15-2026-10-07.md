## Brief (what is new against v14)
14 items, two independent Claude authors (never GPT), 7 items each, disjoint construction
lists; every distractor grammatical in isolation and wrong only in context; key letters
flat (A/B/C/D 3-4 each across 14); explanations quote options.

1. **Weighted toward agreement and verb form.** v14's hard yield came 3 of 5 from Form,
   Structure, and Sense (agreement x2, finite verb). v15: **at least 8 of 14** items are
   Form/Structure/Sense, of which **at least 6** are subject-verb agreement or verb form
   (finite vs nonfinite, tense/aspect sequence, voice, mood), each with a second rule
   interacting. **At most 6** boundary items.
2. **Never two items on one template.** v14 SEC14A-02 and SEC14B-01 shared the singular
   "has + participle" against plural nouns template. A template is the (subject
   construction x what misleads x option grid) triple. Within v15, no two items may share
   one; and no v15 item may reuse a LIVE hard template, named here so authors avoid them:
   locative inversion with a postposed plural subject (v14 A-01); singular "has/have +
   participle" after a long subject ending in plural nouns (v14 A-02, B-01); whether-clause
   subject (v14 B-01); "the number of" (v10); gerund-phrase subject containing plurals
   (v10); compound subject with a singular part next to the blank (v10); who-clause verb
   with a singular antecedent (v10); linking verb with a plural complement (v10). Option
   grids must also vary: **at most 3** agreement/verb items may use the 2x2
   number-by-tense grid.
3. **Break "pick the number opposite the nearest noun".** Grader d named it deciding three
   v14 Form items. v15: in **at least 2** of the agreement items the noun immediately
   before the blank AGREES in number with the key (the attractor sits elsewhere, or the
   item turns on tense/form rather than number).
4. **Added-connector tell.** All three v14 graders: an option that adds a connector word
   is never the key in the punctuation items. The fix is not another flip: **at most 2**
   boundary items may offer any option that adds a word (connector, subordinator,
   relative, adverb) not in the other options, and if 2 do, the added-word option is the
   key in exactly 1 of them. Every other boundary item varies punctuation only: the four
   options contain the same words in the same order.
5. **Lone-strong-mark tell.** Grader d: the lone strong mark is the key on v14 A-03, A-05,
   B-04. v15: in boundary items the key may be the ONLY option carrying a strong mark
   (period, semicolon, colon, dash) in **at most 1** item; in at least 2 boundary items a
   strong-mark option is WRONG while the key is a comma or no mark.
6. **Kept from v14:** at most 3 of 14 items delay the deciding clause with a long fronted
   phrase; no item whose key is decided by the partner of a mark already in the sentence
   (rule 7); no answer-resolving word within four words after the blank; no free-strike
   distractors (aim for <= 1 of 3 strikable without parsing); explanation path correct;
   no single mark family the key in more than 3 boundary items; every mark that is a key
   somewhere is a distractor somewhere; topics must not repeat any v10-v14 topic.
7. **Near-duplicates.** Each author runs `sec-near-dup.mjs` on its file against ALL live
   SEC rows (paged, count-asserted: 373 today) and changes any item with passage trigram
   Jaccard >= 0.20 to a live row or an option skeleton set identical to a live HARD item,
   BEFORE delivery.

## Pipeline
two authors -> merge -> **freeze** (sha recorded, committed) -> structural pre-flight
(bank-helper check, check-sec-preflight.mjs, key spread, length ranks, and the brief's
decidable counts 1-6 computed from the frozen file, sec-near-dup.mjs re-run on the frozen
file, read by hand) -> options-only blind attack (3 fresh solver samples) + 3 fresh
two-phase with-source graders (ws-d/e/f) -> gate-verdict.mjs + rule 7 ->
elimination-paired.mjs --batch (A23 bar) -> mk-rw-qc.mjs -> ledger -> insert as
rw-v15-sec-hard -> verify-sat-hard-route.ts reading_writing + form-capacity.mjs.
**No audit-and-repair round, and no repair after freeze.** Anything a stage after freeze
catches (pre-flight included) is dropped or held, never repaired and re-counted. Every
agent is Claude. A brief count (1-6) missed by the frozen file is RECORDED, not repaired;
it drops nothing on its own.

**NEW pre-flight drop (decidable, the SEC14B-06 defect):** an item where the word
immediately before the blank is also the first word of every option, or the word
immediately after the blank is also the last word of every option, is DROPPED at
pre-flight (every reading doubles a word).

**With-source render:** `BLIND=bare node bank-helper.mjs blind <frozen batch>`, asserted
before any grader sees it: no item's `subskill` string, and none of the words "dash pair",
"boundar", "agreement", "closing", "opening", appears in the render. A render that fails
the assertion is not used.

**Grader brief change.** v14's grader brief told graders that options "often repeat the
word before the blank". That sentence excused SEC14B-06, which no grader flagged. It is
REMOVED. In its place graders are asked to flag any option set in which every option
duplicates a word already printed next to the blank (`recommend_drop`).

**Two phases.** Phase 1 cold (no key in existence for the grader) is snapshotted (sha)
before the key file is written; phase 2 adds key_ok / cold_miss / path_coherent /
recommend_drop. Phase-1 fields are verified unchanged after phase 2; a grader that
changed a phase-1 field has its phase-1 snapshot used.

## Ceiling check
A "candidate exceeds control by more than 10" bar can fire only while the control is at
or below 90%. **If the control returns above 90% the blind half is reported NOT MEASURED,
not passed.** If the control returns at or below its own best-fixed-letter line + 5
points, the blind half is likewise reported NOT MEASURED.

## Bars
- **Blind (batch level):** candidate minus matched control <= **+10** points. Above that,
  the batch is HELD whole.
- **Unanimity** is a batch-level rate against the control's rate only (A53); never a
  per-item drop on its own.
- **Per-item drop** (gate-verdict.mjs rules 1-6, rule 7 applied from the same files):
  1. key disputed by any grader (Conventions also needs 3/3 key votes at insert)
  2. not exclusive by majority
  3. distractors weak by majority
  4. median of 2+ of 3 distractors free-strikable
  5. explanation path wrong per any grader
  6. a grader-majority resolving word within four words after the blank
  7. a grader majority marks `matches_open_mark: true`
- **Elimination (A23, elimination-paired.mjs --batch):** candidate minus matched live
  control eliminable-rate <= +20 points, control <= 80%, n >= 12 per arm; else the stage
  does not pass. If fewer than 12 survive the with-source rule, the stage is run on all
  authored items before drops so n >= 12, and reported as such.
- **Difficulty:** panel MEDIAN of the three graders, never the author's label.
  **Panel-median easy is DROPPED, not relabelled.**
- **Conservative extensions (as v9-v14):** any item a grader explicitly recommends
  dropping is dropped; any item a majority of graders call off the digital SAT SEC
  blueprint is HELD for Andy, not inserted. A frozen item flagged by sec-near-dup.mjs
  (passage >= 0.20 or skeleton identical to a live hard item) is HELD.
- **Cross-item tell:** if a majority of graders name the same batch-level heuristic, it
  is recorded against the batch even if no item drops on it. If graders name two kept
  items as sharing one template, both are inserted and the pair is recorded (nothing
  enforces one-per-form).
