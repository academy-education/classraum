## Why this is a new method and not a third copy of the v17 brief

REGISTER (2026-10-08, RW v17) held C&S WIC whole at +20.8 over a +10 bar and said a
third agent brief of the same kind must not be tried. Read item by item from the
committed v17 files (`sat-wic-hard-v17.batch.json`, `wic17-oo.*`, `wic17.ws-*.phase1.json`),
what leaked was four things, three of them the tells the ISEE sentence-completion
line (s19-s22) found and fixed on a structurally identical item type:

1. **The key was one pole of an antonym pair (ISEE s19's tell).** In 7 of 12 v17
   items the key's opposite was an option: contingent/definitive (A-02),
   nominal/efficient (A-04), composite/uniform (A-06), nominal/ruinous (B-03),
   integral/optional (B-04), generic/specified (B-05), reciprocal/charitable
   (B-06). Blind picks on those 7: **20 of 21 = 95.2%**. On the other 5: **1 of
   15 = 6.7%**. In the 24 live controls of the same render I read such a pair on
   3 items (muted/exaggerated, dispositive/contested, negotiation/imposition).
   This classification is my hand read, made after the result; it is a hypothesis
   the v18 probe is pointed at, not a measurement.
   **Cause in the brief:** v17 asked for passages that overturn a stated view and
   for a distractor killed by each sentence, so the overturned view's word
   (optional, charitable, uniform, specified, definitive) went into the options as
   the key's opposite.
2. **The key was the rarest / most test-like word (ISEE s20's tell).** The blind
   samples labelled 9 candidate picks "rarer/precise word" or "formal word"; 7 of
   the 9 hit. Every v17 key was an adjective (12/12), against 7 of 24 live
   controls; live keys are mostly nouns and verbs in a precise sense (claiming,
   edition, requires, price, routes).
3. **A cross-item repeat.** "nominal" keyed two items (A-04, B-03): 6 of the 21
   candidate hits, named "option repeated across items". Without them the arm was
   still +12.5.
4. **Every distractor was killed by its own planted clause** (all three graders,
   unprompted): "within seconds" kills sluggish, "read aloud" kills secretive.
   "Pick the option the passage never contradicts" solved most items without the
   argument, and the panel rated 1 hard / 8 medium / 3 easy. This is the brief's
   own rule ("each distractor killed by a different sentence") turned template.

The v18 brief removes the mechanism of each, by the ISEE fix that removed the same
mechanism there:

| v17 leak | ISEE fix borrowed | v18 rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 antonym pole | s19/s20: no antonym or near-opposite among the options; no three options sharing a polarity the key lacks | **R1** no option is the antonym or near-opposite of the key or of another option; the word for a view the passage revises is never an option; `antonym_pairs: []` and `polarity` declared per item, probe-checked |
| 1 (cause) | s20: hard difficulty from the sentence, never a reversal whose opposite is an option | **R4** difficulty from the passage's logic; a contrast word may point at the key only if the key's opposite is absent; "received view overturned" at most 2 of 10 per author |
| 2 test word | s20-s22: distractors as specific and test-worthy as the key, with a named rarity ceiling; key-rarest about 1 in 4 | **R3** rarity ceiling named (below); `rarity_order` declared; key rarest in at most 2 of 10 and most common in at least 2 of 10; at least 4 of 10 keys nouns or verbs |
| 3 cross-item repeat | s19-s22: disjoint initial-letter ranges per author + a word list | **R6** disjoint letter ranges for every option word; no option word used twice in the batch; no key that is a live WIC key or option |
| 4 planted killers | s22 lesson: "hard must survive a reader who knows every word: two-step inference ... a near-miss distractor that fits all but one detail" | **R4** each distractor names its `killed_by`: `logic` (fails a step that combines two or more sentences) or `explicit` (one phrase contradicts it); at most ONE explicit per item; no sentence whose only job is to contradict one distractor |
| (stem side) | s20/s22: no a/an before the blank; a collocation only the key takes (IS22B-02 "disproportionate to") | **R5** no "a"/"an" directly before the blank; the word after the blank is grammatical and idiomatic with all four options (author states it per item) |

## Commission

**40 items, Words in Context, word-shaped** (single word or a two-word phrase; no
gloss options), **32 hard / 8 medium**. Four Claude authors, 10 each (8 hard / 2
medium), one JSON file per item, 5-7 items per run (two runs of 5 each):

    author  option-word letters  lane
    A       a-c                  life and earth sciences
    B       d-i                  history, economics, law, social science
    C       j-q                  arts, literature, music, architecture
    D       r-z                  technology, medicine, linguistics, engineering

Every option word (key and distractors) starts with a letter in the author's
range, so no cross-author word collision is possible. Medium = a hard passage with
a commoner key, not an easier passage (s21).

Item rules beyond R1-R6:
- Passage 50-110 words, academic register, one blank `______`, prompt "Which
  choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase?"
- **Rarity ceiling (all four options):** a word a strong 11th-grader reads
  unglossed in a quality newspaper or a textbook. Out: technical terms
  (ductile, hygroscopic, allopatric), GRE-only rarities (recondite, sedulous,
  pellucid, inchoate, perspicacious, lachrymose, sententious, plangent), and
  senses a 17-year-old would not know. A distractor is never rarer than this,
  whatever the key is. Denylisted in the self-check.
- Argument shapes varied: at least 4 distinct shapes per author (definition by
  example, cause and mechanism, comparison of two cases, concession then claim,
  method and its purpose, scope limit, process sequence ...), "received view
  overturned" at most 2 of 10.
- The key needs at least two sentences for hard items (`key_from_one_sentence`
  false); a medium may be decided by one.
- Proper nouns: invented, not reused across items or authors (v17's "Ines Varga"
  twice); checked at merge.
- Author-only fields, stripped at freeze: `antonym_pairs`, `polarity`,
  `rarity_order`, `killed_by`, `after_blank`, `shape`, `selfcheck`.

## Process (shared 20-subagent cap)

Claude agents only, never GPT. At most 2 of this batch's subagents at once.
Completion judged by files on disk (`sat-cs-wic-v18-work/<A-D>/<ID>.json`), never by
a notification; a subagent idle 6+ minutes is relaunched from its disk state. Every
subagent prompt bans pkill, killall and any process-killing command. No new env var.

## Pre-freeze steering (decides nothing at the gate)

- **Self-check** (`wic18-selfcheck.mjs`, per author file): shape; letter range;
  denylist; batch-wide option-word uniqueness; live WIC key/option collision; "a"/"an"
  before the blank; declared `antonym_pairs` empty; polarity rule; rarity-order
  counts; noun/verb share; at most one `explicit` kill per item; shape counts;
  proper nouns; key strictly longest / strictly shortest each 15-35% batch-wide.
- **Options-only probe** (one fresh Claude agent, never an author/solver/grader):
  the 40 candidates as option sets only, keys dealt flat, interleaved with **six
  calibration items it is not told about** — v17 items A-04, A-06, B-03, B-04, B-05,
  B-06 (each carries a key-antonym pair I read above). Per item: forced picks for
  `test_word` (rarest / most test-like), `antonym_pole`, `odd_one_out`,
  `most_specific`; `opposite_pairs`; `above_ceiling` words. **Bar: each forced pick
  hits the key on fewer than 35% of an author's items (10 -> at most 3).** The
  probe is a reader only if it names an opposite pair on at least 4 of the 6
  calibration items; if not, a second fresh probe is run and both are recorded.
- **Difficulty probe** (one more fresh agent, with passages, no keys): per item
  `difficulty` (e/m/h vs the real module-2 hard route), cold `pick`,
  `explicit_kills` (distractors contradicted by one planted phrase),
  `key_from_one_sentence`.
- **Named for the ONE revision round:** any item with an above-ceiling word, a
  probe-named opposite pair, a polarity breach; the key-hit items of any forced
  pick at >= 35% of its author file; any authored-hard item the difficulty probe
  calls easy or reads as key-from-one-sentence; any cold miss. Revisions touch named
  items only. Then one fresh r2 options-only probe re-reads all 40; r2 is recorded
  at freeze whatever it says.

## Freeze

`wic18-freeze.mjs` merges the four files into `sat-cs-wic-v18.batch.json`, strips
author fields, deals stored choice order from a seeded balanced deck (10 keys per
letter), prints sha256, committed before any solver or grader sees it. **No repairs
after freeze.** A dropped item stays dropped.

## Stages, in order. Stop at the first failed stage: nothing inserts.

1. **Shape / pre-flight** (refuse before freeze): `bank-helper.mjs check`;
   self-check green; `rw17-dupscan.mjs` against ALL live R&W rows (paged,
   count-asserted). A dup-scan flag (passage trigram Jaccard >= 0.20, >= 3 of 4
   options shared, key = a live WIC key) on the FROZEN file -> that item HELD.
2. **Options-only (nosource).** `wic18-oo-render.mjs`: 40 candidates + **the whole
   eligible live control: every verified, unarchived, word-shaped WIC item, hard
   and medium = 39 today** (gloss-shaped excluded as in v17; if the render counts a
   different number, the control is that whole count, reported). Keys dealt flat
   within each arm (candidate 10/10/10/10 = 25.0%; control 39 -> 10/10/10/9 =
   25.6%), arms interleaved. Three samples of one Claude solver prompt (three
   samples, not three solvers), each returns pick, basis, heuristic, note.
   Scored by `score-oo.mjs`: M = arm rate - its own best-fixed-letter line;
   **E = M_candidate - M_control. PASS: E <= +10. HOLD whole: E > +10.**
   - **Attainable range, stated now.** M_c spans [-25.0, +75.0]. The control is
     MEASURED only inside (letter line + 5, 90%] = **(30.6%, 90%]**; WIC word
     controls read 36.4% (wic7) and 37.5% (v17), so inside. With the control at
     any value in that range, E can exceed +10 (headroom >= 10 pts below 100%) and
     can be <= +10, so the bar fires both ways. **If the control is outside the
     range the blind half is NOT MEASURED, not passed**: the batch then continues
     only if M_c <= +10 against its own letter line (stricter, no control credit).
   - 120 candidate picks through three correlated samples are worth about 40:
     roughly +-8 points of noise on M_c. Reported, the bar is the bar.
   - Printed, not decided: hard sub-arm (authored), unanimity rate vs control,
     candidate hit rate on picks whose stated heuristic is a rarity/test-word
     reading, and on items the r2 probe paired with an antonym.
3. **Elimination (A23).** `elimination-paired.mjs render rw-v18-cs-wic --domain
   "Craft and Structure" --tag wic18-elim --batch <frozen> --match subskill --ratio 1`
   (40 controls from the 49 live WIC rows; ratio 2 would need 80). Separate files,
   three samples per arm. Bar as committed in the script: margin <= +20, control
   <= 80%, n >= 12.
4. **With-source.** `BLIND=bare bank-helper.mjs blind <frozen>`, asserted to carry
   no subskill string. Three fresh Claude graders (ws-d/e/f), never an author,
   probe or solver. Phase 1 cold answers + every judgement written to disk and
   sha-recorded before phase 2 shows key and explanation. Fields per item:
   key_ok, my_answer, exclusive, second_defensible, n_struck, distractor_quality,
   key_from_one_sentence, resolving_word_after_blank, difficulty (vs the real
   module-2 hard route), above_ceiling (array of the item's words), antonym_pair
   (null or two option words), path_coherent, recommend_drop, off_blueprint, note.
   **Stage bar: any grader's cold pick misses the key on more than 4 of 40 items
   -> FAIL whole.** Per-item, `wic18-gate.mjs`:
   - DROP: gate-verdict.mjs rules 1-6 unchanged (1 key disputed by any grader, a
     cold miss counts; 2 non-exclusive by majority; 3 distractors weak by
     majority; 4 median n_struck >= 2; 5 explanation path wrong per any grader;
     6 resolving word after the blank by majority); key above ceiling per any
     grader or the same distractor above ceiling per >= 2; an antonym pair named
     by >= 2 graders (R1 breach); recommend_drop by any grader.
   - HELD for Andy: majority off_blueprint.
   - **Difficulty = panel MEDIAN; median easy -> DROP.** Rule 8: a grader majority
     marking `key_from_one_sentence` caps the item at medium.
   - **"Hard" for the hard route = panel median hard and not rule-8 capped.**
5. **Tells**, on the kept file: `verify-answer-key-spread.ts --batch` no FAIL;
   key strictly longest and strictly shortest each <= 35%; 0 option words repeated
   across kept items; solver heuristic: if all three samples name one heuristic and
   the candidate picks it decided score > 70% on >= 15 picks, FAIL.
