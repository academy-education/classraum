## Authoring rules (the brief carries each verbatim)

1. **One mechanism per item, all 60 distinct.** Each item declares `mechanism`
   (2-4 short keyword phrases). No item may share its mechanism with another
   in this batch, with any live maths row in any family, or with any prior
   Advanced Math batch item (v16-v24 including held, dropped and stage-0
   dropped). A mechanism duplicate is the same chain of steps on the same kind
   of givens to the same asked quantity; a new surface story over the same
   chain IS a duplicate. Authors receive the live SAT Advanced Math dump (447),
   the all-family live maths dump (3,165) and the prior batch files, and run
   at least two keyword searches per item before writing it, recording the
   searches and the nearest hit in `dup_search`.
2. `solve` (independent recompute) and `distractor_solve` for **every**
   distractor, rounding included (skill §2).
3. Distractors from **different named wrong paths**; no derivational hub, no
   key-as-unique-composite, no +-pair containing the key, no closure (key =
   sum/difference/mean/double of two options), no evenly spaced run beside the
   key, no grid of one quantity through factors, no decimal-shift sibling of
   the key, no singleton parity/sign/integrality/roundness/multiple-of-k tell.
4. **(c) No one-sided error family**: an extreme key needs distractors with at
   least two distinct `error_kind`s **and** errors whose direction is not
   predictable from the stem. Each distractor declares
   `distractor_meta: {error_kind, direction}`.
5. **No cheap single bound on an extreme key**: `cheap_bound_check` names the
   cheapest one-sided bound readable from the stem and shows at least one
   distractor lies on the key's side of it.
6. **Shape check (A86's largest drop class).** Every option is a LEGAL value of
   the quantity asked, and no option violates a qualitative property the stem
   hands over: the function's shape (convex / concave, increasing /
   decreasing, symmetric about a stated axis), a stated cap or rate, an
   exponent above or below 1, solutions coming in ± pairs, an integer or
   positive quantity by context. Each item records `shape_check`: the
   properties the stem hands over and, for each, which options survive it (at
   least two must survive every one, together).
7. **(d) Interior parity**: the two interior options (ranks 2 and 3 by value)
   have the same display form and display complexity within 1; the key is not
   the cleaner interior value. **(e)** No two options summing to 1, 90, 180,
   360 (or 100 with %); no shared-denominator structure singling out the key.
8. **Plug-back and ask type (A87).** No item asks for a quantity a student can
   confirm by substituting an option into a printed equation or condition in
   one routine step: not the solution x of an equation, not a zero, not the
   input where f(x) = c, not a parameter that makes a printed equation true.
   **No `greatest` / `least` / "what is the value of" ask whose options can be
   tried in order** (descending for greatest, ascending for least) and
   confirmed in <= 2 routine substitutions. Ask for a derived quantity (a
   product or sum of solutions, a coordinate of something built from the
   solution, a parameter fixed by a structural condition, a value of a
   different expression). Each item records `ask_type` and `plugback_check`
   (how a student would verify an option by substitution, and how many
   routine substitutions in the cheap order reach the key; it must be more
   than 2, or substitution must be impossible). **Direction ban**: no
   sign/monotonicity reading from the stem that kills sign-mismatched options
   without solving.
9. **Within one author's file, a repeated error kind does not always fall on
   the same side of the key** (A87: a mechanism's characteristic error sat
   below the key on every one of five items). An author who uses the same
   `error_kind` on three or more items puts it above the key on at least one
   and below on at least one.
10. No reviewer-facing text in `explanation`; no option value shared across
    items in one author's file; ASCII hyphen-minus `-` in choices and in
    compound words ("xy-plane"), never U+2212.
11. **Medium means two real steps**; a single routine step is easy, and easy is
    capped at insert (below). Easy items (A01, C09) are real SAT easy items,
    still under rules 1-10. Hard items have a decision point that routine
    procedure (including Desmos) does not bypass.

Author fields `mechanism`, `quantity_asked`, `ask_type`, `plugback_check`,
`shape_check`, `distractor_meta`, `cheap_bound_check`, `interior_parity_check`,
`arith_class_check`, `dup_search`, `bounds`, `self_audit` are stripped before
any grader or solver reads the file: the grader render is made from a copy
with every author-only field deleted, and `make-grade-render.mjs` names any
unrecognised field it keeps, which must be none; the blind render carries
options only.

## Stage 0 — pre-flight (per author file and on the merged set)

`adv25-preflight.sh <file>` = `adv24-preflight.sh` plus `adv25-slots.mjs`:

- `math-bank-helper.mjs verify`: every key and every `distractor_solve`
  recomputes (N/N, 3N/3N). Mutation: one changed key and one changed
  distractor body must both be refused.
- **Assigned slot honoured** (`adv25-slots.mjs`): a mismatch is a return.
- `check-sign-pair.mjs` zero key-in-pair; `check-key-is-composition.mjs` zero;
  `check-explanation-hygiene.mjs` zero; `check-math-hub.mjs` and
  `check-symbolic-hub.mjs` recorded (hub margin > +10 = return).
- `key-extremity-gate.mjs` PASS on the merged set; **key-position band on the
  merged set: smallest and largest each 14-20, interior 23-30 at 60** (scaled
  to the frozen count: each extreme within ±3 of 17/60 × N, interior within
  -3/+4 of 26/60 × N); `check-key-magnitude.mjs sat/math` reported.
- `check-interior-niceness.mjs`: any `parity`, `KEY-CLEANER`, `complement` or
  `denominators` line is a return.
- `check-key-arith-class.mjs N` as a reading list; every lone-key line read by
  me against its stem.
- `stem-duplicates.mjs --family sat`, `check-math-near-dup.mjs` (prompt
  Jaccard >= 0.60 or an equal option multiset = return),
  `check-duplicate-option-sets.mjs sat/math`.
- **Duplicates, two channels:** `math-mechanism-dup.mjs N` over every live
  maths row (every FLAG read by hand), **and my own hand keyword search per
  item** of the all-family live maths dump, the prior Advanced Math batch
  files (v16-v24 incl. held / dropped / stage-0 dropped) and this batch (2-4
  regexes per item, every hit read), recorded per item in
  `adv25.dupsearch.json` with the searches run, the hit count and the
  decision. A same-mechanism hit is a return.
- Merge: duplicate ids, shared key values, shared option values, closure.
- Reported, not gated: ask-type → key-rank rule (the best single rule's hit
  rate, as alg26 R7) and, per author, error-kind direction balance (rule 9).

**One return per author** for any stage-0 failure; the returned file re-runs
all of stage 0. An item still failing after its return is **dropped before
freeze**, not returned again. A batch-level stage-0 bar (extremity gate,
key-position band) failing after the return **stops the batch**. No model
reader sees any item before the freeze commit.

## Stage 1 — options-only attack (blind half)

`make-adv25-oo.mjs` = `make-adv24-oo.mjs` with EXCLUDE += `sat-math-v25-adv`
and seed 20261027: **composition-matched control, one live Advanced Math item
per candidate in the candidate's AUTHORED band** (eligible today: hard 48,
medium 118, easy 5; need 17 / 41 / 2 at most), numeric option sets only, keys
dealt flat per arm, minus glyph normalised, interleaved. Three blind samples of
one Claude solver, recorded as three samples of one solver. `score-oo.mjs`.

- **Batch HOLD** if candidate minus control > **+10** points.
- **Batch HOLD** if the candidate unanimous-correct rate exceeds the control's
  by > **15** points.
- **Ceiling:** attainable control range 25% (letter line) to 100%; both bars
  fire only while the control is <= 90%. A control above 90% = blind half NOT
  MEASURED: the batch stops (not a pass).
- Per item: unanimity alone never drops; it needs with-source corroboration.
- Reported, not gated: hit rate extreme-key / interior-key per arm.

An options-only result is never reported as "does not leak".

## Stage 2 — with-source graders (decides per item)

Three fresh Claude graders (D, E, F), each over all frozen items in two runs
(positions 1-30, 31-60 of the seeded order). **Stage 1 cold**
(`make-grade-render.mjs` of the frozen file: stem + options, re-dealt, no
key/explanation/paths/author fields): solve, exclusivity, free eliminations
with stem visible (struck options), plug-back count in the cheap order,
`error_directions` per distractor and `one_sided`, `extreme_single_bound`,
`interior_parity`, arithmetic-class tell, Desmos/insight bypass, difficulty,
on_blueprint, duplicate note. **Written to disk before stage 2 opens the keyed
file** (key, explanation, distractor paths): key_ok, path_coherent,
distractors_weak, drop_recommend, final difficulty. Completion judged by files
on disk.

**Per-item drop, any one:**
1. key disputed by any grader (cold answer != key and not withdrawn as a pure
   arithmetic slip on re-check);
2. not exclusive by majority;
3. distractors weak by majority;
4. median >= 2 of 3 distractors free-strikable without solving, or the key
   identifiable free by any grader (confirmed by me);
5. a `distractor_solve` / explanation path that does not produce its value
   (any grader, confirmed by me);
6. a majority report plug-back in the cheap order reaches the key in <= 2
   routine substitutions;
7. a confirmed (a)/(b) arithmetic-class or option-structure tell (any grader,
   confirmed by me);
8. a confirmed (c) one-sided error family on an extreme key (any grader,
   confirmed by me, from the graders' `error_directions`, never the author's
   declaration);
9. a confirmed (d)/(e) interior-parity, complement or shared-denominator tell
   (any grader, confirmed by me);
10. an extreme key with a confirmed single bound cheaper than the item's work
    putting all three distractors on or beyond it (any grader, confirmed by me);
11. a mechanism duplicate of a live row or of an earlier item (seeded order)
    in this batch, named by >= 2 graders or by one grader and confirmed by me
    (the later item drops);
- **EXT-1:** any grader's `drop_recommend` -> drop.
- **HOLD:** majority off the SAT Advanced Math blueprint -> held for Andy
  (`sat-math-v25-adv.held.batch.json`), not inserted.

**Difficulty banked = three-grader panel median** (question verbatim from
adv24): "hard" = a decision point that routine procedure (including Desmos)
does not bypass; "medium" = careful multi-step procedure reaches the key;

## Stop rule

Stop at the first failed gate: a batch-level stage-0 bar after the return, a
stage-1 hold, a control above the ceiling, or the stage-4 extremity gate. On a
stop nothing is inserted and nothing is repaired; the result is recorded with
denominators.
