## Sitting 1 — Choose a Response (run `crv10-cofounder-2026-09-03`, 20 items)

- You will see four possible replies and NO audio line. Pick the reply
  you think is the answer, from the options alone. Guess when you have
  nothing; that is the point.
- Then mark whether one reply is clearly the only good one (unique) and
  whether the exchange sounds like real speech (authentic).
- About 10 minutes. Keys are dealt flat, so pure guessing scores 25%.

## Sitting 2 — ACT Science (run `act-science-cofounder-2026-09-03`, 20 items)

- Becomes available the moment Sitting 1 is submitted (one open run per
  reviewer). Same screen.
- You will see the question and four options, WITHOUT the passage,
  tables or figures. Pick from the options alone. If you can answer from
  what you know about science or from the way the question is worded,
  do — that is exactly what we need to measure.
- About 12 minutes.

What the numbers decide (fixed before the sitting, same rule as ACT):
- at or below ~40% blind: clean; cr-v10 stays live, Science unhides.
- at or above ~60%: a person can guess it too; cr-v10 is pulled, Science
  stays hidden and is rebuilt.
- between: a second reader.

The model attack scored these at 34% (Choose a Response) and 71%
(Science); on the last ACT sitting the model said 76-79% and you scored
10%, which is why the human number is the one that counts.
