# ATV2 b3 pre-freeze review — atv2-b3-quads.json (verbatim reviewer report, 2026-08-18)

Reviewer independent of the author. File read in full (8 lectures × 4 pivots × 4
settings = 128 settings; no truncation). Cross-batch comparison against the frozen
pilot (atv2-quads.json), atv2-b1-quads.json, and atv2-b2-quads.json. One mechanical
fact verified before calibrating severity: `atv2-render.mjs` places the three
non-chosen settings onto letters via a per-pivot seeded shuffle
(`rng(SEED:place:<lid>:<pid>)`), so the file-order slot of a setting does NOT
survive to the screen. Slot-position observations below are therefore recast as
FAMILY-COMPOSITION observations (which the solver does see, slate by slate);
question order within a set (q1→q4) also survives and is treated as visible.

## §4 CROSS-LECTURE AND CROSS-BATCH — three items must be fixed before freeze

**§4-A (REQUIRED, batch-level): the defer/deflect/recuse/reframe verdict family
saturates the attitude pivots.** Tally across b3's seven attitude slates:

    p1/q4 s4   "…leaves the idea hard to test"            deflect (lone in slate)
    p2/q2      —                                          none (clean)
    p3/q3      —                                          none (s4 is a committed rejection)

    p6/q3 s4   "Unsettled either way…"                    defer (lone in slate)
    p8/q3 s4   "disagreement about what it set out to…"   reframe (lone in slate)

Five of seven slates carry the family (six instances), and in three of them it is
the LONE non-position. The pilot's advisory ceiling was three of six lectures,
and the pilot's required §4 fix said explicitly: let some slates omit the defer
option entirely. b1 and b2 obeyed (roughly one instance each — b2-p7/q2 s3 "with
neither"; b1 essentially none); b3 regresses past the pilot's own level. Letter
placement is shuffled per pivot, so this is not a slot tell — it is a slate-
composition recipe: a solver who has seen a few ATV2 attitude items learns every
slate contains exactly one wash-my-hands option, which is the kind of cross-item
classifiability the design's anti-rigid-brief section forbids and the pilot review
blocked on. **Fix: swap the family out of at least three of {p1/q4 s4, p5/q2 s4,
p6/q3 s4, p8/q3 s4}, replacing each with a committed family the slate lacks (a
second acceptance on different evidence, an inversion, a scope rejection — the
moves p2/q2 and p3/q3 already model). Keep at most 2–3 carriers batch-wide, and
not all lone.** Positive note, recorded so it is not undone: the attitude TARGETS
vary well (colleague's hypothesis, named critic ×2, institutional decision ×2,
agency report, general idea), and s1-in-file-order is endorse-family in six of
seven slates — invisible after the placement shuffle, but reorder while editing
anyway; it costs nothing.

agency option.** b3-p1/q3 s4 "Turn the routine monitoring over to the regional
