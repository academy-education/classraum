## Brief (what is new)
12 items (so the elimination stage has n >= 12 per arm on the authored set), siblings (not copies) of the constructions that graded panel-median hard
in v10-v12: a clausal subject (that-/whether-clause) opening the second independent
clause (SEC11A-03, SEC12A-06); no mark before a delayed object (SEC11B-01, SEC12B-01);
no mark after a long restrictive subject with an internal comma pair (SEC11B-02); a
closing comma pair around a nested dash pair before a complement (SEC11B-04); comma +
coordinator before a clause that opens with a subordinate clause (SEC12B-02). Siblings
vary the mark that wins and the shape, never the same option template as a live item.

Recorded tells the brief must avoid, across the merged batch:
- **v12 drops:** no answer-resolving word within four words after the blank (a main
  verb right after a closing mark was the v12 drop); no free-strike distractors (median
  < 2 of 3 strikable); explanations must state a correct path.
- **v11 mark tell:** a dash is the key in >= 2 items, a colon in >= 1, a "however"
  construction in >= 1; a period option is a WRONG answer in >= 3 items; no single mark
  family is the key in more than 3 of 12.
- **v12 added-word tell:** an option that ADDS a word (conjunction, preposition,
  relative) IS the key in >= 2 items that are not otherwise easy; added-word options
  are wrong elsewhere too, so the feature does not predict either way.
- Two independent Claude authors (never GPT), 6 items each, disjoint construction
  lists; every distractor grammatical in isolation and wrong only in context; no two
  items on one template; key letters flat.

## Pipeline
two independent Claude authors -> merge -> structural pre-flight (make-attack.mjs key
spread, length ranks, template, punctuation asymmetry and the mark-family key histogram,
read by hand) -> options-only blind attack (3 fresh solver samples) + 3 fresh with-source
graders (ws-d/e/f) -> gate-verdict.mjs -> elimination-paired.mjs --batch (A23 bar) ->
mk-rw-qc.mjs -> ledger -> insert as rw-v13-sec-hard. **No audit-and-repair round, and no
repair after any gate stage.** Anything a gate stage catches is dropped or held, never
repaired and re-counted.

## Control

## Ceiling check
A "candidate exceeds control by more than 10" bar can fire only while the control is at
or below 90%. **If the control returns above 90% the blind half is reported NOT MEASURED,
not passed.** If the control returns at or below its own best-fixed-letter line + 5
points, the blind half is likewise reported NOT MEASURED.

## Bars
- **Blind (batch level):** candidate minus matched control <= **+10** points. Above that,
  the batch is HELD whole.
- **Unanimity** is a batch-level rate against the control's rate only (A53); never a
  per-item drop on its own.
- **Per-item drop** (gate-verdict.mjs, any one):
  1. key disputed by any grader (Conventions also needs 3/3 key votes at insert)
  2. not exclusive by majority
  3. distractors weak by majority
  4. median of 2+ of 3 distractors free-strikable
  5. explanation path wrong per any grader
  6. a grader-majority resolving word within four words after the blank
- **Elimination (A23, elimination-paired.mjs --batch):** candidate minus matched live
  control eliminable-rate <= +20 points, control <= 80%, n >= 12 per arm; else the stage
  does not pass. If fewer than 12 survive the with-source rule, the stage is run on all
  authored items before drops so n >= 12, and reported as such.
- **Difficulty:** panel MEDIAN of the three graders, never the author's label.
  **Panel-median easy is DROPPED, not relabelled.**
- **Conservative extensions (as v9-v12):** any item a grader explicitly recommends
  dropping is dropped; any item a majority of graders call off the digital SAT SEC
  blueprint is HELD for Andy, not inserted.
- **Cross-item tell:** if a majority of graders name the same batch-level heuristic, it
  is recorded against the batch even if no item drops on it.

## Discard / reporting
- Fewer than 2 hard survivors: insert what passes; the hard route stays at 5 and the
  commission is reported as not having bought form 6.
- Zero hard survivors is reported as a failed hard commission regardless of insert count.
