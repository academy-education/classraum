v25's "none narrower than 6 letters" floor cannot hold for five disjoint ranges over 26 letters (5 x 6 = 30). It
is replaced by a supply floor. The self-check refuses at start-up unless:
- the ranges are disjoint and cover a-z;
- no author's range overlaps its v25 range;
- each range holds >= 15% of the initials of the excluded-word pool, a proxy for how much WIC-register vocabulary
  starts there.

### Exclusion
The self-check refuses any option word that is:
- in `sat-cs-wic-v26.live-words.json`: live WIC, v17 held, and the v18/v19/v20/v23/v24 frozen files;
- **or** read directly from the frozen files: v19, v20, v23, v24 (40 items each) and **every v25 word** from
  `sat-cs-wic-v25.worlds.json` (32 items x 4 choices: kept and dropped, served and unserved; a four-world item has
  the same four choices in every world);
- or a stem or inflection of any of these, or a denylisted word, or a stem used twice in the batch.

The frozen-file net works without the live-words file (break-tested below).

### Per-item and per-author rules
v25's, unchanged, plus one new per-world rule:
- **No pivot.** No sentence may open with But / Yet / However / Still / Even so, and there may be no ", but" /
  ", yet" / "; but" / "; yet" clause. "But also" and "but for" are exempt.

## Pre-freeze steering: the world probe, RAISED (decides nothing at the gate)
The order is v25's: self-check green, then the world probe, then ONE revision round, then freeze. The probe runs
BEFORE the revision round, as in v25.

`wic26-wprobe.mjs`, seed 20262683:
- **Render.** Four renders. Render r holds world (r + i) mod 4 of item i. Each render goes to one fresh reader
  (`WPROBE-BRIEF.md`). `render` also writes `sat-cs-wic-v26-wprobe.snapshot.json`: the sha256 of every world's
  passage as the reader saw it.
- **Naming.** A world is named on v25's five reasons, plus two new probe fields:
  - `literal_fit`: a wrong option literally describes something the passage reports (v25 finding 6);
  - `presupposes_absent`: a phrase takes for granted something the passage never establishes (v25 finding 7).
- **NEW: an easy world goes back with the reader's note.**
  - `score` refuses (exit 2) if any world rated easy lacks a note naming what gave it away.
  - `score` writes `<L>/PROBE-FEEDBACK.json` for each author itself, with every named world's reasons, the
    reader's picked word, second-defensible word, literal-fit word and note, verbatim. v25 built this file by hand.
- **NEW: the revision must act on it.**
  - `verify` compares every world with the snapshot. It fails (exit 1) if a world its reader rated EASY is
    byte-identical after the revision round, if a world's key changed, or if an item vanished or appeared.
  - `wic26-select.mjs` runs `verify` and refuses to freeze unless it is green.
  - Other named worlds left unchanged are listed, not failed.
- **ONE revision round**, then freeze, whatever the outcome.

**What verify proves.** It proves that the author edited a world rated easy, not that the world got harder. A
one-word edit passes it. The stage-4 difficulty bar decides hardness, as in v25.

## Selection rule (v25's, with v26 constants)
`wic26-select.mjs`:
1. Refuse unless:
   - `wic26-selfcheck.mjs --final` is green;
   - each of the 5 authors has exactly 8 files;
   - **`wic26-wprobe.mjs verify` is green (new)**.
2. Write `sat-cs-wic-v26.worlds.json` and record its sha256.
3. Selection seed: **20262690 + int(sha[0:8]), mod 2^32.** mulberry32 draws world index floor(4u) per item, in id
   order.
4. **No re-roll** (v25's rejected-quota finding stands).
5. Letters come from a balanced deck, 10 per letter, rng(seed + 7919).
6. Write `sat-cs-wic-v26.batch.json` (served world only) and `sat-cs-wic-v26.selection.json`.
7. **No post-selection edits.** A served world that fails any gate drops its item WHOLE. An item is never
   re-served from another world. Unserved worlds are never banked or recycled, and their words stay excluded.

## Stages and bars (v25's, unchanged)
The run stops at the first failed stage.

- **Stage 1.** bank-helper structural check plus dupscan on `sat-cs-wic-v26.batch.json`.
- **Stage 2.** Options-only attack: `wic26-oo-render.mjs cs scripts/study-bank/sat-cs-wic-v26.batch.json --tag
  wic26`, seed 20262691, then three samples (`OO-SOLVER-BRIEF.md`) written to `wic26-oo.solver-{a,b,c}.json`,
  scored by `score-oo.mjs`.
  - **PASS if E = M_cand - M_ctl <= +10.**
  - Control: the WHOLE eligible word-shaped live WIC pool. The dry render counted **121** (30 hard, 91 medium),
    which now includes v25's 21 four-world rows. Those sit near chance by construction, so they lower the control
    rate and push E UP. That works against a pass, never for one.
  - Candidate line: 25.0% (10/10/10/10), so M_cand spans [-25, +75].
  - Control line: 25.6% (31/30/30/30). The control is measured only inside (30.6%, 90%]. v25 read 37.3% on 100
    rows; with the 21 added near 29%, about 36% is expected.
  - Outside that range, stage 2 is NOT MEASURED, and the batch continues only if M_cand <= +10.
  - A pass shows that selection and rendering did not leak. It is not evidence that the items are good.
- **Stage 3.** `elimination-paired.mjs render rw-v26-cs-wic --domain "Craft and Structure" --tag wic26-elim --batch
  sat-cs-wic-v26.batch.json --match subskill --ratio 1`. Margin <= +20, control <= 80%, n >= 12.
- **Stage 4.** `wic26-gate.mjs`. Three fresh graders (`GRADER-BRIEF.md`), never an author, probe reader or solver.
  - The cold phase is sha-recorded (`wic26.ws-phase1.sha256`) before the key is shown.
  - **Stage bar:** any grader cold-missing more than 10% fails the whole batch. At 40 items that means more than 4.
  - Per-item DROP, HELD and rule 8 are exactly v25's.
- **Stage 5.**
  - key spread: no FAIL;
  - key strictly longest <= 35%, and strictly shortest <= 35%;
  - no repeated option words;
  - **attrition check kept**: E_kept = M_kept - M_ctl <= +10 on the kept candidates. With fewer than 8 kept the set
    is HELD, not passed.
  - **One fix:** v25's gate compared the raw float `E > 10`, and an exact +10.0 FAILED (45.0% - 25.0% =
    20.000000000000004). v26 compares on 0.1-point rounding. A pre-fix run failed the +10.0 case. Post-fix, +10.0
    passes and +10.8 fails.

## What the method still cannot fix
Everything in v25's section of the same name still holds:
- per-world exclusivity and difficulty are the author's to get right;
- only the served world is graded;
- the four-world construction has with-source tells of its own;
- the gate is model-only, and the human sitting decides verbal cohorts.

New for v26:
- Rules A-E are brief rules. Only the pivot is checked mechanically, and only lexically. Kill-only clauses,
  literal fit, near-opposites, ceiling words and presupposition are caught by the probe reader, who decides
  nothing, or by graders on the served world only. An unserved world with a literal-fit distractor is never
  measured, which is acceptable only because it is never served.
- `verify` checks that an easy world was edited, not that it improved.
