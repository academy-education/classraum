# Sentence pairs

For each pair, two orderings of the same words are shown (punctuation other than the final period is deliberately absent).

P01
  X: Everyone enjoyed the concert except my brother.
  Y: Everyone except my brother enjoyed the concert.

P02
  X: He promised to return the book he borrowed.
  Y: He borrowed the book he promised to return.

P03
  X: She had trained for the race she managed to finish.
  Y: She managed to finish the race she had trained for.
