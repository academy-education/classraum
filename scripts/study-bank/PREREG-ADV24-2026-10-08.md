## Authoring rules (the brief carries each verbatim)

1. **One mechanism per item, all 60 distinct.** Each item declares `mechanism`
   (2-4 short keyword phrases). No item may share its mechanism with another
   in this batch or with any live maths row in any family. Authors receive a
   dump of all live SAT Advanced Math rows (409) and the v16-v21 Advanced Math
   batch files, and search them before writing each item.
2. `solve` (independent recompute) and `distractor_solve` for **every**
   distractor, rounding included (skill §2).
3. Distractors from **different named wrong paths**; no derivational hub, no
   key-as-unique-composite, no +-pair containing the key, no closure (key =
   sum/difference/mean/double of two options), no evenly spaced run beside the
   key, no grid of one quantity through factors, no singleton
   parity/sign/integrality/roundness/multiple-of-k tell (ACT (a)/(b)).
4. **(c) No one-sided error family**: an extreme key whose three distractors
   all err in a direction a student can read off the stem. Each distractor
   declares `distractor_meta: {error_kind, direction}`; an extreme key needs
   at least two distinct `error_kind`s **and** errors whose direction is not
   predictable from the stem.
5. **No cheap single bound on an extreme key** (ACT v25 rule 10): for each
   extreme-key item, `cheap_bound_check` names the cheapest one-sided bound
   readable from the stem (sign, monotonicity, containment, one-step estimate
   over the whole quantity asked, one step finer than the obvious cap) and
   shows at least one distractor lies on the key's side of it.
6. **(d) Interior parity**: the two interior options (ranks 2 and 3 by value)
   have the same display form (integer / decimal with the same places /
   fraction / radical) and display complexity within 1; the key is not the
   cleaner interior value. **(e)** No two options summing to 1, 90, 180, 360
   (or 100 with %); no shared-denominator structure singling out the key.
7. **Plug-back ban**: no item where trying the options in ascending order (or
   descending on a "greatest" stem) reaches the key in <= 2 routine
   substitutions. **Direction ban**: no sign/monotonicity reading from the stem
   that kills sign-mismatched options without solving.
8. No reviewer-facing text in `explanation`; no option value shared across
   items in one author's file; ASCII hyphen-minus `-` in choices and in
   compound words ("xy-plane"), never U+2212.
9. Easy items are real SAT easy items (one clear step), still under rules 1-8.

Author fields `mechanism`, `quantity_asked`, `distractor_meta`,
`cheap_bound_check`, `interior_parity_check`, `arith_class_check`, `bounds`,
`self_audit` are stripped before any grader or solver reads the file.

## Stage 0 — pre-flight (per author file and on the merged 60)

- `math-bank-helper.mjs verify`: every key and every `distractor_solve`
  recomputes (N/N, 3N/3N); extremity verdict printed. Mutation: one changed key
  and one changed distractor body must both be refused.
- `check-sign-pair.mjs` zero key-in-pair; `check-key-is-composition.mjs` zero;
  `check-explanation-hygiene.mjs` zero; `check-math-hub.mjs` and
  `check-symbolic-hub.mjs` run and recorded (hub margin > +10 = return).
- `key-extremity-gate.mjs` PASS on the merged 60; **key position band on the
  merged 60: smallest 14-20, largest 14-20, interior 23-30**
  (`key-rank-position.mjs`); `check-key-magnitude.mjs sat/math` reported.
- `check-interior-niceness.mjs`: any `parity`, `KEY-CLEANER`, `complement` or
  `denominators` line is a return.
- `check-key-arith-class.mjs 60` as a reading list; every lone-key line read by
  me against its stem.
- `stem-duplicates.mjs --family sat`, `check-math-near-dup.mjs` (prompt
  Jaccard >= 0.60 or an equal option multiset = return),
  `check-duplicate-option-sets.mjs sat/math`.
- **Duplicates:** `math-mechanism-dup.mjs 60` over every live maths row (every
  FLAG read by hand) **plus my hand keyword search of the full live maths dump
  per item**, recorded per item; within-batch overlaps checked the same way.
- `merge-halves.mjs`: duplicate ids, shared key values, shared option values,
  closure.

**One return per author** for any stage-0 failure (v20's one-return rule); the
returned file re-runs all of stage 0. An item still failing after its return
is **dropped before freeze**, not returned again. A batch-level stage-0 bar
(extremity gate, key-position band) failing after the return **stops the
batch**. No model reader sees any item before the freeze commit.

## Stage 2 — with-source graders (decides per item)

Three fresh Claude graders (D, E, F), each over all 60 in two runs of 30.
**Stage 1 cold** (`make-grade-render.mjs` of the stripped frozen file: stem +
options, re-dealt, no key/explanation/paths): solve, exclusivity, free
eliminations with stem visible (struck options), ascending plug-back count,
`error_directions` per distractor and `one_sided`, `extreme_single_bound`,
`interior_parity`, arithmetic-class tell, Desmos/insight bypass, difficulty,
on_blueprint, duplicate note. **Written to disk before stage 2 opens the
keyed file** (key, explanation, distractor paths): key_ok, path_coherent,
distractors_weak, drop_recommend, final difficulty. Stage completion is judged
by files on disk.

**Per-item drop, any one:**
1. key disputed by any grader (cold answer != key and not withdrawn as a pure
   arithmetic slip on re-check);
2. not exclusive by majority;
3. distractors weak by majority;
4. median >= 2 of 3 distractors free-strikable without solving, or the key
   free-strikable by any grader;
5. a `distractor_solve` / explanation path that does not produce its value
   (any grader, confirmed by me);
6. a majority report ascending plug-back reaches the key in <= 2 routine
   substitutions;
7. a confirmed (a)/(b) arithmetic-class or option-structure tell (any grader,
   confirmed by me);
8. a confirmed (c) one-sided error family on an extreme key (any grader,
   confirmed by me, from the graders' `error_directions`, never the author's
   declaration);
9. a confirmed (d)/(e) interior-parity, complement or shared-denominator tell
   (any grader, confirmed by me);
10. an extreme key with a confirmed single bound cheaper than the item's work
    putting all three distractors on or beyond it (any grader, confirmed by me);
11. a mechanism duplicate of a live row or of an earlier item in this batch
    found after freeze (the later item drops);
- **EXT-1:** any grader's `drop_recommend` -> drop.
- **HOLD:** majority off the SAT Advanced Math blueprint -> held for Andy
  (`.held.batch.json`), not inserted.

**Difficulty banked = three-grader panel median**, question verbatim from
v18-v23: "hard" means the item belongs on the module-2 hard route because it
has a decision point that routine procedure (including Desmos) does not
bypass; "medium" means careful multi-step procedure reaches the key; "easy"
means one routine step. **Panel-median easy is inserted as easy** (the live
mix holds easy), **capped at 6 items**; any easy survivors past 6 (in id
order) are held, not inserted, so the kept mix stays near live.
