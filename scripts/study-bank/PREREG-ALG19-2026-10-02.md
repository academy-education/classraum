# sat-math-v19-alg — pre-registration

Written 2026-10-02 before any item exists. Committed before authoring.

## Why this batch
The SAT Math module-2 HARD route is 8 forms, capped by Algebra hard = 65 at
~8 per form (`form-capacity.mjs`, `bank-state.mjs counts`, 2026-10-02). Next
binding domain on the hard route is Advanced Math (114 / 8 = 14). So:

    +7 panel-median-HARD Algebra   ->  hard route 9 forms
    +15                             ->  10 forms

Full forms are bound by Algebra 398/15 AND Advanced Math 397/15; form 27 needs
405 in both, so this batch alone cannot move the full-form count.

## Why v18 failed, and what changes (A65)
v18 Algebra: 14 authored as hard, 9 inserted, all nine panel-median MEDIUM.
The three items the panel called hard were off the SAT Algebra blueprint
(rational equation, rational inequality, divisor counting). Every on-blueprint
item was judged "insight-bypassable": the intended insight was a nicer route
to an answer routine procedure also reaches (small-integer solutions; one
tier comparison; a decorative "exactly one solution" clause).

So the v19 brief changes the TARGET, not the length: each item must contain a
decision point that routine procedure does not bypass, while staying inside
the published Algebra domain (linear equations in one variable, linear
functions, linear equations in two variables, systems of two linear equations
in two variables, linear inequalities in one or two variables). Named
mechanisms: literal parameters with a solution-count condition where the
coefficient condition alone yields a wrong candidate; quantities fixed only as
a combination; linear functions recovered from transformed/abstract
information; integer optima of two-constraint inequalities not at the vertex;
setups whose trap is in translation (staggered starts, replacement, net
change). Off-blueprint constructs are banned in the brief.

## Pipeline (v17 pipeline, unchanged)
author (two halves, separate subagents) -> sandbox `verify` -> pre-flights ->
INDEPENDENT no-arithmetic auditor (stem + options, before the gate) -> ONE
repair -> GATE with fresh blind solvers and fresh with-source graders.
All models are Claude subagents.

## Pre-flight (all must pass on the merged file before any solver runs)
- `math-bank-helper.mjs verify`: every key and every `distractor_solve` recomputes.
- `check-sign-pair.mjs`: zero ±pair sets containing the key.
- `check-key-is-composition.mjs`: zero key-as-unique-composite.
- `check-explanation-hygiene.mjs`: zero reviewer-facing text.
- `merge-halves.mjs`: no duplicate ids/keys/closure relations.
- `check-key-extremity.mjs`, `check-key-singleton.mjs`, `check-math-hub.mjs`:
  run and recorded; extremity is a distribution target (A56/A58), not a per-item rule.
- All option sets numeric (the live hard non-`v2` control is 31/32 numeric).

## Control (blind half)
Options-only render (`make-oo-render.mjs --control N --control-difficulty hard`),
N = candidate count, drawn from live Algebra HARD band excluding `v2`
(A65/A66/A68: `v2` carried the ±pair tell) and this month's session cohorts
`sat-math-v17-alg,sat-math-v18-alg,sat-math-v19-alg`. Eligible pool on
2026-10-02: 32 hard non-`v2` items, 31 numeric. Pool and band printed by the
script and copied into the register entry.

## Ceiling check
Non-`v2` hard controls measured 21.4% (A68) and 26.2% (A67, Advanced Math).
The +10 bar can fire in either direction while the control sits at or below
90%. If the control returns above 90% the blind half is NOT MEASURED, not
passed. If the control returns at its letter line the bar stands as written.

## Bars
- Blind: candidate minus matched control <= +10 points. Unanimity is reported
  as a batch-level RATE against the control's rate only (A53: three samples of
  one solver), never as a per-item drop.
- Per-item drop, any one (median of three graders on counts):
  1. key disputed by any grader
  2. not exclusive by majority
  3. distractors weak by majority
  4. median >= 2 of 3 distractors free-strikable without arithmetic
  5. explanation path that does not produce its printed value
- EXT-1: any item a grader explicitly recommends dropping is dropped.
- HOLD: any item a grader majority calls off the published SAT Algebra
  blueprint is held for Andy, not inserted.
- Difficulty: the banked label is the three-grader PANEL MEDIAN, never the
  author's. Graders are asked the same question as v18: "hard" means the item
  belongs on the module-2 hard route because it has a decision point that
  routine procedure does not bypass; "medium" means careful multi-step
  procedure reaches the key. Easy is rejected (BANK_BAND default hard).

## Need and discard
- Medium survivors ARE inserted, labelled medium (honest, and Algebra binds
  full forms). They do not count toward the hard route.
- Zero panel-median-hard survivors = a FAILED HARD COMMISSION, reported as
  such regardless of insert count.
- 1-6 hard survivors: inserted; hard route stays 8; reported as partial.
- If the gate finds a class of free elimination the auditor missed entirely,
  that is recorded as a pipeline failure regardless of item count.
- No second repair round. A repaired item does not re-enter the gate a second time.
