# MAP Growth pilot 5, 2026-10-07: ADAPT our own gated items. Pre-registration

Written and committed **before any adapted item exists.** Results go in
`MAP-ADAPT-PILOT-2026-10-07.md`.

**Owner idea, 2026-10-07:** build MAP Reading/Language items by ADAPTING our
own already-gated items from other tests into MAP's format, instead of writing
comprehension from scratch.

Why this is a different bet from pilots 1-4 (REGISTER A79/A81, MAP-RESEARCH
§14-17): those failed on QUALITY of agent-written comprehension (exclusivity,
difficulty, naturalness), not on guessability once the variant method was
used. Here the key logic, the distractor structure and (for most sources) a
blind human result already exist. The agent only re-levels and re-formats.
The question is whether that survives the trip down to grades 5-8.

## Source policy (unchanged from pilots 1-4, plus one rule)

- No NWEA item, live or reconstructed, is opened by anyone, **never the
  Herald / cloud.dherald.com PDFs** (memory: map-herald-pdfs-are-live-items).
  Authors and graders get only NWEA's public CCSS_2024 strand names
  (MAP-RESEARCH §12) and general CCSS ELA grade 5-8 standards.
- **New rule: sources are live, verified items this project wrote** (source
  `hand`, method claude-authored + QC). Nothing generated by another vendor's
  model and nothing harvested from a published test.
- Claude subagents only (never GPT), at most two at once. Prompts are fixed in
  `map-adapt/prompts.md`, the adapter brief in `MAP-ADAPT-BRIEF.md`, both
  committed with this file. No adapter, grader or solver sees this file, any
  bar or any earlier MAP item.

## MAP's shape, from the code and the research

There is no MAP blueprint in code. MAP appears only as an onboarding label and
as `available: false` in `preferences/page.tsx`. No family, no inserter, no
ledger, no topic. The shape below therefore comes from MAP-RESEARCH:

- Choice count: four (§4, "plausible but unconfirmed"; used by pilots 1-4).
- Reading 2-5 and 6+ share three strands: Literary Text, Informational Text,
  Vocabulary. They are balanced, 13 items each, and item sets usually have 4
  items per passage (§4, §5, §12).
- Language Usage 2+ is balanced across Writing, Grammar/Usage and Mechanics.
- Bands, as in pilots 3-4: 180-189 typical grade 5, 190-199 grade 6, 200-209
  grade 7, 210-219 grade 8.

## Adaptation rules (summary; the brief is binding)

- R1: the key does not change. Vocabulary keeps the word and sense. WIC and
  transitions keep the key word.
- R2: each kept distractor keeps its source role.
- R3: five-choice SSAT sources drop exactly one distractor (recorded).
- R4: passages are rewritten once per set: 200-350 words, FK 4.0-8.9, and
  every key-support and kept-distractor refutation is kept. Flat denials are
  turned into narration where possible.
- R5: standalone passages are 40-120 words.
- R6: stems in plain MAP style.
- R7: the usual option rules.
- R8: per item, `changes`, `distractor_map` and `dropped_distractor` record
  exactly what changed.

Two adapters work in parallel: one on the 12 set items, one on the 12
standalones.

## Stages, in order. STOP at the first stage that fails

**Stage 0: fidelity and exact checks (pre-freeze; repairs allowed once)**
- Fidelity: one fresh reviewer (prompt 2) per source-adapted pair judges
  `key_preserved`, `new_defensible` and `roles_preserved`.
- Exact checks: `map-adapt-checks.mjs` runs X1-X5 plus batch 1's E1
  (readability) and E3 (key length, <=25%), imported unchanged.
- Any item failing F or an exact check gets ONE repair (prompt 2r), a re-check
  by a fresh reviewer, and re-run checks. Still failing means it is dropped.
- **Stage 0 fails if fewer than 22 of 24 survive.**
- Survivors are frozen (commit). A dropped item counts as a FAIL in C and
  S1-b, whose denominator stays 24, and its source is not rendered for P.
- `roles_preserved` is reported, not deciding.

**Stage 1: options-only (after freeze).** `map-adapt-render.mjs oo` writes
TWO blind files:
- adapted items + controls R (8) and V (12)
- the SAME items' SOURCES in their live form (5 options for SSAT) + the same
  controls, under a different seed

Three fresh samples of one Claude solver per file (prompt 3). Unanimity is
read as a rate (CLAUDE.md 2026-09-25: three samples, not three solvers).

| bar | threshold | source of the bar |
|---|---|---|
| **A** guessability, comprehension + writing (n=17) | PASS if mean <= 50% AND unanimous-on-key <= floor(0.3n) = 5; else FAIL. INVALID if control R falls outside 10-45%. | batch 3's A (SUPPORTS = mean <= 50%, unanimous <= 3/10), scaled; BETWEEN counts as not passing |
| **V** vocabulary guessability (n=7) | mean <= control V + 20 points. INVALID if control V < 30% or >= 80%. | batch 3-4's screen flag, now deciding (batch 4's vocabulary was 88.9% vs V 52.8%) |
| **P** paired, adapted vs its SOURCE (n=24 pairs, 72 picks a side) | delta = (adapted hit - adapted chance) - (source hit - source chance) **<= +10 points**. Chance is mean(1/k), so 20% for a five-option source and 25% otherwise. INVALID if the source margin leaves no headroom (source margin + 10 >= 100 - adapted chance) or if the two files' pooled controls (R+V, 20 items) differ by > 25 points. | new; owner's bar |

INVALID re-runs the screen once with fresh samples. INVALID twice stops the
pilot, reported as INVALID, never as a pass.

*Range checks:*
- A: control R measured 25.0/25.0/33.3 in batches 2-4, inside 10-45 with room
  both sides. The candidate can land anywhere 0-100.
- V: control V was 58.3/58.3/52.8, so the bar sits near 73-78 and can fail.
  The >=80 clause keeps it from becoming unreachable.
- P: the adapted margin can reach 75, and the INVALID clause fires exactly
  when +10 over the source would exceed that, so the bar can always fail when
  valid.

**Stage 2: with-source.** Two fresh graders (prompt 4) see the adapted items,
key unmarked. The schema is batches 2-4's. The per-item function is batch 3's
`wsItem`, imported unchanged.

| bar | threshold | source of the bar |
|---|---|---|
| **C** exclusivity | both pick the key AND neither names a second defensible answer, on **>= 20 of 24** | batches 1-4's 20/24 |
| **S1-b** pilot-pass | conditions 1-4 (key, no second answer, grade_fit fits for both, no option dead by both) on **>= 20 of 24** | batches 3-4, unchanged |
| **S1-c** calibration | `easier` **<= 8 of 48** AND `harder` **< 12 of 48** | batches 3-4, unchanged |
| **S1-d** band | each grader's mean (band_assigned - target) within **[-0.5, +0.5]** bands | batches 3-4, unchanged |

*Range checks:* C and S1-b span 0-24. S1-c's counts span 0-48 each, and both
bars sit inside. S1-d can fail from both sides.

**Stage 3: naturalness.** Two fresh judges get prompt 5, which is batch 3's
wording with the count changed to 9 and the grade range to 5-8. They rate 9
shuffled passages: the 3 adapted passages and **batch 2's 6 comprehension
passages**, the same control batch 3 used.

| bar | threshold |
|---|---|
| **E** | pooled candidate median (2 judges x 3) **>=** pooled control median (2 x 6) |

- `map-wv.mjs scoreNat` is imported unchanged.
- INVALID if the control median is 1.
- A judge who omits a rating or reason, or gives all 9 the same rating, is
  discarded and replaced.

**Stage 4 (only if 0-3 all pass): insert STAGED.** Insert as `verified=false`
under the new cohort `map-adapt-p5`. Each row's `verify_meta` carries:
- `source_item_id`, source family and cohort
- the `changes` list
- the stage results
- `cross_test_exposure`: the source ids and, at insert time, the count of
  real (non-internal) students already served each source

MAP is NOT made reachable, verified or shipped; the owner decides release. If
the schema refuses a `map` family without a code change, the insert stops
there and is reported. No shipped-test, topic or blueprint file is touched.

All renders and scorers refuse (exit 2) on missing or short input. Each has
`--selftest`, run before this commit, that breaks every bar at its margin.

## Predictions, stated so they can be wrong

- The most likely failure is **E**. The SSAT "worlds" sources rule options out
  with flat denials. R4 asks the adapter to turn those into narration, and
  that is exactly where agent prose has failed four times.
- **S1-c `easier`** is second: simplifying a passage tends to make the key
  stated near-verbatim (pilot 3).
- **A and P** should pass for SSAT and transitions. The evidence items are the
  likeliest to push P up, because prose findings are the leaky shape.

## What can't be learned here, stated in advance

- Whether band labels are right: graders are not response data (MAP-RESEARCH
  §10).
- Whether students find these hard: MAP has no human sitting. The source
  sittings were at grade-11 reading level and do not transfer to an adapted
  grade-6 text.
- Cross-test exposure is a product risk, not a quality bar. A student who met
  the SSAT passage will recognise its MAP adaptation. That is recorded for the
  owner, not decided here.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-07, after freeze, before any pick was scored): leakage-free split

**What happened.** The first options-only render put all four items of each
passage set into one file per side. One sample had been collected on each
side. The coordinator relayed a source-side solver's report: L03/L41 (P2's
inference and main-idea items) and L04/L42 (P3's purpose and inference items)
carry the same option content, and the solver matched them.

**Diagnosis.**
- Neither listed render defect applies:
  - Sources and adaptations were in separate files.
  - No source is a control: the controls are ISEE, the sources SSAT/SAT.
- It is two items of one passage group in one sheet. That is the
  cross-item leak the standing rule exists for: `attack-split.mjs`, memory
  `agent-rewrite-inverts-tell`, "interleaving items from the same passage in
  one blind file inflates the attack".
- This prereg should have applied that rule and did not.

**Change.** The instrument changes; no bar does.
- Each side is dealt into **K=4 files**, with no file holding two items of
  one passage.
- Standalones are spread by seeded order.
- All 20 controls go into every file.
- Each file gets **its own three fresh samples**: 24 solver agents in all.
- Arms pool across files.
- Controls pool over 12 samples per control item. Control R/V validity and
  bars V and P read the pooled control.
- Unanimity is still per item over its 3 samples.

**Second render fix, found while splitting.** The per-arm flat deal keyed
every singleton arm "A". Candidates are now dealt flat across all candidate
arms per choice width, from a random offset. Bar A's threshold is absolute,
and P's chance line is mean(1/k), so neither depends on the deal.

The two samples taken on the interleaved render are kept as
`*.DEFECTIVE-RENDER.*` and are **not scored**. Their content was not read
before this amendment beyond the coordinator's relay.
