pipeline are v23's (PREREG-SEC23-2026-10-23.md with its Amendments 1-2 and Result), which are v22's. They are
restated here so they cannot be reinterpreted afterwards. What changes is the construction table and five new
bars. Each change answers a v23 finding (sec23.ws-{d,e,f}.json phase 1+2, sec23.gate.json, sec23.cover.json):

- **The second dimension moves off the time clause.** All three v23 graders named "number x tense, settled by a
  when/while/until clause" (d: "tense is always fixed by a nearby when/until/as/whenever clause"; e: "check the
  when-clause strikes half of every grid"; f: "scan for that clause's tense"). v23 had 13 tense/aspect grids in 18
  agreement items. **v24 lets a time expression decide at most 6 of 18.** The other 12 are decided by a pronoun's
  antecedent (5), auxiliary type do/have (4) or a conditional (3). No tense is decided by a modal or a parallel
  verb (Amendment 1).
- **No two agreement items share an option shape and a decider type.** Every v23 near-duplicate pair shared an
  option shape: the three "being" grids (A-06, B-05, C-02), two past passives (A-05, B-02), two past progressives
  after "when" (C-04, C-06), two verb x possessive grids (A-02, C-03), two progressives behind an interrupter
  (A-04, B-04). In v24 no grid appears more than twice, the being grid once, and the five pronoun items use five
  different option shapes.
- **One inversion, not three.** The inversion triple (A-01, B-01, C-01) was named by 3 of 3 graders and the cover
  held two hard items for it. v24 has one inversion slot (C6), a direct question, a family not in the live bank.
- **Boundary option sets are fixed by the table, with at most one at-a-glance option each.** The gate dropped
  C-07 and C-08 for median n_struck 2. Their sets offered a mismatched dash/comma pair, a lone comma, and a list
  with no marks. Graders also named "a semicolon before a FANBOYS never wins". The v24 table states every boundary
  item's four marks, and no option puts a semicolon or colon before a coordinating conjunction.
- **Kept from v23:** nearest-noun balance (9 of 18 agree), the decider inside the blank's sentence, no voice or
  simple-vs-progressive grids, the banned adverbials, and the shared-word rule. That rule now covers every i|j
  grid, so no item can reproduce Amendment 2's identical {was, were, is, are} set.

## Ceiling check: measurable range, stated before the run

have four options too. The +10 bar can fire only if the control is <= 90%. The control history (56.9-72.2%, v23 at

- **Control above 90%:** the blind half is reported NOT MEASURED, not passed.

## Bars (v23's, unchanged)
- **Blind (batch level):** candidate minus matched control <= **+10** points. Above that, the batch is HELD whole.
- **Unanimity** is a batch-level rate against the control's rate only, never a per-item drop on its own.
- **Per-item drop** (sec24-gate.mjs):
  1. key disputed by any grader, or a cold miss (Conventions also needs 3/3 key votes at insert);
  2. not exclusive by majority;
  3. distractors weak by majority;
  4. median n_struck >= 2;
  5. path wrong per any grader;
  6. a majority resolving word within four words after the blank;
  7. a majority `matches_open_mark: true`;
  - R: any recommend_drop;
  - E: panel-median easy.
- **Elimination (A23):** candidate minus matched live control eliminable-rate <= +20 points, control <= 80%,
  n >= 12 per arm, run on all 24 authored items.
- **Difficulty:** the panel MEDIAN, never the author's label. A panel-median easy item is DROPPED.
- **Conservative extensions:** an explicit recommend_drop drops; majority off-blueprint is HELD for Andy; a
  frozen item held under rule 14 is HELD.
- **Template pair, minimum cover with a fixed tie-break (v22's rule):**

    snapshot, both endpoints otherwise kept. A group of k contributes all its pairs. Phase-2-only pairs are
    recorded only.
  - **Hold set:** the vertex cover chosen by (1) fewest panel-hard items, (2) fewest items, (3) the
    lexicographically first list in the fixed order.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-23, before any author started; no item exists)
**Decision (coordinator): take the safer path on the four soft tense deciders.** The first table let a modal
("will", habitual "would") or a parallel finite verb (a timeless clause before a semicolon; a whereas-clause)
decide tense in A2, B1, B3 and C1. None of these makes the wrong tense ungrammatical. v22 lost four items to a
second defensible option, and the gate drops any item a grader marks non-exclusive. All four were replaced before
authoring by deciders under which the wrong form is an error:

| Slot | Was | Now | Why the wrong form is an error |
|---|---|---|---|
| A2 | b\|g, tense by "will" | **pronoun (object)**: c+p {is/are V-en by it/them}, key "are V-en by it"; the blank's clause follows a semicolon whose clause names a singular agent | "by them" has no possible referent (clause 1's only plural noun cannot be the agent); the verb takes the plural head |
| B1 | i\|j, tense by a parallel timeless clause | **auxiliary type (do)**: sentence-final whereas-ellipsis, j\|m + "not", key "do not" | the elided verb copies the main clause's lexical verb ("resists"), so "are not" is ungrammatical |
| B3 | c\|h, tense by a whereas-clause | **conditional**: unless-clause with a present verb, b\|g, key "are V-ing" | a past main verb cannot take a present first-conditional unless-clause |
| C1 | i\|j, tense by habitual "would" | **conditional**: habitual if-clause with a past verb, i\|j + word, key "was + word" | a present main verb cannot take a past habitual if-clause |

Knock-on changes, each made to hold an existing balance, with no balance loosened:
- **A1** reverts to a PRESENT whenever-clause ("are + word"). This holds the past share at 5 of 9 (55.6%) after
  two non-time tense items left the time count, and keeps A1 / C1 (now the two i\|j items) 4 of 4 apart.
- **B4** becomes c\|h with a PLURAL container head ("Samples of the deepest core") and **B6** becomes singular

  matched A3 on number and position (2 of 4). Swapping the two B numbers keeps every Key x Near cell, the
  per-author plural count and nearest-agree 9, and puts A3 / B4 at 3 of 4.
