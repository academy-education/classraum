   inverse tell.** The brief REQUIRED every main-idea set to carry one option broader than the key, one narrower

   student strikes it, plus the one-paragraph detail, by shape alone. Same lesson as MAP Science b3 and MAP Math:

   distractor casts drawn per item from a menu (two misreadings, two details, a broader option) with the key itself
   sometimes the broader-looking summary; no fixed cast; cap any cast at half the main-idea items.
