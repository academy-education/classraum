# sat-math-v21-adv — pre-registration

Written 2026-10-02 before any item exists. Committed before authoring.

## Why this batch
`form-capacity.mjs` / `next-form.mjs` (2026-10-02, after v20): SAT Math is 26
complete forms, bound by Advanced Math 402 at 15 per form; form 27 needs 405,
deficit **3**. Algebra 407, Geometry 252/189, PSDA 296/189 — no other domain is
short, so 3 Advanced Math survivors buy form 27.

A71 (v20) fell short because the panel rated 10 of 17 EASY. The kept five were
the multi-step ones (two-point exponential with a cube root, factor theorem with
two unknowns, rational equation with no solution, |quadratic| meets a line,
equation in sqrt(x) solved as a quadratic). The easy ten were one-step
plug-ins (factored-form vertex, vertex-form evaluate, completing the square,
given-factor coefficient, table transformation).

## Commission
10 items, two halves of 5 by two independent Claude authors (never GPT), max two
subagents at once. Each half: 4 aimed at medium, 1 aimed at hard. All option
sets numeric. Constructs fixed per item here (disjoint across halves, none a
repeat of a v20 easy construct):

    A1 exponential model with a RATE CONVERSION (stated per one unit, asked per another)   key SMALLEST
    A2 function transformation with a PARAMETER fixed by a condition, then evaluated        key interior
    A3 condition on roots via the DISCRIMINANT plus a second condition (count integer k)    key LARGEST
    A4 nonlinear (rational) model in context: solve, then INTERPRET (pick by context)       key interior
    A5 HARD: line meets parabola at two points a given horizontal distance apart -> param   key interior
    B1 revenue model BUILT from a stated price/quantity trade-off; maximum value            key LARGEST
    B2 linear-quadratic system: sum of the y-coordinates of the intersections               key interior
    B3 equivalent rational expression a + b/(x + c) by division; value of a constant        key SMALLEST
    B4 equation quadratic in 2^x (or 3^x): sum of the solutions for x                       key interior
    B5 HARD: zero + minimum value of a monic quadratic -> vertex depth; greatest c          key LARGEST

Key at an extreme on **5 of 10 by construction (3 largest, 2 smallest)**,
assigned before authoring so it is never chosen after a review. Brief carries the
A71 sentence verbatim: **"the far-side distractor must be a strong error a
student cannot strike"** — an undershoot/overshoot that exists only to put the key
at an extreme is the cheap, strikeable one.

Authoring constraints carried from A56-A71 and the skill: `distractor_solve` for
every distractor, rounding included; sandbox recompute; no +-pair with the key,
no key-as-unique-composite, no closure (key = mean/sum/difference/double of two
options), no derivational hub, no singleton parity/sign/integrality/roundness
tell, no expression options, no reviewer-facing text in `explanation`; the
discriminant-equals-zero motor appears at most once (A3 only); stems state no
answer-form constraint that kills options. No option value shared across items.
Each author writes item 1 to disk before drafting item 2.

## Pipeline
author (two halves) -> merge-halves -> sandbox `verify` (keys + distractors +
extremity) -> pre-flights -> INDEPENDENT no-arithmetic with-source auditor (stem +
options) -> at most ONE repair round, only on auditor-flagged items -> pre-flights
again -> GATE: fresh blind options-only solvers (three samples of one solver) +
three fresh with-source graders. **No second repair round.** An item the auditor
finds with >= 2 free strikes or a disputed key is dropped, not repaired. An item
repaired once that the gate faults is dropped.

**A repair replaces one distractor with one on the SAME side of the key**, so a
repair can never move an extreme key to the interior or the reverse (A71: the
repair that fixed a strike pushed the batch under the extremity bar).

## Replacement rounds and an extremity refusal — decided now
- **No replacement or re-authoring rounds, for any reason** (A71 commissioned C
  and D after seeing audit results; that is not allowed here). Attrition is what
  the 10 were sized for.
- If the gated file has >= 10 items and `key-extremity-gate.mjs` refuses it:
  **nothing is inserted**, no distractor is moved, no override is used; the
  batch is reported as held.
- If audit drops leave the gated file under 10 items, the gate prints "no
  measurement"; that is reported as no measurement (with the raw rate), never as
  a pass. The same applies to the kept file. Dropping items can only lower n,
  never raise the rate on purpose, because extremity is fixed per item above.

## Ceiling check
v20's matched control measured 37.3%; non-v2 hard Advanced Math 26.2% (A67). The
+10 bar can fire in either direction while the control sits at or below 90%. A
control above 90% means the blind half is NOT MEASURED. An options-only result is
never reported as "does not leak".

## Bars
- Blind: candidate minus matched control <= +10 points (batch level). Unanimity
  only as a batch rate against the control's.
- Per-item drop, any one (three graders): key disputed by any grader; not
  exclusive by majority; distractors weak by majority; median >= 2 of 3
  distractors free-strikable without arithmetic; explanation path that does not
  produce its printed value.
- EXT-1: an item any grader explicitly recommends dropping is dropped.
- HOLD: majority off the published SAT Advanced Math blueprint -> held, not
  inserted.
- Difficulty: banked label = three-grader PANEL MEDIAN, never the author's, on
  the v18-v20 wording unchanged: judged "on the module-2 hard route"; hard = a
  decision point routine procedure does not bypass; medium = careful multi-step
  procedure reaches the key; easy otherwise. **Panel-median easy is not
  inserted** — and no re-reading of the scale after the result.

## Need and discard
- >= 3 survivors: inserted as `sat-math-v21-adv`; form 27 expected, confirmed
  by `form-capacity.mjs` after insert, not inferred.
- 1-2 survivors: inserted (sound items); reported as NOT buying form 27.
- 0 survivors, or an extremity refusal: nothing inserted; reported.
- Hard yield reported as the panel-median count; not a target.
- A class of free elimination the auditor missed entirely, found at the gate, is
  recorded as a pipeline failure regardless of count.
