# sat-ii-hard-v21 (cohort rw-v21-ii-hard): pre-registration

This file was written before any item was authored. The method reruns RW v17's I&I arm
(PREREG-RW17-II-CS-2026-10-07.md), which inserted 12 of 12 items, 7 of them hard. Below, the bars from that
prereg are restated and the WIC v20 five-stage pipeline is applied. One rule is new, from v17's recorded
finding. RW17 governs anything not stated here.

## State before (2026-10-23, after rw-v20-cs-wic)
- form-capacity "SAT MODULE-2 HARD ROUTE": **9 forms**, capped by **Information and Ideas: 66 hard**, ~7 per form.
- Form 10 needs I&I 70 (**+4**); form 11 needs 77 (**+11**). C&S has 82 hard, so it covers form 10.

## Commission
24 items from four Claude authors, 6 each, on disjoint topic areas:
- A: life sciences
- B: earth, space and physical sciences
- C: social sciences and economics
- D: humanities, history and the arts

Each author writes 3 **textual Command of Evidence** items ("which finding, if true, would most directly
support/weaken ...") and 3 **quantitative Command of Evidence** items (a table, completing the text with data).
Every item aims at hard.

**Rule kept from RW17 (one frame).** Options never differ along the axis the stem names.
- Textual: all four findings share one design and one vocabulary.
- Quantitative: at least three of the four options are accurate readings of the table, in one sentence frame,
  and the key needs a comparison across two or more cells.

**New rule (v17 finding).** v17's textual items put the key and one distractor as the same comparison in
opposite directions. A pattern-reader can halve that set; the item was right 9 of 12 times blind. So a textual
option set must be either:
- a full 2x2: two contrasts × two directions, where the key is one cell and its reverse is another, as in
  II17A-01, which scored 0/3 blind; or
- a set with no reversed pair at all.

A lone reversed pair holding the key is refused.

**Also new.** On quantitative items, the key's rank in any numeric ordering of the options (largest, smallest,
closest to the subject) must vary across an author's three items.

**Proper nouns.** Each author draws names only from its own initial-letter range (A: A-F, B: G-L, C: M-R,
D: S-Z). Merge refuses any name that repeats across authors.

## Pipeline (v20 stages, I&I instruments)
0. **Merge and freeze.** Record the sha. Nothing is repaired after the freeze; there is no revision round.
1. **Shape.** `bank-helper check`, plus `rw17-dupscan.mjs` against all live R&W rows. Dup-scan flags are HELD.
2. **Options-only.** `rw17-oo-render.mjs ii <frozen> --tag ii21-oo` (the RW17 control: 12 hard textual CoE plus
   12 quantitative CoE live), then three solver samples and `score-oo.mjs`.
   - Bar: candidate minus control <= **+10**, else the batch is held whole.
   - The control must land in **(30%, 90%]**. Outside that range the half is reported NOT MEASURED and the
     with-source half decides.
   - (RW17's control measured 63.9%.)
3. **Elimination.** `elimination-paired.mjs --domain "Information and Ideas" --match subskill --ratio 1`.
   Bar: margin <= **+20**, control <= 80%.
4. **With-source.** Graders d, e and f.
   - Phase 1 is cold. Its sha256 is recorded before the key is shown. Phase 2 sends the key.
   - The gate applies rules 1-6 of the RW17 prereg, and these too:
     - a cold miss counts as a key dispute;
     - recommend_drop by any grader drops the item;
     - a panel-median easy item is dropped, never relabelled;
     - a majority `off_blueprint` holds the item;
     - **rule 8:** a majority `key_from_one_sentence` (one sentence or one cell decides it) caps the item at
       medium.
   - Stage fails if any grader cold-misses more than 10% (2 of 24).
5. **Tells.**
   - `verify-answer-key-spread.ts --batch <kept>`.
   - Key strictly longest <= 35% of kept, and strictly shortest <= 35%.
   - No proper noun repeated within the kept set.

Difficulty is always the panel MEDIAN. Medium survivors are inserted and do not move the hard route.

## Amendment 1 (before freeze, after authoring; no item seen by any grader or solver)
Author A found that the 2x2 design cannot be used for a "weaken" item. Under "weaken", the reversed form of the
comparison both accounts predict also weakens the hypothesis, which makes it a second defensible answer. B and
D were told this before they finished. All four authors report that every weaken item is no-reverse and every
2x2 item is a support item. Separately, the merge checker's name-range rule was narrowed: generic place words
such as Pond, Chapel and County are now skipped, and only the distinctive token is checked. Its first run
flagged 30 such false positives and no real violations.
