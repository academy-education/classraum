## atv2-p2 (Neuroscience, "Delcourt sleep study") — worst lecture in the batch

**REFUTABILITY — severe:** the study is a transparent clone of the real targeted-memory-reactivation paradigm (Rudoy 2009 / Rasch cueing work: sound cues played during slow-wave sleep to reactivate specific word-pair memories). A well-read test-taker recognizes it instantly, and then:

- p2/q2 s1 — "To explain how the researchers targeted particular memories for reactivation" — is the real-world-TRUE function of sound cues in that literature. s2 ("a disturbance that fragmented participants' sleep") is the opposite of the real protocol; s3 (sounds used to "confirm which sleep stage") is wrong in the real world (EEG does that, not the cues). The pivot is not four equally plausible settings; it is one famous fact plus three settings a psych undergraduate can rank.
- p2/q1 s4 — "The sound cues improved recall mainly for pairs participants had nearly forgotten" — is itself a recognizable real finding (cueing benefits weakly-learned items). The fictionalized name does not hide the paradigm; the mechanism vocabulary does.

**ORTHOGONALITY — two hard leaks:**

- p2/q1 s4 → p2/q2 s1. If q1's truth is "sound cues improved recall mainly for pairs participants had nearly forgotten," the cues demonstrably targeted specific pairs, which confirms q2 s1 and refutes q2 s2/s3/s4 (a cue that selectively rescues particular pairs is not a sleep-fragmenting disturbance, not a stage-verification tone, and not an arousal control). One key hands the reader the other.
- p2/q3 s3–s4 → p2/q4 s4. "the recordings sampled too little brain activity to show replay" or "It cannot be tested with scalp recordings, so judgment should wait" each make q4 s4 — "Use brain imaging to look for the reactivation directly" — the near-forced next step. Half of q3's slate predicts q4's key.

**ASYMMETRY:** q3 s2 ("The data fit it, but an account based on attention has not been ruled out") is the responsible-scientist hedge in a slate of endorse/doubt/defer; and q4 s4 is the textbook next-step next to three flatter siblings. Both compounded by §4.

**Verdict: NEEDS REWRITE — p2/q2 (whole pivot, especially s1 vs s2/s3), p2/q1 s4, p2/q3 s3, p2/q3 s4, p2/q4 s4.** Minimum fix: break the TMR resemblance (different cue modality or purpose per setting won't do it — the paradigm itself is the tell), decouple q1's slate from cue mechanics, and remove the recordings-limitation ↔ imaging-next echo.

## atv2-p3 (Art History, Verrine Hours)

**REFUTABILITY:** none decisive. All four q1 pigment settings (lapis throughout / azurite base under lapis glaze / azurite margins–lapis figures / indigo imitation) are attested real practices; none is uniquely confirmable for a fictional manuscript. Good quad.

**ORTHOGONALITY — one hard leak:**

- p3/q2 s2 ↔ p3/q4 s4. "Its way of dividing labor makes assigning a page to a single painter misleading" and "Given how the workshop operated, asking which single master painted it is the wrong question" are the same thesis stated twice. If either is the key, the other is inferable at far above chance; and the converse entanglement is also bad — if q2's key is s2, then q4 s1 ("The attribution holds, because the underdrawing throughout is in his hand") becomes hard to co-assert in one coherent lecture, shrinking q4's live slate. This pair must be broken: one of the two settings needs a genuinely different content.

**ASYMMETRY:** p3/q4 s4 is additionally a meta-option — it rejects the question's premise while s1–s3 answer it. The "reframe the question" move is the recognizable sophisticated-seminar answer; next to "holds / faces only / fails," it stands out even before the q2 echo.

**Verdict: NEEDS REWRITE — p3/q2 s2 and p3/q4 s4 (break the echo; only one pivot may carry the division-of-labor thesis), and re-examine p3/q4 s1 for co-assertability once that is done.**

## atv2-p4 (Ecology, atoll pigeons)

**REFUTABILITY:** no known real event; the Guam/atoll-frugivore resemblance is generic, not outcome-identifying. Minor biology note: p4/q2 s1 — "The remaining pigeons were digesting the seeds instead of passing them intact" — a post-cyclone switch from gut-passage to seed destruction is the mechanistically strangest sibling (why would surviving birds change digestion?). Writable (diet-shift → longer retention/grinding) but it is the option a biology-literate solver ranks last. Advisory.

**ORTHOGONALITY — one real problem:**

- p4/q1 s2 vs the entire q2 slate. "Seedlings were appearing in areas the species had not previously occupied" asserts dispersal *expanded*, while all four q2 settings are mechanisms of dispersal *failure* ("digesting the seeds", "stopped crossing the island's open ground", "Rats were removing most of the fallen fruit", "fewer birds fed on it"). If the RNG picks q1 = s2, no q2 setting can be coherently asserted in the same transcript. Either q1 s2 must be rewritten to a failure-compatible surprise, or q2's slate must include at least one mechanism consistent with range expansion.

**ASYMMETRY:** p4/q3 s4 — "To explain where the feeding experiment was actually carried out" — is the lone logistical function in a slate of three argumentative ones (and mildly strains q2, whose experiment concerns the island's pigeons). p4/q4 s1 ("A small trial of it is already underway") is half the length of its siblings. Both advisory.

**Verdict: NEEDS REWRITE — p4/q1 s2 (coherence with q2 slate). Advisory: p4/q2 s1, p4/q3 s4.**

## §4 CROSS-LECTURE PATTERN — one real recipe, must be fixed before freeze

**The verdict pivots share a slot-locked template.** p1/q4, p2/q3, and p3/q4 all run, in the same order: s1 = endorse, s2 = hedged partial ("may account for some… but not by itself" / "The data fit it, but… has not been ruled out" / "He probably painted the faces, while assistants completed the rest"), s3 = reject/debunk, s4 = defer-or-reframe. p5/q4 uses the same four families shuffled; only p6/q4 escapes the template. This is exactly the CR-V7 failure mode from the project's own postmortem: letters can be rotated later, but the *prose family occupying each slot* is the recurring shape, and a solver who has seen two of these pivots can classify all four options of the third by speech act. Random keying protects the key letter, not the item's face validity, and if the pipeline ever biases toward any verdict family (or a grader is asked "which reads keyed"), this is the tell it will find. Fix: shuffle the slot order per lecture AND swap at least one verdict family out per pivot (e.g., let one slate omit the defer option entirely and carry two flavors of endorsement instead).

Secondary pattern, advisory: the "defer judgment / can't be tested yet" family appears in three lectures (p1/q4 s4, p2/q3 s4, p5/q4 s1); acceptable as a family, but do not let it survive in the same slot twice after the shuffle.
