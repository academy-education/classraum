## Brief
24 items from three independent Claude authors (never GPT), 8 each, on disjoint topic areas:
- A: life sciences, medicine, agriculture.
- B: earth, space and physical sciences, engineering.
- C: history, the arts, humanities, social sciences.

Proper nouns are optional; prefer none or a role. Each author takes names only from its own

### Why all 24 are agreement (lesson a)
Only agreement produced hard items at a usable rate: v17 12/14, v16 7/7. Verb form went 0/7
across v16-v17, and punctuation 2/8. The only two hard punctuation shapes are now live
templates, and so are their families:
- v16 B-08, a dash after a fronted list;
- v17 B-09, no mark after a long reduced-relative subject. The "no mark between a long subject
  and its verb" family is also live in v10 A-03 and v11 B-02.

A punctuation slot would therefore be a near-certain live-template pair or a medium. Inversion
shapes from v17 (So/Such, as + do, Not until, fronted adjective/participle) are live or held,
so the one inversion trigger kept here (nor) and the one fronted inversion (-ing participle)
are new.

Known cost, recorded: forms 10-11 will draw SEC hard items that are heavily agreement. The real
digital SAT mixes Boundaries and Form, Structure, and Sense. This is a composition cost, not a
gate.

### Rules for every item (carried from v17 unless marked NEW)
1. **NEW (b): number.** Each item's key number, Near number, misleader position and After noun
   are as in its row. "Attractor on both sides" means both of these:
   - by number: plural attractors for plural keys and singular attractors for singular keys,
     6/6/6/6 as above;
   - by position: the misleader sits adjacent in 11 slots, earlier in 11, and in the subject word
     itself in 2. A wrong-number noun after the blank appears in 9 slots.
2. **NEW (c): aspect and tense.**
   - The deciding cue is a FINITE VERB in another clause or sentence (its tense, or the logical
     order of events). It is quoted in the explanation.
   - Banned anywhere in any v22 passage: temporal "since", "ever since", "to this day", "to the
     present (day)", "until now", "so far", "thus far", "to date", "up to now", "for the past/last
     N", "in recent years/decades", "lately", "recently", "nowadays", "at present", "currently".
   - "now", "still", "already", "yet", "once" may not appear in the blank's own sentence.
   - No tense settled by a printed date or year.
3. **NEW (d): voice.** Each voice item uses its assigned device. Rules from v17 that still hold:
   - no agent or by-phrase within four words after a passive key;
   - no object within four words after an active key.
   No interrupter immediately after the blank except in B7.
4. **NEW: grids and the odd-option tell.**
   - Options are exactly the assigned complete 2x2.
   - No option contains a word the other three lack. So there is no adverb, no "had", "would",
     "will" or other modal, and no odd auxiliary in any option.
   - A word shared by all four options (a determiner or particle, as B5 and C8 must carry) is
     allowed. It is never an adverb.
   - **Length:** key length divided by the shortest option's length is < 1.6. Key uniquely
     longest in at most 6 of 24 and uniquely shortest in at most 6 of 24, at most 2 of each per
     author.
5. **NEW: per-author variety.**
   - Key plural in exactly 4 of the author's 8 items.
   - The key at the letter its row assigns, so A, B, C and D each appear exactly twice per author.
   - No key form used twice by one author.
   - The blank's sentence position varies: not always the last sentence.
6. **NEW: inversions.** In B5 and C8 the subject's head noun is at least five words after the
   blank. In B5 the deciding lexical verb is also at least five words after the blank.
7. Every distractor is grammatical in isolation and wrong only in context.
8. No answer-resolving word within four words after the blank. Free strikes are at most 1 of 3.
   The explanation path must be correct.
9. At most 3 of 24 items delay the decider with a long fronted phrase. A7 and C8 count; one more
   is allowed batch-wide, kept as a spare for a replacement item (no slot is assigned one).
10. No banned trap:
    - literary present, singular they, "one" as a noun;
    - collective nouns, data/media;
    - subjunctive, so no "insist/require that";
    - who/whom or pronoun case;
    - contractions, dictionary variants;
    - "one of the ... that", "none of", "neither of", "the number of", "a series/pair of".
11. **No live hard template reused:**
    - the v15/v16/v17 common-brief lists (PREREG-SEC15 rule 2, PREREG-SEC16 rule 2, PREREG-SEC17
      rule 7);
    - v17's banned constructions: not X but Y; every X and every Y; fronted participial phrase +
      singular head; compound-antecedent relative; So-inversion; as + do; fronted
      adjective-complement inversion; past perfect after a later discovery; comma after a long
      Although clause; ; however,; no mark after a reduced-relative subject; colon before "one
      thing";
    - v17's held frames, which stay out: the only one of the Xs that; it-cleft; embedded
      that-clause after a plural matrix subject; more than one X; Not until + do; fronted -ed
      participle inversion; Such-inversion;
    - "including" and "such as" interrupters.
12. **Near-duplicates.**
    - Each author runs `sec-near-dup.mjs` on its file against ALL live SEC rows (paged,
      count-asserted). It changes any item flagged (passage trigram Jaccard >= 0.20, or an option
      skeleton identical to a live HARD item) BEFORE delivery.
    - A frozen item still flagged is HELD.
13. **Declarations.**
    - Each author writes, per item, the v17 `template` triple in `items.json`.
    - In `<X>/declarations.json` it writes the row fields (frame, misleader word, Near word, After
      word, decider verb quoted, device, grid forms, letter) and its hostile-reader results.
    - **Pre-freeze comparison (NEW):** I diff each declaration against its row and the 24 option
      grids against each other. A deviation from the row, or a cross-author collision, is
      resolved by ONE replacement item from the author whose slot is later in table order. Only
      once, and recorded. A second deviation or collision is recorded, and both items are frozen
      as they stand.

## Decidable counts
`sec22-brief-counts.mjs` is written and committed before any item exists, and break-tested on
v17. On v17 it must reproduce:
- singular keys 10 of 15 agreement items;
- passive key 2 of 5 voice-varying;
- the four "since/to the present" anchors in A-02, A-07, B-03, B-07.

On the frozen file it computes the balances table: key number, Key x Near (Near read from the
declarations and printed for a hand read), voice and perfect shares, "been"/"being" keyed shares,
key forms, letters per author, the length ratio, uniquely longest/shortest, the banned-adverbial
scan of every passage, any option word absent from the other three, and the 2x2 completeness of
every grid.

The bars on the frozen file:
- passive key in >= 1/3 and <= 1/2 of voice-varying items;
- perfect key in >= 1/3 and <= 1/2 of aspect items;
- key plural in 10-14 of 24;
- each Key x Near cell 4-8;
- 0 banned adverbials;
- 0 extra-word options.

Every classified option is printed for a human read. A count missed by the frozen file is
RECORDED, not repaired. It drops nothing on its own. The script refuses (exit 2) on an
unreadable or empty batch, an item whose key is not among its choices, or a declarations file
whose ids do not match the batch.

## Pipeline (v17's, unchanged except the cover rule and the schedule)
1. Three authors.
2. Pre-freeze comparison (rule 13).
3. Merge, stripping `template` and declarations.
4. **Freeze**: sha recorded and committed with `sec22-brief-counts.mjs` and
   `sec22-template-cover.mjs`.
5. Structural pre-flight:
   - bank-helper check, check-sec-preflight.mjs;
   - key spread, length ranks;
   - sec22-brief-counts.mjs;
   - sec-near-dup.mjs re-run on the frozen file;
   - the doubled-word drop;
   - read by hand.
6. Options-only blind attack (3 fresh Claude solver samples) and 3 fresh two-phase with-source
   graders (ws-d/e/f).
7. gate-verdict.mjs, then the template cover.
8. elimination-paired.mjs --batch (A23 bar).
9. mk-rw-qc.mjs, then the ledger.
10. Insert as rw-v22-sec-hard.
11. verify-sat-hard-route.ts reading_writing 11, and form-capacity.mjs.

**No audit-and-repair round, and no repair after freeze.** Anything a stage after the freeze
catches, pre-flight included, is dropped or held, never repaired and re-counted. Every agent is
Claude. **The run stops at the first failed gate** (blind bar, elimination bar): nothing is
inserted from a batch that fails one.

**Schedule.** This needs the owner's per-batch agent approval, under the standing rule. At most
3 of this batch's subagents run at once, in this order:
1. authors A+B+C;
2. solvers a+b+c;
3. graders d+e+f phase 1;
4. phase-1 snapshots: sha256 of each file recorded in `sec22.ws-phase1.sha256`, BEFORE the key
   file is written;
5. phase 2 d+e+f;
6. elimination candidate a+b+c;
7. elimination control a+b+c.

Completion is judged by files on disk. Every subagent saves after every 2 items. A subagent idle
6+ minutes is relaunched from its disk state; a relaunched grader re-reads its own partial file
and the render only.

**Pre-flight drop (decidable, kept):** an item is DROPPED at pre-flight if either holds:
- the word immediately before the blank is also the first word of every option;
- the word immediately after the blank is also the last word of every option.

**With-source render:** `BLIND=bare node bank-helper.mjs blind <frozen batch>`, asserted before
any grader sees it:
- no item's `subskill` string appears in the render;
- none of the words "agreement", "attractor", "postposed", "inversion", "dash pair", "boundar",
  "closing", "opening" appears.

A render that fails the assertion is not used.

**Grader brief:** v17's phase-1 brief verbatim. It flags `doubled_word`, `matches_open_mark`,
`off_blueprint`, `resolving_word_after_blank` and `n_struck`, and asks for `summary.tells` and
`summary.near_duplicates`.

**Two phases.**
- Phase 1 is cold: no key exists for the grader. It is snapshotted (sha) before the key file is
  written.
- Phase 2 adds key, key_ok, cold_miss, path_coherent, recommend_drop and drop_reason.
- Phase-1 fields are verified unchanged after phase 2. A grader that changed a phase-1 field has
  its phase-1 snapshot used.

## Ceiling check: measurable range, stated before the run
The candidate's options-only rate lives in [0%, 100%]. A complete 2x2 grid puts its no-signal
expectation at 25%.

The +10 bar can fire only if candidate - control > 10 is attainable. That needs control <= 90%,
because a candidate cannot exceed 100%. The discard line sits inside that range. The history
(63.9-72.2%) leaves 28-36 points of headroom, so both outcomes are reachable.

- **Control above 90%:** the blind half is reported NOT MEASURED, not passed.
- **Control at or below its own best-fixed-letter line + 5 points:** also NOT MEASURED. Neither
  the +10 bar nor a pass is quoted then; the with-source half decides.

Correlation: the three solvers are one model sampled three times. Margins stand as measured.
Intervals are about sqrt(3) wider than printed and are not quoted as verdicts.

An options-only pass is reported as what it measured ("the option set alone does not decide
it"), never as "the batch does not leak". The with-source graders' free-strike count is the
half that sees stem-plus-option eliminations.

## Bars
- **Blind (batch level):** candidate minus matched control <= **+10** points. Above that, the
  batch is HELD whole.
- **Unanimity** is a batch-level rate against the control's rate only (A53), never a per-item
  drop on its own.
- **Per-item drop** (gate-verdict.mjs rules 1-6; rule 7 is applied from the same files):
  1. key disputed by any grader (Conventions also needs 3/3 key votes at insert); a cold miss is
     a dispute;
  2. not exclusive by majority;
  3. distractors weak by majority;
  4. median of 2+ of 3 distractors free-strikable;
  5. explanation path wrong per any grader;
  6. a grader-majority resolving word within four words after the blank;
  7. a grader majority marks `matches_open_mark: true`.
- **Elimination (A23):** candidate minus matched live control eliminable-rate <= +20 points, with
  control <= 80% and n >= 12 per arm. Otherwise the stage does not pass. It is run on all 24
  authored items, so n >= 12 regardless of drops.
- **Difficulty:** the panel MEDIAN of the three graders, never the author's label. **A
  panel-median easy item is DROPPED, not relabelled.**
- **Conservative extensions (as v9-v17):**
  - any item a grader explicitly recommends dropping is dropped;
  - any item a majority of graders call off the digital SAT SEC blueprint is HELD for Andy, not
    inserted;
  - a frozen item flagged by sec-near-dup.mjs is HELD.
- **Template pair, minimum cover with a fixed tie-break (NEW, replaces v17's
  "lower difficulty, higher id" rule):**
  - **Edges.** A pair is an edge when it is named as one template by 2 or more of the 3 graders
    in `summary.near_duplicates` **as it stands in the phase-1 snapshot**. A pair first named in
    phase 2 is recorded only. A listed group of k items contributes all of its pairs. Pairs are
    unordered. An edge counts only if both items are otherwise KEPT: they survived rules 1-7,
    recommend_drop, off-blueprint, the near-dup hold and the easy drop. An edge to an item
    already not shipping is ignored.
  - **Hold set.** H is a vertex cover of the edge graph: every edge has at least one endpoint in
    H. Among all covers, H is chosen by these keys, in order:
    1. fewest panel-hard items in H;
    2. fewest items in H;
    3. tie-break: list each candidate cover's items in the FIXED ORDER below and take the
       lexicographically first list. In plain words: hold the item that comes earliest in the
       order.
  - **Fixed order:** sha256 of the id string, ascending. It is computed now, so neither author
    position nor id number decides:

        1 A-05   2 B-08   3 B-01   4 C-08   5 A-03   6 B-02   7 C-06   8 C-01
        9 B-07  10 A-01  11 A-06  12 B-03  13 B-05  14 B-04  15 A-08  16 A-07
       17 C-02  18 C-05  19 A-04  20 C-04  21 C-07  22 A-02  23 B-06  24 C-03
    (all prefixed SEC22; e.g. SEC22A-05 = sha256 05725a7d...)

  - **Computation.** The cover is computed per connected component by exhaustive enumeration in
    `sec22-template-cover.mjs`, which is committed with the freeze, before any grader runs. It
    refuses (exit 2) on a component over 20 items rather than approximate, and on a grader file
    with no `near_duplicates` field.
  - **Break-test before use.** Replaying v17's seven majority pairs with v17's panel
    difficulties (B-02 medium, the rest hard) must:
    - hold exactly 6 items, 5 hard + B-02: one per two-item component, and {A-01, B-02} for the
      A-01/B-01/B-03/B-02 component. v17's own rule held 7, 6 of them hard.

    A second break-test uses a synthetic graph: a hard item H paired with two mediums M1 and M2.
    It must hold {M1, M2} with key 1 and {H} without it. That shows key 1 is the one doing the
    work. The replay does not re-count v17. It tests the script.
  - The held items go to `sat-sec-hard-v22.held.batch.json`. A pair named by one grader is
    recorded only.
- **Cross-item tell:** a heuristic named by a majority of graders is recorded against the batch,
  even if no item drops on it. It is checked by name against v17's four tells and the declared
  near-collisions above.

## Discard / reporting
- Fewer than 4 hard survivors: insert what passes. SEC does not reach form 10's 70, and the
  remaining gap is reported.
- Zero hard survivors is reported as a failed hard commission, regardless of insert count.
- 11 or more hard survivors reach form 11's 77 on SEC alone. The route is quoted from
  form-capacity.mjs's "SAT MODULE-2 HARD ROUTE" line, cross-checked by verify-sat-hard-route.ts
  reading_writing 11. Whichever domain binds next is named.
- Medium survivors are inserted and do not move the hard route.
- Whatever the outcome, the result and any new finding are appended to REGISTER.md section 5 in
  the same commit as the insert.

## Amendment 2 (2026-10-23, after authoring and before freeze; no grader or solver has seen any item)
**The duplicate-scan's option-skeleton rule is an artifact on this batch, and an option-only match no longer holds
an item.**

`sec-near-dup.mjs` replaces each option's verb with "w". So every item built on a given 2x2 verb grid gets the same
skeleton, whatever the verb or the passage. Author A showed this directly: swapping A1's verb to "accumulate" left
the flag identical. Every slot in this batch is a verb 2x2 by construction. Author A got 8 of 8 option-skeleton
flags against live items and author B got 4 of 8, while the highest passage trigram Jaccard was 0.026, against the
0.20 bar. The flagged live items were read, and none shares the candidate's frame or topic.

**The hold rule for SEC22's duplicate scan is now:**
- passage trigram Jaccard >= 0.20 with any live R&W row, OR
- an option set IDENTICAL as strings to a live item's (the same words, not the same skeleton).

Skeleton-only matches are recorded in the ledger, not held. Within-batch skeleton pairs go to the graders'
template-pair check (`sec22-template-cover.mjs`), which is the instrument built for that question.
