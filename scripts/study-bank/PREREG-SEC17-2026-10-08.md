## Brief (what is new against v16)
20 items, two independent Claude authors (never GPT), 10 each, construction lists assigned
by me and disjoint (one construction per item, listed in the author prompts and reproduced
below); every distractor grammatical in isolation and wrong only in context; key letters
flat (A/B/C/D 5 each across 20; author A A3 B2 C3 D2, author B A2 B3 C2 D3); explanations
quote options.

2. **Verb form, 2 items.** V1 (author A): a past-perfect KEY decided by the logic of another
   past verb far from the blank (an earlier action reported or discovered later), NOT by a
   printed date and NOT by a "by the time" point (live template). Offers no "would" and no
   "will". V2 (author B): a "will" KEY — future from a present or present-perfect reporting
   frame far from the blank, with past-tense verbs nearby tempting "would"; offers "would"
   and a "had + participle" distractor. No v16 template (counterfactual "Had ... not",
   "would" after "assured", nonfinite key after a complete clause, parallel verbs).
3. **Voice (v16 tell 1: passive keyed in 4 of 5).** An item is VOICE-VARYING when its
   options include both a passive and an active finite form. Commissioned: 5 (N1, N3, N5,
   N6, N7), passive key in 2 (N3, N6) = 40%. **Bar on the frozen file: passive key in at most
   half and at least a third of voice-varying items** (the lower bound so the tell is not
   simply inverted). The voice decision comes from meaning (who acts), never from an object
   within four words after the blank: an active key is intransitive in use or its object is
   delayed five or more words; no agent/by-phrase within four words after a passive key.
   No other item offers a passive option.
4. **Punctuation (v16 tell 2: comma never keyed, strong mark 3 of 4).** 4 items, identical
   words in identical order across options, no added connectors. Keys: P1 (A) COMMA, P2 (A)
   SEMICOLON, P3 (B) NO MARK, P4 (B) COLON or DASH (not v16's colon-before-NP-with-relative
   nor dash-after-fronted-list). **Bar: comma key in at least 1, strong-mark key in exactly
   2 of the 4.** Every punctuation item offers at least one strong mark; a strong-mark key is
   never the only strong-mark option, and the competing strong mark is ruled out by a
   fragment or the list/clause structure, never as a ;/. twin. Never offer both "; X" and
   ". X"; never a colon and a dash both introducing the same explanation; no key decided by
   the partner of a mark already in the sentence. A comma key must not be a closing comma.
5. **Perfect tense (v16 tell 3: a "had + participle" option in 8 of 12 verb items, never
   keyed).** "had + participle" options appear in exactly 3 items (V1 key, V2 distractor, I6
   distractor). **Bar: the had-form is the key in at least a third of the items that offer
   it, and at most half.** No other item offers a "had" option.
6. **Modals (v16 tell 4: "would" beat "will" in both).** Only V2 offers both "would" and
   "will", and "will" is its key. **Bar: of items offering both, "would" is the key in at most
   half, and at least one keys "will".** No other item offers "would" or "will".
7. **No template pairs (v16 A-03/B-03 shared one; v14 and v15 each shipped one).** A template
   is (subject construction x what misleads x option grid). No two v17 items share one, and no
   v17 item reuses a LIVE hard template: the v15/v16 common-brief list plus v16's banked
   constructions — either/or proximity + perfect (A-01), "Only ..." limiting inversion +
   has/have (A-02), relative verb on the nearest plural after a singular head + passive
   perfect (A-03), expletive "there" + delayed both/and subject (A-04), "including"
   interrupter number x voice (B-01), fraction + of + singular mass noun (B-02), plural +
   floating "each" + progressive passive (B-03), dash after a fronted list resumed by "any
   one of these" (B-08), inverted "Had ... not" counterfactual (A-05), nonfinite key after a
   complete clause (B-04), "would" after "assured" (B-05), colon before an NP with a relative
   clause (B-07). No two items share an option grid (same auxiliary/verb-form set). Each
   author declares the template triple per item; before freeze I compare the 20 declarations
   and the two files' option grids. A cross-author collision is resolved by ONE replacement
   item from the author whose slot is lower in the table, once, recorded; a second collision
   is recorded and both items are frozen as they stand (the graders then judge it).
8. **Kept from v16:** no answer-resolving word within four words after the blank; free
   strikes <= 1 of 3; explanation path correct; at most 3 of 20 delay the decider with a long
   fronted phrase (N3 counts; inversion items keep their fronted phrase short except one);
   key not uniquely longest/shortest in more than 5 of 20, length ratio < 1.6; no
   literary-present, singular-they, "one"-as-noun, collective-noun, data/media, subjunctive
   (so no "insist/require that"), who/whom or pronoun-case, contraction, dictionary-variant,
   "one of the ... that", "none of", "neither of", "the number of", or "a series/pair of"
   traps; no tense settled by a printed date or year; topics must not repeat any v10-v16
   topic_tag; no proper noun repeated.
9. **Near-duplicates.** Each author runs `sec-near-dup.mjs` on its file against ALL live SEC

## Pipeline (v16's, unchanged except the schedule)
two authors -> pre-freeze template comparison (rule 7) -> merge -> **freeze** (sha recorded,
committed) -> structural pre-flight (bank-helper check, check-sec-preflight.mjs, key spread,
length ranks, sec17-brief-counts.mjs, sec-near-dup.mjs re-run on the frozen file, the
doubled-word drop, read by hand) -> options-only blind attack (3 fresh Claude solver
samples) + 3 fresh two-phase with-source graders (ws-d/e/f) -> gate-verdict.mjs + rule 7 ->
elimination-paired.mjs --batch (A23 bar) -> mk-rw-qc.mjs -> ledger -> insert as
rw-v17-sec-hard -> verify-sat-hard-route.ts reading_writing (and with 10 forms) +
form-capacity.mjs. **No audit-and-repair round, and no repair after freeze.** Anything a
stage after freeze catches (pre-flight included) is dropped or held, never repaired and
re-counted. Every agent is Claude. **The run stops at the first failed gate** (blind bar,
elimination bar): nothing is inserted from a batch that fails one.

**Schedule (new, operational only).** Many batches run concurrently under a 20-subagent cap,
so at most 2 of this batch's subagents run at once, in this order: authors A+B; solver a +
grader d; solver b + grader e; solver c + grader f; (phase-1 snapshots, then the key file);
phase 2 for d+e; phase 2 for f + elimination candidate a; elimination candidate b+c; control
a+b; control c. Every phase-1 grade is snapshotted before the key file is written, so all
three graders are cold whichever wave they ran in. Completion is judged by files on disk;
every subagent saves after every few items; a subagent idle 6+ minutes is relaunched from its
disk state (a relaunched grader re-reads its own partial file and the render only).

**Pre-flight drop (decidable, kept):** an item where the word immediately before the blank is
also the first word of every option, or the word immediately after the blank is also the last
word of every option, is DROPPED at pre-flight.

**With-source render:** `BLIND=bare node bank-helper.mjs blind <frozen batch>`, asserted
before any grader sees it: no item's `subskill` string, and none of the words "dash pair",
"boundar", "agreement", "closing", "opening" appears in the render. A render that fails the
assertion is not used.

**Grader brief:** v16's phase-1 brief verbatim (it asks graders to flag `doubled_word` and
does not contain the v14 sentence that options "often repeat the word before the blank").

**Two phases.** Phase 1 cold (no key in existence for the grader) is snapshotted (sha) before
the key file is written; phase 2 adds key / key_ok / cold_miss / path_coherent /
recommend_drop / drop_reason. Phase-1 fields are verified unchanged after phase 2; a grader
that changed a phase-1 field has its phase-1 snapshot used.

## Ceiling check
The +10 bar can fire only while the control is at or below 90%. **Control above 90%: the
blind half is reported NOT MEASURED, not passed.** Control at or below its own
best-fixed-letter line + 5 points: likewise NOT MEASURED.

## Bars
- **Blind (batch level):** candidate minus matched control <= **+10** points. Above that, the
  batch is HELD whole.
- **Unanimity** is a batch-level rate against the control's rate only (A53); never a per-item
  drop on its own.
- **Per-item drop** (gate-verdict.mjs rules 1-6, rule 7 applied from the same files):
  1. key disputed by any grader (Conventions also needs 3/3 key votes at insert)
  2. not exclusive by majority
  3. distractors weak by majority
  4. median of 2+ of 3 distractors free-strikable
  5. explanation path wrong per any grader
  6. a grader-majority resolving word within four words after the blank
  7. a grader majority marks `matches_open_mark: true`
- **Elimination (A23):** candidate minus matched live control eliminable-rate <= +20 points,
  control <= 80%, n >= 12 per arm; else the stage does not pass. Run on all 20 authored items
  so n >= 12 regardless of drops.
- **Difficulty:** panel MEDIAN of the three graders, never the author's label.
  **Panel-median easy is DROPPED, not relabelled.**
- **Conservative extensions (as v9-v16):** any item a grader explicitly recommends dropping is
  dropped; any item a majority of graders call off the digital SAT SEC blueprint is HELD for
  Andy, not inserted. A frozen item flagged by sec-near-dup.mjs is HELD.
- **Template pair (new, conservative):** two otherwise-kept items named as one template by a
  majority (2+ of 3) of graders: the one with the lower panel difficulty (tie: the higher id)
  is HELD, not inserted, so no named pair ships. A pair named by one grader is recorded only.
- **Cross-item tell:** a heuristic named by a majority of graders is recorded against the batch
  even if no item drops on it.
