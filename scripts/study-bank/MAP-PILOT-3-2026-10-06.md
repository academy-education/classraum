## Strand 2: whole-passage-variant comprehension

**The options-only leak is gone, as the method guarantees, and everything the
draw cannot protect failed.** The SSAT pilots had always traded one failure
for another: pilot 3 had natural prose and easy items. Here the MAP shape
failed on both sides at once. The graders' and judges' own words name five
mechanisms:

1. **Keys are stated, not inferred (F, C).**
   - P02-3 and P02-5 keys are lifted from the passage almost verbatim ("the
     ferry had become too risky to run in high water"; "a threat to the
     ferryman's living").
   - Making one choice the unique answer in one version, against three
     rivals present in that same version, pushed the author to state it
     outright.
   - The inference items that were actually inferential (P01-2) lost
     exclusivity instead: "the doorway moment alone does not decide".
2. **The vocabulary items are transparent collocations (F):** "set myself a
   goal" and "raised the idea". Five-sense multiple-meaning words did not
   make them hard.
3. **Rivals are flatly false in the drawn version (G).**
   - Every rival option has to concern something every version discusses.
   - In the drawn version a rival is therefore either refuted outright
     ("the cookies were never broken into pieces") or thinly mentioned, and
     graders read refuted ones as dead.
   - This is SSAT pilot 3's "flat dismissal" mechanism, which the brief
     forbade and which the 4-choice, 290-word shape reproduced anyway.
4. **The prose reads "assembled rather than written" (E).**
   - Both judges, independently, gave P01 3 and P02 2.
   - They cited repeated anchor words ("high water", "living", needed so
     every option is present in every version), "members" with no
     antecedent, and abrupt characters.
   - The six unconstrained batch-2 passages rated 4-5.
   - The absent-option rule, together with ~290 words and four worlds, is
     what made the text repetitive.
5. **A cross-item keyword thread (D reviewer, recorded).**
   - Within one version the five keys share that world's keyword ("help"
     across P01 v2, "count" across P02 v2).
   - So a reader who solves the theme or central-idea item can match the
     other answers by that word.
   - This is a with-source version of the "rigid brief, cross-item tell"
     corollary in CLAUDE.md. Varying the answer KIND did not prevent it.

D also recorded 27 of 40 question-versions in which some choice is "never
really discussed". So the lexical absent-option check (verify reported 0
hits) has the false negatives that SSAT pilot 4's prereg predicted for it.

## What this says, and what not to do next

1. **Strand 1 is close and its failure is specific.** 23/24 pass the
   batch-1/2 conditions. The binding defect is dead distractors, and most of
   them come from one rule: "every vocabulary option is a genuine meaning".
   That rule should become "every option is a meaning a reader could
   plausibly read INTO THIS SENTENCE". The 18 pilot-pass items are fit to
   show a human; they are not shippable and carry no validated RIT.
2. **The bottom band (180-189) is where calibration fails now.** Graders put
   two grade-5 items at 170-179, and only 4 of 8 survive. A next brief
   should anchor each band to the grade's own standard (L.5 for 180-189) and
   avoid labelling grade-5 standards grade 6.
3. **Comprehension: stop agent authoring for MAP.**
   - This is the third MAP comprehension method to fail:
     - batch 1: shape leak
     - batch 2: same-kind claims, still 83% options-only
     - batch 3: whole-passage variants, natural on neither side
   - It is also the fourth failure of the variant method overall, after
     SSAT pilots 1-3.
   - The variant method does exactly what it promises (options-only at
     chance), and the with-source half fails every time in one of two
     directions.
   - Per the standing rule (stop after a failed rewrite; do not commission
     another round on a mechanism that cannot be stated), MAP comprehension
     at RIT 180-209 needs a human author, or re-banding of owned material
     where it exists (MAP-RESEARCH §13). It does not need a fourth agent
     pilot.
4. **No human sitting exists for MAP**, and a human sitting decides verbal
   cohorts. Nothing here changes that.
