## Method and bars: v23's (= v20's = v19's), unchanged
The commission is the same: 40 WIC, 32 hard / 8 medium, four authors, 10 items each, 8 hard each. Rules R1-R6 are the
same, and so are the pre-freeze steering probes:
- options-only r1
- ONE revision round
- r2
- the difficulty probe

After the probes come the freeze and the five stages, with the SAME bars as PREREG-WIC23 / WIC20 / WIC19 / WIC18:

- **Stage 1:** bank-helper structural check OK; dupscan against the live bank.
- **Stage 2:** options-only attack, three samples.
  - The control is the WHOLE eligible live word-shaped WIC pool, hard + medium (v19 and v20 rows are in it; v23
    added none).
  - PASS if E (candidate margin minus control margin) <= +10, read against the measured control's attainable range.
    For scale, v23's control came in at 36.3% with margin +11.3, so there is room above it.
  - Unanimity is a batch-level comparison against the control's rate, never a per-item verdict.
- **Stage 3:** options-only elimination, margin <= +20.
- **Stage 4:** three with-source graders.
  - Phase 1 is cold and sha-recorded before the key is sent.
  - Any grader cold-missing more than 4 of 40 fails the whole batch.
  - Per-item rules are as in `wic24-gate.mjs`, which is v23's gate renamed: gate-verdict rules 1-6; key above ceiling
    per any grader; same distractor above ceiling per >= 2; antonym pair per >= 2; recommend_drop per any;
    panel-median easy. HELD on majority off-blueprint or dup-scan. Rule 8 caps majority key-from-one-sentence at
    medium.
- **Stage 5:** tells on the kept set. Key spread ok; key strictly longest and strictly shortest each <= 35%; no
  repeated option words.
- Authors check the batch key-strictly-longest band (15-35%) with `--final` before the r2 probe.

The run stops at the first failed stage, as v23 did.

## What changes, and only this (each tied to v23's result)
1. **Names.** The `wic24-*` scripts are v23's with tags and paths changed: rw-v24-cs-wic, sat-cs-wic-v24, wic24.*,
   sat-cs-wic-v24-work, ids WIC24A-01..WIC24D-10.
2. **Letter ranges: none narrower than 6 letters, rotated.**
   - v24: A a-f (6), B g-l (6), C m-r (6), D s-z (8).
   - v23 was A d-i, B j-q, C r-z, D a-c. v23's D (a-c, 3 letters) was the narrowest range and stayed OVER on both
     probe axes after revision.
   - `wic24-selfcheck.mjs` asserts at start-up that the four ranges are disjoint, cover a-z, and are each >= 6
     letters. Otherwise it refuses (exit 2).
3. **Exclusion.** `wic24-livewords.mjs` re-pulls the live bank and adds every option word of the frozen v23 file
   (sat-cs-wic-v23.batch.json, sha 988535e7). That file's words are not live, because it was held.
   `wic24-selfcheck.mjs` also excludes every v19, v20 AND v23 option word, read directly from the frozen files. It
   refuses if any of the three is missing or is not 40 items.
4. **Author brief: v23's lessons, made into rules.**
   - **How v23 leaked.** The options-only solvers beat the control by picking (a) the rarest/most test-like word,
     (b) one member of an opposite or near-opposite pair, and (c) the odd one out by polarity.
   - **Key not the rarest.** Each author's key is NOT the rarest option in at least 8 of 10 items. This is the same
     count rule v23 already enforced (rarest <= 2), now stated as the lesson.
   - **Key never the odd one out.** In NO item may the key be the odd one out in polarity or register. Each option
     gets `polarity` (+ / - / 0) and `register` (everyday | neutral | formal | technical | literary). The self-check
     requires the key's label on each axis to be shared by at least one distractor. v23 only refused the case where
     three distractors share a polarity the key lacks; this is stricter.
   - **Deletion test.** Every item declares `deletion_test`: the blank's own sentence kept verbatim (containing
     `______`, at most 60% of the passage's words), with every neighbour deleted, must leave >= 2 options defensible,
     the key among them. A one-line `why` is required. This answers the v23 difficulty probe, which rated 20 of 40
     items easy mainly because one sentence or the blank's clause decided the key.
5. **Seeds are new**, so no deal repeats v23's:

       probe r2           20262482
       difficulty probe   20262483

## What these changes cannot do
The self-check verifies the authors' LABELS, not the words. An author who labels honestly but misjudges rarity or
register passes the script and fails the attack. So the r2 probe and stage 2 still decide, and v23 showed that the r2
probe is not a sufficient predictor of stage 2.

The v23 solver notes also name "most common academic sense" (12/19 correct on candidate items) and "most precise word"
(9/19) more often than "odd one out" (5/6). In other words, part of the leak came from the key being the most natural
collocation, not only the rarest word. No rule here targets that directly. If v24 leaks again, look there first.
