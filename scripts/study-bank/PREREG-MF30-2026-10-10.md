## What is new (A92's lessons, each with its mechanism)

1. **PLUGA: Algebra plug-back is decided by a cold probe, not by the author (A92: plug-back drops
   1 → 6, all Algebra; B09 D04 A07 A04 were smallest keys confirmed on the FIRST ascending try).**
   The authors declared five "not substitutable", the checker printed `(read) PLUG`, and my hand
   read passed all four S keys on the authors' argument. So, as a **stage-0 hard rule**:
   - every Algebra item whose key is reached at try 1 or 2 of the natural order (ascending;
     descending on a greatest / maximum / largest / most stem; `mf28-checks` plug order) is
     listed by `mf30-checks` PLUGA, whatever its `substitutable` claim;
   - **`mf30-plugprobe.mjs`** renders every Algebra item (all of them, so the selection is not a
     signal) plus **twelve plants from sat-math-v29-full** for a fresh Claude probe
     (`MF30-PROBE-BRIEF.md`): stem and options only, options ascending, relabelled X01.. in a seeded
     order (20261066 + round), the map held outside the probe's folder. The probe tries options in
     the natural order and records the first one a ROUTINE check confirms;
   - an item is **FLAGGED** when the probe confirms the KEY by a routine check at try 1 or 2: a
     FLAGGED item is a stage-0 return (once); still FLAGGED after its return, it is dropped before
     freeze. A probe confirming a non-key option (**DISAGREE**) is read by hand as a possible key /
     exclusivity defect and is a return;
   - **the probe is valid only if** it flags all 4 positive plants (A92's B09 D04 A07 A04, ascending
     try 1) AND at most 3 of 6 negative plants (mf29 Algebra items with the key at try 1-2 that no
     grader flagged: B02 C03 B11 C10 C01 B06). B08 and A09 (A92 drops whose key is at ascending try
     4) are reported, not scored: this rule cannot reach them, and the graders' r6 after freeze
     remains the measurement for that shape. **An invalid probe decides nothing**: one fresh re-run;
     if that is invalid too, the strict fallback applies - every Algebra item with the key at natural
     try 1-2 is treated as substitutable (returned, then dropped if still at try 1-2).
   - Calibration, recorded before use: PLUGA lists 13 of mf29's 38 frozen Algebra items, 4 of which
     the graders dropped for r6; `mf30-checks` selftest proves B09 D04 A07 A04 reach the probe and a
     try-4 item and a non-Algebra item do not.
   The probe is stage-0 QC before freeze; the post-freeze graders are fresh and independent, so a
   post-freeze r6 count remains the measurement, but stage-0 returns made against a same-kind
   instrument will flatter it, and that is said when it is reported.
2. **INTER: no solved intermediate as a distractor (A92: A01 A03 C02 C05).** No distractor may be a
   value the key follows from through ONE printed rate, purchase or relation. Author brief rule 14;
   every item writes `intermediate_check` (missing = gating), printed as `(read) INTER`, and I read
   every one at stage 0: a yes is a return. **No mechanical detector**, measured before use: an
   "affine function of two printed numbers" net (key = (o ± a)·b, o·a ± b, a − o·b, ...) fires on 30
   of mf29's 70 frozen items and 24 of mf28's 58, and catches 1 of the 4 known (C02). Not built.
3. **KEYPRINT: the key is not a number printed in the stem (A92: A11, key 45 = the minutes in 7:45).**
   `mf30-checks` KEYPRINT, gating: the key's value equals a number in the prompt or in a table's labels,
   cells or caption. Equal in absolute value only: `(read)`. Calibration: gates 1 of mf29's 70 (A11),
   0 of mf28's 58.
4. **STRUCT: a structure that signs every error takes no extreme key (A92: five of the six one-sided /
   single-bound drops; D01 "for every y", P09 the top of an arc).** `mf30-checks` STRUCT, gating on an
   S/L key: the stem contains "for every / all / each / any", vertex, maximum, minimum, highest, lowest,
   peak or "top of". Every S/L item also writes `structure_check` (missing = gating), printed as
   `(read) STRUCT` and hand-read: a structure the regex cannot see (a strictest bound, a monotone "every
   slip lowers it") is a return. Calibration: the regex gates 7 of mf29's 41 frozen extreme items - D01
   D03 P09 (dropped one-sided) and B04 C04 P01 P03 (kept) - and 5 of mf28's 34. So it also refuses
   items the graders passed: an authoring constraint, designed around with the part check, not a claim
   that every hit is a tell.

Also new, because this batch adds two domains: **no figure** in any domain (Geometry describes its
figure in words; 80.6% of maths figures were decorative, FIGURE-BLIND-RESULT.md); PSDA may attach
one `graphic` of type "table" / "twowaytable" (the shape the runner renders), and `mf30-strip.mjs` and
`mf30-kept.mjs` add `graphic` to the allow-list so the grader and the student see it.

## Stage 0 — pre-flight, per author file, BEFORE the one return

`mf30-assemble.mjs <x>` folds the parts; `mf30-preflight.sh <file>` gates exactly as mf29 with
`mf30-checks.mjs` (PAIR, RUN, DIR, DPAIR, DSUM, PLUG, FAR, **KEYPRINT, STRUCT, INTER**) in place of
mf29-checks; the sandbox mutation (one key, one distractor body) is refused on the first file in.

**The plug-back probe** runs once on all Algebra items when A-D are in (round 1), and once more on the
returned Algebra items (round 2), each with the twelve plants and the validity bar above.

By hand, on EVERY author's file: (a) every extreme-key item: could a student read the direction of the
natural errors from the stem and strike all three without solving? (b) every `(read) FAR` line;
(c) every `(read) BOUND` line; (d) every `(read) PLUG` item (non-Algebra; Algebra goes to the probe);
(e) every `(read)` PAIR / DPAIR / DSUM line; **(f) every `(read) INTER` line and every distractor
against the one-printed-relation rule; (g) every `(read) STRUCT` line**. A yes is a return. Plus my
hand keyword search per item (`mf30-kw.mjs`), all recorded in `mf30.stage0.json` / `mf30.dupsearch.json`.

**One return per author**, carrying every stage-0 finding at once (incl. the round-1 probe's FLAGGED
and DISAGREE items); the returned items re-run all of stage 0. An item still failing after its return
is **dropped before freeze**. Batch-level, per domain, on the merged set: `key-extremity-gate` PASS and
the key-position band (commissioned ± 3): **Algebra S 3-9 / I 5-11 / L 3-9; Advanced Math S 2-8 / I
3-9 / L 1-7; Geometry S 1-7 / I 3-9 / L 2-8; PSDA S 2-8 / I 3-9 / L 1-7.** A batch-level failure in a
domain after the returns **stops that domain** (the others proceed).

**Re-dump before freeze** (`mf30-dump.mjs`, prior set widened to `sat-math-v29-full*`, `sat-geo-*`,
`sat-psda-*`): every live maths row added since the authoring dump is read against all items;
`math-mechanism-dup` re-runs over the live table and its NEAR list is read. A duplicate drops the item.

## Stage 1 — options-only attack (blind half), PER DOMAIN

`make-mf30-oo.mjs --domain alg|adv|geo|psda` (= make-mf29-oo.mjs; EXCLUDE adds sat-math-v29-full and
sat-math-v30-full and keeps `v2`; seeds 20261062-65): one live control per candidate from the same
domain in the candidate's authored band, numeric only, keys dealt flat, interleaved. Eligible today:
Algebra hard 30 / medium 139; Advanced Math hard 48 / medium 118; Geometry hard 9 / medium 24; PSDA
hard 39 / medium 33 (Geometry and PSDA are mostly `v2`, which stays excluded; both pools still cover
the 3 hard / 12 medium needed). Three samples of one Claude solver; each sample answers all four
renders in one run. `score-oo.mjs` per domain.

Per domain: **HOLD that domain** if candidate minus control > **+10** points, or the candidate
unanimous-correct rate exceeds the control's by > **15** points. Ceiling: both bars fire only while
the control <= 90%; above that the domain's blind half is NOT MEASURED and that domain stops.
Unanimity alone never drops an item. An options-only result is never reported as "does not leak".

## Stage 2 — with-source graders (decides per item)

Three fresh Claude graders (D, E, F), each over all frozen items in three runs (thirds of the seeded
order, positions 1-22 / 23-44 / 45-65; `mf30-strip.mjs` + `make-grade-render.mjs`), under the folder
protocol (`mf30-grade-stage.mjs`, `mf30-coldcheck.mjs`). Fields as mf29 (MF30-GRADER-BRIEF.md, with
the four domains in `domain_judged`). At most 2 graders at a time.

Drop rules (`mf30-verdict.mjs` = mf29-verdict.mjs over four domains, written before any grader runs):
**1** key disputed by any; **2** not exclusive by majority; **3** distractors weak by majority; **4**
median >= 2 free strikes; **6** plug-back <= 2 routine tries by majority; **11** template of an EARLIER
item (seeded order) by >= 2; **EXT-1** any `drop_recommend`. Confirmed by me from any one grader: r4key,
r5, r7, r7pair (incl. DSUM's difference), r8 one-sided on an extreme key, r9, r10 single bound on an
extreme key, r11 one-grader template / live duplicate. Every decision in `mf30.confirm.json`. **HOLD**
off-blueprint by majority. Drop signals are read from the stage-2 files only.

Difficulty banked = three-grader panel median. **Easy cap per domain at the live mix: easy <= max(1,
round(rate x kept)), rate Algebra 0.021, Advanced Math 0.030, Geometry 0.060, PSDA 0.105**, earliest in
the frozen order first; the rest held in `.held.batch.json`.

## Stage 4 — kept set, gate per domain, insert

`mf30-verdict.mjs --write` writes the per-domain sets; each re-runs stage 0 as a set. **`mf30-kept.mjs`
runs `key-extremity-gate` on EACH domain's kept set separately (>= 40.0%, refusing). A domain that fails
- or has fewer than 10 scorable items, which is no measurement and not a pass - is held whole**: nothing
in it inserted, no item dropped, moved or edited to pass, no override, no re-run under another rule.
Passing domains go to `sat-math-v30-full.kept.batch.json` (stripped) and are inserted once:

    BANK_BAND=mixed BANK_COHORT=sat-math-v30-full \
      node scripts/study-bank/math-bank-helper.mjs insert \
      scripts/study-bank/sat-math-v30-full.kept.batch.json scripts/study-bank/mf30.qc.json

after this batch's ledger entry is written onto `ledger.json` re-read from HEAD (only this entry staged;
nobody else's change discarded; others' number formatting preserved). Then `form-capacity.mjs`,
`next-form.mjs`, `verify-sat-hard-route.ts math` (and `math 12`). REGISTER §5 and this batch's ledger
entry in one commit. Commit locally; no push.

## Stop rule

Stop at the first failed gate, per domain: a batch-level stage-0 bar after the return, a stage-1 hold
or a control above the ceiling, or the stage-4 extremity gate. On a stop nothing in that domain is
inserted and nothing is repaired; the result is recorded with denominators.
