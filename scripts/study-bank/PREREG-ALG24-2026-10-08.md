## What changes from v23 (and what does not)

Same target definition, same graders' question, same blind and with-source
halves, same bars, plug-back and direction bans, **no auditor and no repair
round**. Changes:

1. **Two families only, 10 items each.**

   **Half A — family "whole-interval bound" (v23 A6 shape).** A linear function
   (or a linear expression in x) containing ONE constant k is required to
   satisfy a bound for EVERY x in a stated closed interval. Because it is
   linear, the requirement holds exactly when it holds at both endpoints, and
   the sign of the slope — which depends on k and is NOT known from the stem —
   decides which endpoint meets which bound. The admissible k range must
   straddle the value where the slope is zero, and the asked quantity must need
   BOTH sides of it (a solver who assumes one slope sign gets a distractor).

   **Half B — family "count over a constant that need not be an integer"
   (v23 B7 shape).** The stem calls k only "a constant". Conditions make some
   derived quantity (a solution, a coordinate, an intercept, a function value)
   an integer in a range, and the asked quantity is a count or sum over the
   VALUES OF k. The routine error — treating k as an integer — must give a
   different answer, and that answer is one distractor.

2. **Each item a different setup — no shared templates.** Each author declares
   per item a `setup` triple from the matrix below; no two items in the batch
   may share a full triple, no two items in one family may share both (i) and
   (iii), and no item may reproduce v23 A6 or B7's triple. Different numbers on
   the same triple is a shared template and is not allowed.

       HALF A   (i) where k sits:  slope, fixed y-intercept | slope, pivot point
                    inside the interval | slope, pivot point outside the
                    interval | a function VALUE at a stated point (slope then
                    depends on k) | the slope of a combination (f(x) + mx, or
                    f(x) - g(x) with g fixed)
                (ii) bound: lower only | upper only | two-sided | between two
                    fixed lines
                (iii) asked: width of the k-range | sum of greatest and least k
                    | spread of f(t) for t outside the interval | greatest or
                    least possible f(t) | number of integers f(t) can equal |
                    another derived quantity the author names
                v23 A6 = (pivot inside, two-sided, spread of f(t)) — banned.
       HALF B   (i) what is integer: solution of a one-variable equation |
                    x-coordinate of an intersection of a fixed line and a line
                    with k | y-coordinate of such an intersection | an
                    intercept of a line with k | a function value f(t) where f
                    involves k | the solution of an equation in which k is the
                    x-coefficient
                (ii) extra condition: compared with k itself | a range on a
                    second derived quantity | a quadrant / sign condition | a
                    range only
                (iii) asked: number of values of k | sum of all values of k |
                    greatest or least value of k | another derived quantity the
                    author names
                v23 B7 = (solution of one-variable equation, compared with k,
                number of values of k) — banned.

   A pre-freeze script check enforces the triple rule (`setup` field, stripped
   from the frozen file as v23's template triples were). Graders are also asked
   to name any two items that are one template (rule 7 below).

3. **Banned (owner, after v23):** "system plus a third condition"; any item
   with three unknowns; any item with more than two equations. In this batch:
   at most two variables (x, y), exactly one named constant (k), at most two
   equations in total — a condition that the solution "lies on" another line is
   an equation and counts. Inequality and integer conditions on a derived
   quantity are conditions, not equations.

4. **Grader F's tell (v23), closed off.** No item may contain a step in which a
   constant satisfies k² = n (or any quadratic), so no two-root item exists in
   which a side condition rejects a root offered as an option. Both families
   are linear in k; division by a k-expression is allowed only in half B (i)
   "k is the x-coefficient", and there k is then solved linearly from x.

5. **Blueprint.** Every item is on the SAT Algebra blueprint: linear equations
   in one or two variables, linear functions, systems of two linear equations
   in two variables, linear inequalities in one or two variables. No quadratic
   in x, no absolute value, no divisibility or remainder reasoning beyond
   "is an integer". **Each grader explicitly confirms blueprint per item with a
   one-line reason** (`on_blueprint`, `blueprint_reason`).

6. **Plain characters.** ASCII hyphen-minus `-` only, in every text field. A
   pre-freeze check refuses U+2010-U+2015, U+2212, U+FE63 and U+FF0D anywhere
   in an item. (v23 author A wrote U+2212 27 times; v23's kept file still
   carries them.) `≤` `≥` are allowed.

7. **Key position.** Each half commissions exactly 5 items with the key at an
   extreme (largest or smallest option) and 5 interior, with each extreme
   (largest, smallest) used at least twice per half. On the frozen 20 the
   extreme share must be **40-60% (8-12 of 20)**; on the KEPT file it must also
   be 40-60%. A kept file outside 40-60% is a failed gate: nothing is inserted
   and nothing is moved to fix it.

## Pipeline

author (two halves, separate Claude subagents, one per family) -> `verify`
(sandbox recomputes every key and every `distractor_solve`) -> pre-flights ->
FREEZE (commit + sha) -> gate on the frozen file:

- **blind half**: options-only render, three samples of one Claude solver;
- **with-source half**: three fresh Claude graders. Stage 1 on a COLD file
  (stem + options, no key, no explanation): solve, list free eliminations
  INCLUDING the ascending plug-back count and any direction/sign kill,
  exclusivity, difficulty, blueprint (with reason), and any item that is one
  template with another. Stage 1 is written to disk BEFORE stage 2 opens the
  keyed file (explanations + distractor paths). **Stage completion is judged by
  files on disk**, never by a sub-agent notice.

Claude models only. No GPT model anywhere. No new env var is expected; any one
added is documented in `.env.example` in the same commit.

## Bars (v23's, plus rule 7)

- **Blind:** candidate minus matched control <= +10 points. Unanimity is a
  batch-level RATE against the control's rate, never a per-item drop. Above
  +10, nothing is inserted.
- **Per-item drop**, any one (three graders):
  1. key disputed by any grader (stage-1 cold answer != key after re-check)
  2. not exclusive by majority
  3. distractors weak by majority
  4. median >= 2 of 3 distractors free-strikable without arithmetic (stem
     visible; direction/sign kills count), or the KEY free-strikable by any
     grader
  5. a `distractor_solve` / explanation path that does not produce its value
  6. a majority of graders report that ascending plug-back (descending for a
     "greatest" stem) reaches the key in <= 2 routine substitutions
  7. **NEW: a grader majority names the item as one template with an EARLIER
     item (by id) in this batch** — the later item of the pair is dropped.
- **EXT-1:** any item a grader explicitly recommends dropping is dropped.
- **HOLD:** any item a grader majority calls off the SAT Algebra blueprint is
  held for Andy (`.held.batch.json`), not inserted.
- **Difficulty:** banked label = three-grader PANEL MEDIAN. Question to graders
  (verbatim from v18/v19/v22/v23): "hard" means the item belongs on the
  module-2 hard route because it has a decision point that routine procedure
  (including Desmos) does not bypass; "medium" means careful multi-step
  procedure reaches the key. Easy is rejected, not relabelled.
- **Key extremity on the kept file: 40-60%** (rule 7), else nothing inserted.
