### 1-2. The world probe asks the panel's questions
`wic27-wprobe.mjs`, seed 20262783. Render, snapshot, feedback, the one revision round and `verify` are v26's. The
reader brief (`WPROBE-BRIEF.md`) now asks the GRADER-BRIEF phase-1 fields in the grader's own words:
`exclusive`, `second_defensible`, **`n_struck`**, `distractor_quality`, `key_from_one_sentence`,
`resolving_word_after_blank`, **`difficulty` with the grader's definition verbatim** ("hard = a strong student must
combine two or more sentences and reject near-misses that fit most of the passage"), `above_ceiling`,
`antonym_pair`. It keeps v26's `explicit_kills`, `literal_fit`, `presupposes_absent` and note, and adds `struck`:
the letters of the `n_struck` options, so the feedback can name the words.

- **Naming.** A world is named if it is rated **easy** or has **`n_struck` >= 2**, the gate's two difficulty drops.
  v26's other reasons stay (key missed, one sentence, explicit kills >= 2, second defensible, literal fit,
  presupposes absent), and the other gate drops are mirrored (not exclusive, distractors weak, a resolving word, an
  antonym pair of two options, an option above ceiling). A world rated hard with `n_struck` 3 is named.
- **`score` refuses (exit 2)** if `n_struck` is not an integer 0-3, if `struck` does not list exactly `n_struck`
  distinct letters other than the reader's own pick, or if a world rated easy or with `n_struck` >= 2 has no note.
- **`verify` fails (exit 1)** if a world rated easy **or with `n_struck` >= 2** is byte-identical after the revision
  round, if a key changed, or if an item vanished or appeared. `wic27-select.mjs` refuses to freeze unless it is
  green.
- **Limit.** One reader per world against the gate's median of three, and `verify` proves an edit, not an
  improvement. The probe is aligned with the gate, not a substitute for it. It decides nothing.

### 3. The author brief (`sat-cs-wic-v27-work/AUTHOR-BRIEF.md`)
Rewritten around what the panel rewarded:
- **The central rule** (the panel's own): in every world, each wrong word survives a surface read: it fits the
  blank's sentence AND the passage's topic, and fails only on the passage's logic.
- **The author self-test:** before writing a world, name for each wrong word the specific inference step that kills
  it. If none exists beyond "the passage doesn't say so", replace the word (never add a kill-only clause, which rule
  A forbids).
- **P1-P5**, derived from the hard vs medium vs easy notes: at least two near-misses per world; no sentence
  paraphrases the key; killing facts stated neutrally, one inference step from the word; the setting tempts toward a
  wrong word; noun/verb sets, nouns for what a thing does, no person-role sets.
- v26's rules A-E kept verbatim in substance.
- **Examples:** the four v26 panel-hard served worlds verbatim (A-02, B-04, C-01, D-06) with the graders' notes,
  and three counter-examples (C-04 easy, A-08 easy, A-05 kept medium). All their words are spent and denylisted.

**The self-check's part in this is wording discipline, not measurement.** Every kill must carry `surface_fit` and
`inference` (>= 15 characters each), and an `inference` worded as an absence claim ("never says", "no evidence",
"nothing suggests", "unsupported", ...) is refused. Run over all 480 v26 kill `why`s, the same pattern fires on
**7**, none in a kept world: v26 authors wrote absence-kills as positive-sounding sentences ("the passage describes
a survey, not a remote station"), which no lexical rule can see. An earlier, broader pattern fired MORE often in
the panel-hard worlds (0.75 per world) than in kept-medium ones (0.39). So the rule is inert as a predictor, and it
must never be quoted as evidence that a world is hard. CLAUDE.md applies directly: an author declares the
constraints it has already satisfied. The probe's `n_struck` and the panel decide.

## Selection rule (v26's, with v27 constants)
`wic27-select.mjs`:
1. Refuse unless `wic27-selfcheck.mjs --final` is green, each of the 5 authors has exactly 8 files, and
   `wic27-wprobe.mjs verify` is green.
2. Write `sat-cs-wic-v27.worlds.json` and record its sha256.
3. Selection seed: **20262790 + int(sha[0:8]), mod 2^32.** mulberry32 draws world index floor(4u) per item, in id
   order.
4. **No re-roll.**
5. Letters from a balanced deck, 10 per letter, rng(seed + 7919).
6. Write `sat-cs-wic-v27.batch.json` (served world only) and `sat-cs-wic-v27.selection.json`.
7. **No post-selection edits.** A served world that fails any gate drops its item WHOLE. An item is never re-served
   from another world. Unserved worlds are never banked or recycled, and their words stay excluded.

## Stages and bars (v26's, unchanged)
The run stops at the first failed stage.

- **Stage 1.** bank-helper structural check plus dupscan on `sat-cs-wic-v27.batch.json`.
- **Stage 2.** `wic27-oo-render.mjs cs scripts/study-bank/sat-cs-wic-v27.batch.json --tag wic27`, seed 20262791,
  three samples (`OO-SOLVER-BRIEF.md`) to `wic27-oo.solver-{a,b,c}.json`, scored by `score-oo.mjs`.
  - **PASS if E = M_cand - M_ctl <= +10.**
  - Control: the WHOLE eligible word-shaped live WIC pool. The dry render counted **143** (34 hard, 109 medium),
    which now holds **43 four-world rows** (v25's 21, v26's 22).
  - Candidate line 25.0% (10/10/10/10), so M_cand spans [-25, +75]. Control line 25.2% (36/36/36/35); the control is
    measured only inside **(30.2%, 90%]**.
  - **Attainable range.** v26 read 35.3% on 121 rows with 21 four-world rows near chance. With 43 of 143 now
    four-world, about 35% is expected, about 5 points above the floor. Those rows push the control rate down and E
    up, against a pass, never for one. If the control lands at or below 30.2%, stage 2 is NOT MEASURED, and the batch
    continues only if M_cand <= +10, as in v26. Both sides of the +10 bar are reachable anywhere inside the
    measured range.
  - A pass shows that selection and rendering did not leak. It is not evidence that the items are good.
- **Stage 3.** `elimination-paired.mjs render rw-v27-cs-wic --domain "Craft and Structure" --tag wic27-elim --batch
  sat-cs-wic-v27.batch.json --match subskill --ratio 1`. Margin <= +20, control <= 80%, n >= 12.
- **Stage 4.** `wic27-gate.mjs`. Three fresh graders (`GRADER-BRIEF.md`, v26's unchanged), never an author, probe
  reader or solver. Cold phase sha-recorded (`wic27.ws-phase1.sha256`) before the key is shown. Any grader
  cold-missing more than 4 of 40 fails the batch. Per-item DROP, HELD and rule 8 exactly v26's.
- **Stage 5.** Key spread no FAIL; key strictly longest <= 35% and strictly shortest <= 35%; no repeated option
  words; **attrition E_kept = M_kept - M_ctl <= +10** on the kept candidates, compared on 0.1-point rounding; fewer
  than 8 kept is HELD, not passed.

## What the method still cannot fix
Everything in v25's and v26's sections of the same name still holds: per-world exclusivity and difficulty are the
author's to get right; only the served world is graded; the four-world construction has with-source tells of its
own; the gate is model-only, and the human sitting decides verbal cohorts.

New for v27:
- The central rule and P1-P5 are brief rules. The self-check checks only that the self-test was written down, and
  refuses one lexical form of an absence claim. Whether a wrong word really survives a surface read is the probe
  reader's and the graders' judgement.
- The probe now asks the gate's questions, but one reader per world is one sample. A world the reader scores
  `n_struck` 1 may still come out median 2 at the gate.
- Narrowing to noun/verb sets trades a rule for supply: five ranges over a growing exclusion pool (1,328 words).
