# String-matching does not solve this bank — and the echo trap is real

**2026-09-02.** Four difficulty graders independently said some version
of "the answer is stated almost verbatim" and "a reader who can match a
string gets it". Decidable, so measured over the whole population.

The strategy is executed literally, as a candidate could: score each
option by the longest run of its content words appearing CONTIGUOUSLY in
the passage, pick the highest. No comprehension, no grammar, no reading
of the question.

    section              key longest   distractor longest   score/control   margin
    toefl/reading           26.4%           37.6%           26.4 / 27.2      -0.8
    toefl/listening         25.3%           40.0%           25.3 / 26.4      -1.1
    sat/reading_writing      9.8%           39.8%            9.8 / 33.6     -23.8
    ssat/reading            23.2%           34.1%           23.2 / 24.6      -1.4
    isee/reading            32.5%           30.8%           32.5 / 33.3      -0.9

**Every margin is negative.** String-matching is not merely useless
here, it is actively worse than guessing a fixed letter.

## The reason is the interesting part

In every family, a DISTRACTOR carries the longest verbatim run more
often than the key does — 37.6% against 26.4% in TOEFL reading, 40.0%
against 25.3% in listening, and 39.8% against 9.8% in SAT.

That is the echo trap working as designed: the key paraphrases, and the
distractor quotes something the candidate genuinely saw or heard. It is
what a listening grader named as the one properly listening-specific
mechanism in the bank, and the measurement says it is present
throughout, not just in the fifteen items they could point at.

SAT is the strongest at -23.8, which fits: its items are the most
consistently written to a professional spec.

## What this refutes

The graders' PERCEPTION was that these items are answerable without
comprehension. The difficulty grades support the weaker claim — many are
easy — but the mechanism they proposed is wrong. Easy is not the same as
string-matchable, and this bank is specifically resistant to the latter.

Eleventh structural proxy built here, sixth to return nothing. It is
also the FIRST one today to come back clean, which is worth saying
plainly after a day of finding defects: on this dimension the bank is
sound, and the item writing is doing something right that no one had
checked.

to propose this gets one command instead of an afternoon.
