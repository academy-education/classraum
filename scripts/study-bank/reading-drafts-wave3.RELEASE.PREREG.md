# Reading drafts, wave 3 (SSAT 11-12, ISEE 9-10): release route, pre-registered (2026-10-23)

**Each cohort's section is committed with its staged insert, before any run is drawn.** This uses the same method
and rules as `ssat-reading-drafts-9-10` and `isee-reading-drafts-6-8`. Owner (2026-10-23): "Keep using max agents."

Every batch is 6 passages x 6 items, written by a Claude agent to `SSAT-READING-AGENT-BRIEF.md` (ISEE: 4 choices,
with the draft-5 quality bar). Before authoring, the existing-passages list was refreshed with every staged draft.
Each batch is pre-screened by the co-founder-standard grader, briefs A and B. A flagged distractor is rewritten
and the checks are rerun, or the item is dropped.

Release follows the option-2 rule. Every third batch per family gets a full sitting:
- SSAT: draft 10 full, then 11 and 12 spot, then 13 full.
- ISEE: draft 6 full, 7 and 8 spot, 9 full, 10 spot.

A spot run is one seeded passage (sha256 of the cohort name, mod 6), 6 items.
- All "Only defensible": all 36 are released.
- Any flag: that item is archived and the rest go to a full sitting.
