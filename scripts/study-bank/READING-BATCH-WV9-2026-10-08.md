## What this shows

1. **Removing "detached" moved the confusion; it did not end it.** With five felt directions, the
   rivals are now adjacent felt directions. The pairs that kept recurring:
   - wistful vs admiring (a loss that is also a tribute);
   - amused vs critical (irony that reads as a jab);
   - worried vs wistful (an ending that both looks back and looks ahead).

   The licensing pre-check caught every one of them before any deciding grader saw them. After one
   rewrite, 3 of the 4 failing P01 versions still licensed a neighbouring direction for both judges.
2. **Felt tone without announcing it is hard to make exclusive.** The no-announcement rule held:
   no fixer reached for "comic". The cost is that the inferable cues stay ambiguous.
3. **Across WV5-WV9, the attitude item is the binding constraint in every batch.** Main-idea, detail,
   inference, purpose and vocabulary items have been exclusive throughout, apart from the single
   vocabulary sense pair in WV5.
4. **The grouped screen works as intended.** It refused the one unit with a dominant world (P01 at
   v2, 12/18) and passed the two balanced ones.
