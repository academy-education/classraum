### Item II17A-01  (Information and Ideas)
Passage: In the Varga Mountains, entomologist Petra Halloran has found that a flightless ground beetle, the ridge runner, has darker wing covers at high elevations than at low ones. Halloran hypothesizes that the dark coloring helps the beetles absorb heat in cold surroundings. Her colleague Marcus Ide proposes instead that the pigment shields the beetles from ultraviolet radiation, which grows more intense with altitude. Both accounts fit the elevation pattern. Halloran points out, however, that the range's steep north-facing slopes, which receive little direct sunlight, are markedly colder than the south-facing slopes at the same elevation and are also exposed to far less ultraviolet radiation.
Question: Which finding, if true, would most directly support Halloran's hypothesis?
  (A) Beetles from south-facing slopes had darker wing covers than beetles from north-facing slopes at the same elevation.
  (B) Beetles from low elevations had darker wing covers than beetles from high elevations on slopes facing the same direction.
  (C) Beetles from north-facing slopes had darker wing covers than beetles from south-facing slopes at the same elevation.
  (D) Beetles from high elevations had darker wing covers than beetles from low elevations on slopes facing the same direction.

### Item II17B-01  (Information and Ideas)
Passage: Records from the civil court of the port town of Halvering show that lawsuits over unpaid debts fell by nearly half in the decade after 1680. Historian Ines Calloway attributes the decline to a merchants' arbitration board founded in 1679, which settled disputes privately but heard only cases in which both parties were registered merchants. Other scholars point instead to a slump in Halvering's herring trade during the same decade, arguing that it reduced borrowing throughout the town's economy, among merchants and craftsmen alike, and so left fewer debts to dispute. The nearby inland towns had neither an arbitration board nor a herring trade.

  (C) Debt suits between merchants fell while debt suits between craftsmen held steady.
