### Template self-check
**Criterion (SEC24's, carried):** (a) no two slots share a number construction; (b) no two agreement slots
share an option shape AND a decider type (a bar on the frozen file); (c) no option shape more than
twice; (d) no inversion, at most one "being" grid.

**What the criterion cannot do, stated before the run.** v24 met (a)-(d) and graders still named
mechanism-level templates: every verb x pronoun grid as one, every "tense set by a later clause" as one.
Under the minimum cover, a majority-named group of k items keeps one. So the survivors of this batch are
bounded by the number of template groups graders perceive, not by 32. The table's mechanism families:

| Family | Slots | Declared near-pairs inside it |
|---|---|---|
| Verb-phrase ellipsis (aux copied from another clause) | A4 (have, though), B5 (do, cataphoric although), C6 (be, semicolon, past), D1 (be, but, present) | C6/D1 both be; v24 named its two ellipsis items one template (3/3) |
| Pronoun split, object | A6 (semicolon), B7 (infinitive), C5 (because), C7 (by-agent, but), D6 (so) | C7 shares SEC24A-02's by-agent mechanism (live hard); frame, tense and numbers differ |
| Pronoun split, possessive | A5 (because), B4 (and), C4 (opening PP), D2 (semicolon), D7 (but) | A5/C5 both open with a because-clause; A6/D2 both use a semicolon; C7/D7 both ", but" |
| Tense or aspect by a clause or phrase | A1, B1, C1 (present conditionals); D4, D5 (past conditionals); A2, B3, B6, C2 (time clauses); A3, B2, C3 (time phrases) | the v24 graders' "number x tense decided by a present/past clause" class |
| Aux by a distant participle | A7 | shares SEC24B-06's mechanism (live medium); no "not", no comment clause |
| Boundary | A8, B8, D3 | v24's six boundary items drew no majority pair |
| Possessive noun | C8, D8 | declared pair; the cover decides |

If graders collapse every family to one group, survivors are at most about 10. v24 kept 13 of 22 after
the cover from about six families, so a realistic range is wider; it is not predicted here. **A component
larger than 20 items makes `sec25-template-cover.mjs` refuse (exit 2), as SEC24's did; if that happens
the run stops at the cover and an amendment is written before anything else, never an approximation.**

## Pre-freeze steering probe (NEW)
`sec25-sprobe.mjs`, seed 20262583, brief `sat-sec-v25-work/SPROBE-BRIEF.md`.
- **Render.** Two renders (r0, r1). Each holds ALL 32 items, so each reader sees the whole batch and can
  name cross-item templates as a grader does. Ids P01..P32 in a seeded order per render; options in the
  authored order; no id, slot, author, key or topic_tag in a blind file. A render-time snapshot records
  each item's key letter and the sha256 of its word-token sequence. The render refuses if a snapshot
  exists (one probe round only).
- **Readers.** Two fresh Claude readers, one per render, never an author, solver or grader of this batch.
  They answer the GRADER-BRIEF phase-1 fields **in the graders' own words**: my_answer, exclusive,
  second_defensible, convention, resolving_word_after_blank, n_struck, matches_open_mark, doubled_word,
  distractor_quality, difficulty, off_blueprint, note, plus `struck` (the n_struck letters) and
  `not_hard_because` (required on easy/medium), and `summary.tells` / `summary.near_duplicates`.
  **The difficulty definition, verbatim from the grader brief:** "difficulty: easy | medium | hard, graded
  against the REAL digital SAT module-2 hard route (most convention items there are medium; call it hard
  only if it would sit among the hardest SEC items)".
- **Named MUST-revise** (the gate's drops plus the panel's signature): any reader misses the key; any
  reader marks it not exclusive or names a second defensible option; any reader gives n_struck >= 2,
  easy, weak distractors, a resolving word after the blank, matches_open_mark or off_blueprint; NO
  reader rates it hard; any reader rates distractor_quality below strong; a template pair named by both
  readers names the LATER slot in table order. **Listed only:** a pair named by one reader; a tell that
  names the item.
- **Feedback.** `score` writes `sat-sec-v25-work/<X>/PROBE-FEEDBACK.json`: every named item with both
  readers' fields and notes verbatim, struck option texts, template partners.
- **One revision round.** Authors revise every MUST item, keeping the row (letter, grid, key number,
  decider type), then re-run the counts and the exclusion check.
- **Verify.** `sec25-sprobe.mjs verify` exits 1 if a MUST item's word-token sequence is unchanged (a
  whitespace or punctuation-only edit does not count, except on a boundary item), if a key letter moved,
  or if an item vanished or appeared. **`sec25-freeze.mjs` refuses to freeze unless verify exits 0.**
- **Limits.** Two readers against the gate's median of three; verify proves an edit, not an improvement.
  The probe decides nothing at the gate. The pre-revision probe ratings are kept and reported against the
  panel's results (as WIC27 did), so whether the probe predicted the panel is measured, not assumed.

## Ceiling check: measurable range, stated before the run
Candidate rate in [0%, 100%]; a complete 2x2 puts no-signal at 25%, and boundary and noun-form items have
four options too. The +10 bar can fire only if the control is <= 90%; the control history (56.9-72.2%,
v24 59.7%) leaves 28-43 points of headroom, so both outcomes are reachable.
- **Control above 90%:** the blind half is NOT MEASURED, not passed.
- **Control at or below its own best-fixed-letter line + 5:** also NOT MEASURED; the with-source half
  decides.

The three solvers are one model sampled three times: margins stand as measured, intervals are about
sqrt(3) wider than printed and are not quoted as verdicts. An options-only pass is reported as "the
option set alone does not decide it", never "the batch does not leak".

**Expected direction, stated so it cannot be read after.** Options-only, A8's key is the only unmarked
option, D3's key is the only question mark, and C8/D8's keys are the only plural-possessive and one of
two possessives. A solver can win up to those 4 of 32 by shape (v24 had 3 of 24). If the candidate
exceeds the control by more than +10, the batch is held whole.

## Bars (SEC24's, unchanged)
- **Blind (batch level):** candidate minus matched control <= **+10** points; above that the batch is HELD
  whole.
- **Unanimity** is a batch-level rate against the control's, never a per-item drop on its own.
- **Per-item drop** (`sec25-gate.mjs`): 1 key disputed by any grader, or a cold miss (Conventions also
  needs 3/3 key votes at insert); 2 not exclusive by majority; 3 distractors weak by majority; 4 median
  n_struck >= 2; 5 path wrong per any grader; 6 a majority resolving word within four words after the
  blank; 7 a majority `matches_open_mark: true`; R any recommend_drop; E panel-median easy.
- **Elimination (A23):** candidate minus matched live control eliminable-rate <= +20 points, control
  <= 80%, n >= 12 per arm, run on all 32 authored items.
- **Difficulty:** the panel MEDIAN, never the author's label and never the probe's. A panel-median easy
  item is DROPPED.
- **Conservative extensions:** an explicit recommend_drop drops; majority off-blueprint is HELD for Andy;
  a frozen item still flagged by `sec25-exclusion.mjs` is HELD.
- **Template pair, minimum cover with a fixed tie-break (v22's rule):** edges are pairs named by 2+ of 3
  graders in `summary.near_duplicates` in the phase-1 snapshot, both endpoints otherwise kept; a group of
  k contributes all its pairs; phase-2-only pairs are recorded only. Hold set: the vertex cover with (1)
  fewest panel-hard items, (2) fewest items, (3) the lexicographically first list in the fixed order.
  **Fixed order** (sha256 of the id string, ascending; all prefixed SEC25):

        1 D-03   2 B-05   3 C-02   4 B-01   5 D-07   6 C-07   7 D-02   8 B-07
        9 A-08  10 D-04  11 C-03  12 B-08  13 A-06  14 A-05  15 C-04  16 B-02
       17 C-01  18 A-01  19 D-05  20 C-06  21 A-04  22 B-03  23 A-02  24 A-03
       25 D-06  26 A-07  27 B-06  28 D-08  29 C-08  30 C-05  31 D-01  32 B-04

  Computed per component by exhaustive enumeration in `sec25-template-cover.mjs`; it refuses a component
  over 20 items and a grader file without `near_duplicates`. Held items go to
  `sat-sec-hard-v25.held.batch.json`.
- **Cross-item tell:** a heuristic named by a majority of graders is recorded against the batch, checked
  by name against v24's two named tells (present perfect never wins under a past clause; the key avoids
  the number of the noun next to the blank) and the families above.

## Discard / reporting
- The hard count is reported against SEC's form-12 need (84, +3) and form-13 need (91, +10). **Form 13
  also needs I&I 91 (+7) and C&S 104 (+1); SEC reaching 91 alone moves the route to 12, not 13**, and the
  report says so. The route is quoted from form-capacity.mjs, cross-checked by verify-sat-hard-route.ts.
- Zero hard survivors is a failed hard commission, regardless of insert count. Medium survivors are
  inserted and do not move the hard route.
- **The comparisons v25 is built to make:**
  - panel-hard and exclusivity per family (the table above), against v24's per-arm numbers;
  - the probe's pre-revision difficulty and MUST set against the panel's verdicts, in WIC27's table form
    (probe hard/medium/easy x panel hard/medium/dropped), so the probe's alignment is measured;
  - H1: the share of panel-hard items with all-three-strong distractors, again;
  - the number of template groups the graders named, and the cover's hold count, against the families.
- Boundary and noun-form items are reported separately with the graders' n_struck per item.
- Whatever the outcome, the result and any new finding are appended to REGISTER.md section 5 in the same
  commit as the insert.

## What the method still cannot fix
- The brief's H1-H8 and the declarations are wording discipline. An author declares the pulls it already
  believes; the probe and the panel decide.
- Two probe readers are two samples of one model, as the three solvers are.
- The survivors are capped by the number of template groups the graders perceive (above), whatever the
  item count.
- 5 "been" items keyed 0: perfect keys need since/for/so-far deciders, all banned, so "present perfect
  never wins" (a v24 graders' tell) is still true across those 5.
- The gate is model-only; the human sitting decides verbal cohorts.
