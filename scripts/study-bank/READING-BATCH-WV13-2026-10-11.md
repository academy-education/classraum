## The grouped screen: what stopped WV13

Three fresh samples, each with all three units' six questions and no passage (pilot 4 prompt).

| unit | hits by version (v0/v1/v2/v3/v4) | max / limit | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| P02 | **16**/0/0/0/2 | 16 / 7 | REFUSE |
| P03 | **9**/0/0/2/7 | 9 / 7 | REFUSE |
| P04 | **10**/3/3/2/0 | 10 / 7 | REFUSE |

- **Every unit fell on v0, and in every unit v0 is the warm world** (admiring / with admiration). In P02 all
  three samples picked choice 0 on 5 of 6 questions (main idea, detail, inference, attitude, purpose): "the new
  pool keeps the look of the 1936 pool", "tiles relaid on the floor of the new basin", "with admiration". In
  P04, main idea, attitude and purpose went to v0 unanimously. The samples said so: "three questions point to
  the old pool being reused", "the attitude questions were all admiration".
- **The pattern is new in kind, not in size.** WV11's one refused unit leaned on v1. Here all three authors'
  first-listed world is the restoration/tribute story, and the solver's prior for "the author approves" lines
  up with it across questions. With all four author-form units using the same direction order (warm first),
  the warm world is also the only one a reader can predict from the stem plus the attitude choices.
- The vocabulary picks went AWAY from v0 (P02 2 of 3 on v4, P03 2 of 3 on v3, P04 3 of 3 on v1), so the
  vocabulary items did not drive the refusals.
- By the prereg there is no repair after the screen.

## For the owner's decision

- The vocabulary fix holds: persistent judges and re-judging only changed glosses removed the noise refusals
  without passing a real overlap. Remaining vocabulary cost is genuine: a fixer replacing a gloss in one round
  tends to reach for a sense that is derived from or close to another (rating system / size; product town /
  capital city). A list of five pre-vetted senses per headword, judged before authoring, would move that cost
  out of the fix round.
- **The new binding constraint is the grouped screen, on the warm world.** All three surviving units fell on v0,
  the restoration/tribute world each author designed first. A next brief could require the warm world NOT to be
  v0 (randomise which direction each version index carries) and require the warm world's main idea, inference
  and purpose to be no more mutually supporting than any other world's; but that is the PD-v2 "make worlds
  equally coherent" instruction again, which has not held when stated in a brief. Any next step needs its own
  prereg.
