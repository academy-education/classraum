**Upper Mechanics bands are still too high.** Both 230-239 items were
assigned 210-229, and MECH-11 (220-229) was assigned 200-219.
`band_assigned` against the target averages -0.17 bands (A) and -0.21 (B),

**The graders' "medoid" explanation does not survive measurement.**

- **Claim:** both graders, unprompted, said the comprehension keys sit in a
  shared-fragment construction, overlapping a clause with one distractor and
  the other half with another. On that reading, picking the option with the
  most overlap wins.
- **Test:** `map-pilot-2-posthoc.mjs` (post-hoc, self-tested, not gating)
  sums each option's word-overlap with the other three.
- **Result:** the key is the unique maximum on **1 of 6**. The live ISEE
  control is 1 of 8, and batch 1 was 0 of 5.
- **Why the claim fails:** in a prose 2x2 every option shares a half with
  two others, so the pairing is symmetric and halves nothing on its own.
- **What the leak is:** plausibility, meaning world knowledge, a
  self-contradicting distractor, or the literary-sounding key. That is what
  the solvers reported.
- **Consequence for the secondary bar:** the "free elimination by both
  graders 2/6, improved" result is therefore not evidence the leak shrank.
  The blind half says 5 of 6 still leak.

## What this says, and what not to do next

1. **The 2x2 construction works for Mechanics and should be kept.** It
   removed an exact, whole-population tell, and no grader found a
   replacement tell in it. Grader A noted that MECH-01's key is the
   all-capitalized cell. MECH-02's key is the all-lowercase cell and the
   rest are mixed, so no capitalize-everything rule wins.
2. **The comprehension brief did not work, and that is the second failed
   attempt.** The rule "all four options make the same kind of claim" was
   given and largely followed, and five of six items still leaked. Per the
   standing rule after a failed rewrite (stop, do not commission round three
   on a diagnosis that cannot be stated), **no third comprehension batch is
   recommended on this evidence.**
   - RLU-01 is one clean item with a single fixed frame, which suggests a
     narrower rule.
   - n=1 is a hypothesis, not a finding.
   - SSAT Reading (REGISTER 2026-09-21) shows a fixed frame can also make
     things worse.
3. **Band calibration needs data, not another brief.** Two batches of
   graders have both pushed the upper Mechanics bands down. Without response
   data no label is validated (MAP-RESEARCH §10).
4. MAP still has no human sitting, and none of this changes that a human
   sitting decides verbal cohorts.
