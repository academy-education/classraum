## The cause A88 found, and the authoring fix (the point of this batch)

In linear Algebra the natural wrong paths (drop a term, widen a bound, ignore a
constraint, report an intermediate) move the answer in a direction the stem
announces, so an extreme key sat on a one-sided family and graders struck it.
**The fix is in authoring, not in the extreme count:**

1. **Every distractor's error is declared with its direction relative to the
   key and whether the stem predicts that direction** (`distractor_meta:
   {error_kind, direction: above|below, stem_predicts_direction: false, why}`).
   `error_kind` is one of `sign_slip`, `inverted_rate`, `wrong_variable`,
   `wrong_operation`, `wrong_base`, `omission`, `partial_answer`, `other`.
   Each item needs >= 2 distinct kinds, at least one of the first five, and at
   most one `omission`/`partial_answer`.
2. **An extreme key needs errors that land on BOTH sides.** The item carries
   `counter_error {error_kind, solve, direction}`: a natural error whose JS
   `solve` lands strictly on the OTHER side of the key and is not an option. It
   proves the error family is two-sided, so "strike the side the stem points to"
   does not work.
3. **Extreme-key slots may not use an ask whose natural errors move one
   stem-readable way:** greatest / least / maximum / minimum / largest /
   smallest, "sum of all", "possible values" (A88's 15 one-sided drops). Those
   asks may sit on interior slots.
4. **A89's drop class is banned at authoring:** no option pair, one member the
   key, joined by a number printed in the stem (key = option ± printed, key =
   option × or ÷ printed). Also no three options in arithmetic or geometric
   progression through the key (A88 lost A01 and C03 to this at the merge).

Items 1-4 are checked mechanically by `algf27-checks.mjs` (DIR D1-D4, PAIR, RUN;
selftest 13/13: catches adv25's D10, D14, C01; B03's ratio-of-two-printed-numbers
on the reading list; A88-style run; six declaration mutations). Calibration
recorded before use: on A88's frozen 48 the gating PAIR line fires on 21 items
and on adv25's frozen 59 on 22, so this is a real constraint the authors must
design around (they run the checker themselves), not a formality. A printed 1,
twice a printed number, and a ratio of two printed numbers are a hand reading
list.

**The author's declaration is never the verdict.** Rule 8 at stage 2 is decided
from the graders' `error_directions`, as in A88.

## Stage 0 — pre-flight, per author file, BEFORE the one return

`algf27-preflight.sh <file>` gates: `math-bank-helper verify` (N/N keys, 3N/3N

`check-explanation-hygiene`, `check-interior-niceness` (any parity / KEY-CLEANER
/ complement / denominators line is a return), `stem-duplicates`,
`check-math-near-dup`, `check-duplicate-option-sets`, and **`merge-halves`
CLOSURE on the single file** (A88 found A01 and C03 only at the merge, after
returns were spent). Reported and read: both hub checks, `key-rank-position`,
`check-key-magnitude`, `check-key-arith-class`, `math-mechanism-dup`.

By hand, on EVERY author's file (A88 process lesson 1: it was done for one
author only): **for every extreme-key item, could a student read the direction
of the natural errors from the stem and strike every distractor without
solving?** I read each stem against its options and the counter_error, record
the reading per item, and a yes is a return. Plus the hand keyword search per
item (`algf27-kw.mjs` over the live dump and the prior Algebra file), recorded
in `algf27.dupsearch.json`.

**One return per author**, carrying every stage-0 finding at once; the returned
file re-runs all of stage 0. An item still failing after its return is
**dropped before freeze**. Batch-level, on the merged set: `key-extremity-gate`
PASS and key-position band **smallest 13-19, largest 13-19, interior 17-24**
(the commissioned 16/20/16 ± 3, scaled to drops). A batch-level failure after
the returns **stops the batch**.

**Re-dump immediately before freeze** (`algf27-dump.mjs`): every live maths row
added since the authoring dump is read against all items by hand, and
`math-mechanism-dup` re-runs over the live table. A duplicate found there drops
the item (returns are spent by then).

## Stage 1 — options-only attack (blind half)

`make-algf27-oo.mjs` (= `make-algf26-oo.mjs`, EXCLUDE += this cohort, seed
20261029): one live Algebra control per candidate in the candidate's authored
band, numeric only, keys dealt flat, interleaved. Three samples of one Claude
solver. `score-oo.mjs`.

- **HOLD** if candidate minus control > **+10** points; **HOLD** if candidate
  unanimous-correct rate exceeds the control's by > **15** points.
- Ceiling: both bars can fire only while the control <= 90%; above that the
  blind half is NOT MEASURED and the batch stops.
- Unanimity alone never drops an item. An options-only result is never
  reported as "does not leak".

## Stage 2 — with-source graders (decides per item)

Three fresh Claude graders (D, E, F), each over all frozen items in two runs
(halves of the seeded order). **Stage 1 cold** (`make-grade-render.mjs` of the
stripped file: stem + options re-dealt, no key/explanation/paths) written to disk
before **stage 2** opens the keyed file; `algf27-coldcheck.mjs` confirms every
cold row unchanged and older. Fields as adv25 plus **`option_pair_tell`** (A89).

Drop rules (`algf27-verdict.mjs`, written before any grader runs): **1** key
disputed by any; **2** not exclusive by majority; **3** distractors weak by
majority; **4** median >= 2 free strikes; **6** plug-back <= 2 routine tries by
majority; **11** template of an EARLIER item (seeded order) by >= 2; **EXT-1**
any `drop_recommend`. Confirmed by me from any one grader: key free-strikable
(4), incoherent path (5), arithmetic-class tell (7), **option pair joined by a
printed number with the key (7pair)**, one-sided family on an extreme key (8,
from the graders' `error_directions`), interior tell (9), single bound killing
all three on an extreme key (10), a one-grader template or live duplicate (11).
Every decision in `algf27.confirm.json`. **HOLD** off-blueprint by majority.
Drop signals are read from the stage-2 files only; a stage-1 field counts only
through these rules.

Difficulty banked = three-grader panel median (adv25's wording). **Easy cap =
live Algebra mix: inserted easy <= max(1, round(0.020 x kept))** (1 for any kept
up to 74); panel-easy past the cap held in `.held.batch.json`, earliest in the
frozen order first.
