## What this shows

- **The checker and the brief disagree on "plain forms".** The stem rule misses -ing/-ed forms whose
  stem changes at letter 5 (trade/trading, make/making). This kill quote is author support data, not
  student-facing text.
  - The checker is NOT loosened here: doing that after seeing the data would re-decide a
    pre-registered outcome.
  - Recorded instead. Future fixer prompts say "quote a span containing one of the listed words
    exactly".
  - Any change to the rule goes in a new prereg, break-tested both ways.
- **The observer-narrator rule does not by itself make the attitude item work.** In both units, the
  "detached" version still read as carrying an attitude: critical, or admiring. Understated felt
  versions read as detached.
  - Removing the participant's stake did not remove the problem. A reader infers an attitude from
    selection and juxtaposition even when no evaluative word appears.
  - The licensing pre-check caught all of this before any deciding grader saw it.
  - The evidence that indifferent-class attitude keys are fragile is now three units in a row: WV6
    P01, WV7 P01 and WV7 P02.

## Recommendation

1. **Next brief:** keep the attitude item, but drop the indifferent direction. This needs a fifth
   felt class in the lexicon; the candidate is awed/reverent split from warm. That is a lexicon
   change, so it needs a new prereg and a break test.
2. Alternatively, a person writes the attitude items.
