# MAP Growth pilot, 2026-10-02: pre-registration

Written and committed **before any item exists**. The bars below can't be
moved after a number is in. Results go in `MAP-PILOT-2026-10-02.md`.

## What is being built

24 four-option MC items, all original, all in batch files only:

- `map-pilot-rlu.batch.json`: 12 grades 5-7 Reading + Language Usage, targeted
  at RIT 180-209 (the empty bottom bands, MAP-RESEARCH §13). Author A.
- `map-pilot-mech.batch.json`: 12 Language Usage Mechanics: 6 Capitalization,
  6 Spelling, spread across target bands 180-239. Author B.

**Source policy (MAP-RESEARCH §8, §9).** The authors write only from NWEA's
public strand names (CCSS_2024 instructional areas, quoted in MAP-RESEARCH
§12) and general CCSS ELA grade 5-7 standards. No NWEA item, live or
reconstructed, is opened or used for style or difficulty. That includes the
Herald PDFs. "Target band" means "targeted at RIT 190-199". It is never a
measured RIT.

**Disposition, fixed now and independent of the results: NOT INSERTED.**
MAP is not in `SHIPPED_TEST_SLUGS` and has no inserter, ledger family or
blueprint. A staged row (`verified=false`) would still be a `study_item_bank`
row under a family no assembler path expects. The only option that can't reach
a student is to have no row at all, so the pilot stays in batch files.

## Exact checks (script: `map-pilot-checks.mjs`)

Every failure below is repaired once before the attack or the item is dropped.
Once the attack has run there are no repairs, only drops.

- **E1 readability.** Flesch-Kincaid grade on every passage, and on every stem
  or option sentence of 15 words or more, must fall in **[4.0, 9.0)**. A unit
  under 15 words is reported as `unscorable` along with its count. It doesn't
  pass and it doesn't fail, because FK is meaningless on one short sentence
  and a check that can't read its input must not return a number.
  Coleman-Liau is printed next to FK as a cross-check on the syllable
  counter. It is reported but doesn't gate.
  **Break-test first:** the script must score a live SAT R&W passage at
  FK >= 10 and a deliberately simple text at FK < 4. If it can't separate
  them, it is invalid and no E1 result is reported.
- **E2 key position.** The attack render must deal keys flat; the realised
  best-fixed-letter rate is printed. Authored-slot spread is reported only,
  because every render reshuffles.
- **E3 key length.** The key may be uniquely longest in at most 25% of items
  and uniquely shortest in at most 25%. This applies over all 24 and within
  each 12-item file.
- **E4 spelling.** Two checks: NSSpellChecker (macOS) and
  `/usr/share/dict/words` (web2).
  - Polarity `correct`: exactly one option passes NSSpellChecker, and that
    one is the key. Each distractor must fail NSSpellChecker and be absent
    from web2.
  - Polarity `incorrect`: the reverse.
  - Plausibility, so no non-word a child would never write: each misspelling
    is Damerau-Levenshtein distance 1-2 from the correct form, keeps its first
    letter, and is within ±2 letters of its length.
  - A key that is missing from web2 but passes NSSpellChecker (inflected
    forms) is flagged and does not fail.
  - Break-test: a fabricated item with two correct spellings, and one with a
    non-word distractor, must both FAIL.
- **E5 capitalization.** All four options are identical once lowercased. If
  they aren't, the item tests something besides capitals. Each of the six items
  names a `cap_rule`, and no two share one. This is the load-bearing element
  and it must vary (CLAUDE.md, cross-item tell). Break-test: an item whose
  options differ in one word must FAIL.
- **E6 width.** Every item has exactly 4 options.

## Options-only blind attack (a SCREEN, never a verdict)

**Render.** One file holds 24 candidates plus 12 live control items, all four
choices, interleaved. Only the options are shown; the stem and passage are
withheld. Keys are dealt flat by `make-oo-render.mjs`.

**Control.** 12 live, verified, unarchived ISEE verbal items rated **easy**:
6 synonym and 6 sentence completion, drawn by seed. There is no live MAP
control, so this is a sanity control for the instrument, not a matched one.

**Solvers.** Three samples of one Claude solver (CLAUDE.md: "three samples",
not three solvers). Each sees only the blind file.

**Reading the result:**

- Each arm (R/LU n=12, Mechanics n=12, control n=12) is scored against the
  best-fixed-letter rate realised in that arm's own deal, not against a
  literal 25%.
- **R/LU arm.** Flag it if it exceeds the control by more than 25 points
  **and** the control's mean is at most 65%, which leaves headroom.
  - **Ceiling clause:** if the control comes in above 75%, the comparison is
    void and no R/LU verdict is drawn from it. A 25-point bar can't fire
    against a control with less than 25 points of room.
  - **Floor clause:** if the control is at chance, it shows only that the
    instrument runs. That is still reported.
- **Mechanics arm.** Here the option set is the task. Four spellings, or four
  versions of one sentence that differ only in capitals, contain the whole
  item. Options-only is therefore expected at the ceiling and measures the
  construct, not a leak. It is reported and decides nothing.
- **Unanimity** is reported as a per-arm rate against the control's rate. It
  never drops an item on its own (CLAUDE.md 2026-09-25).
- With n=12 per arm and correlated samples, any gap under about 25 points is
  noise. It is reported as noise.

## With-source graders (fresh, two, all 24 items each, key unmarked)

Each grader returns the following for every item:

- its pick
- `second_defensible` (letter or null)
- `grade_fit` for the stated grade target, covering vocabulary and syntax:
  `fits`, `too_hard`, or `too_easy`, with a note
- `band` for the stated target band: `plausible`, `easier`, or `harder`
- `free_elimination`: any option killable with no reading or work

**Per item.** An item reaches the **pilot-pass** list only if:

1. both graders pick the key;
2. neither names a second defensible answer;
3. neither rates `grade_fit` as off for its stated grade.

**Batch.**

- **The method works** if at least 20 of 24 items reach pilot-pass and every
  exact check passes.
- **The band targeting is unsupported** if both graders call 50% or more of
  items `easier` or `harder` than their target.

Either way, pilot-pass means "fit to show a human". It does not mean
"shippable", and no RIT is attached to it.

## What can't be learned here, stated in advance

- Whether any band label is right. Band labels can't be validated without
  response data (MAP-RESEARCH §10).
- Whether students find these items hard. A human sitting decides verbal
  cohorts, and none is part of this pilot.
