## Brief (what is new against v15)
16 items, two independent Claude authors (never GPT), 8 each, construction lists assigned
by me and disjoint (one construction per item, listed in the author prompts); every
distractor grammatical in isolation and wrong only in context; key letters flat
(A/B/C/D 4 each across 16, 2 each per author); explanations quote options.

1. **12 of 16 Form/Structure/Sense, each on a DIFFERENT construction.** 7 subject-verb
   agreement, 5 verb form (mood/conditional sequence, parallel verb forms, a NONFINITE
   key, future-in-past, voice with aspect/modal). **At most 4 punctuation items.**
2. **No shared templates (v14 and v15 each shipped a pair).** A template is (subject
   construction x what misleads x option grid). No two v16 items share one, and no v16 item
   reuses a LIVE hard template: v15's common-brief list plus v15's banked constructions —
   plural subject with early singular attractors and a plural noun before the verb (v15
   B-01), comma before a sentence-final absolute phrase (v15 B-06), infinitive-phrase
   subject (v15 A-04), singular head + appositive/reduced relative ending in plurals with
   finite/nonfinite options (v15 B-04), past perfect before a "by the time" point (v15
   A-02), passive simple past (v15 B-02), indirect-question end punctuation (v15 B-05),
   restrictive participial phrase no mark (v15 B-07), "one of the ... that" (v15 A-01,
   contested). **NEW, pre-freeze (allowed because nothing is frozen yet):** each author
   declares the template triple per item; before freeze I compare the 16 declarations and
   the two files' option grids. A cross-author collision is resolved by ONE replacement
   item from author B, once, recorded; a second collision is recorded and both items are
   frozen as they stand (the graders then judge it).
3. **Agreement: the key must NOT always be the verb that disagrees with the nearest
   noun.** Of the 7 agreement items, **at least 3** have the noun immediately before the
   blank AGREE with the key (proximity rule in either/or; relative-clause verb whose
   antecedent is the nearest noun; partitive "of"-noun; plural subject + floating "each"),
   **at least 2** are decided by an inverted or postposed subject (negative/limiting
   adverbial inversion; expletive "there" with a delayed compound subject), and at most 2
   are "opposite the nearest noun". In inverted items the subject's head noun sits at least
   five words after the blank.
4. **Tense: no tense or aspect choice is settled by a printed date or year.** All of
   v15's date-decided items came back medium or easy. Tense/mood items are decided by the
   logic of another verb (counterfactual conditional, future-in-past after a past
   reporting verb, parallelism with a distant first verb).
5. **Punctuation (<= 4 items): a semicolon, colon or dash is the KEY in at least 2.**
   "Semicolon is never the key" was a tell in v14 and v15. **Never offer both "; X" and
   ". X"** in one item (interchangeable between independent clauses). A strong-mark key is
   never the only strong-mark option; the competing strong mark is ruled out by a
   fragment or by a list structure, not by a ;/. twin. Options in punctuation items carry
   identical words in identical order (no added-connector options at all). No key decided
   by the partner of a mark already in the sentence. No colon and dash both offered where
   both introduce the same explanation.
6. **Kept from v15:** no answer-resolving word within four words after the blank; free
   strikes <= 1 of 3; explanation path correct; at most 2 of 16 delay the decider with a
   long fronted phrase; key not uniquely longest/shortest in more than 4 of 16, length
   ratio < 1.6; no literary-present, singular-they, "one"-as-noun, collective-noun,
   data/media, subjunctive, who/whom or pronoun-case, contraction, or dictionary-variant
   traps; topics must not repeat any v10-v15 topic_tag; no proper noun repeated.
7. **Near-duplicates.** Each author runs `sec-near-dup.mjs` on its file against ALL live
   SEC rows (paged, count-asserted) and changes any item flagged (passage trigram Jaccard
   >= 0.20 to a live row, or an option skeleton identical to a live HARD item) BEFORE
   delivery.

## Pipeline (v15's, unchanged)
two authors -> pre-freeze template comparison (rule 2) -> merge -> **freeze** (sha
recorded, committed) -> structural pre-flight (bank-helper check, check-sec-preflight.mjs,
key spread, length ranks, the brief's decidable counts 1-6 computed from the frozen file,
sec-near-dup.mjs re-run on the frozen file, read by hand) -> options-only blind attack (3
fresh Claude solver samples) + 3 fresh two-phase with-source graders (ws-d/e/f) ->
gate-verdict.mjs + rule 7 -> elimination-paired.mjs --batch (A23 bar) -> mk-rw-qc.mjs ->
ledger -> insert as rw-v16-sec-hard -> verify-sat-hard-route.ts reading_writing +
form-capacity.mjs. **No audit-and-repair round, and no repair after freeze.** Anything a
stage after freeze catches (pre-flight included) is dropped or held, never repaired and
re-counted. Every agent is Claude. A brief count (1-6) missed by the frozen file is
RECORDED, not repaired; it drops nothing on its own.

**Pre-flight drop (decidable, kept from v15):** an item where the word immediately before
the blank is also the first word of every option, or the word immediately after the blank
is also the last word of every option, is DROPPED at pre-flight.

**With-source render:** `BLIND=bare node bank-helper.mjs blind <frozen batch>`, asserted
before any grader sees it: no item's `subskill` string, and none of the words "dash pair",
"boundar", "agreement", "closing", "opening" appears in the render. A render that fails the
assertion is not used.

**Grader brief:** v15's phase-1 brief verbatim. It does NOT contain the v14 sentence that
options "often repeat the word before the blank"; it asks graders to flag `doubled_word`.

**Two phases.** Phase 1 cold (no key in existence for the grader) is snapshotted (sha)
before the key file is written; phase 2 adds key / key_ok / cold_miss / path_coherent /
recommend_drop / drop_reason. Phase-1 fields are verified unchanged after phase 2; a
grader that changed a phase-1 field has its phase-1 snapshot used.

## Ceiling check
The +10 bar can fire only while the control is at or below 90%. **Control above 90%: the
blind half is reported NOT MEASURED, not passed.** Control at or below its own
best-fixed-letter line + 5 points: likewise NOT MEASURED.

## Bars
- **Blind (batch level):** candidate minus matched control <= **+10** points. Above that,
  the batch is HELD whole.
- **Unanimity** is a batch-level rate against the control's rate only (A53); never a
  per-item drop on its own.
- **Per-item drop** (gate-verdict.mjs rules 1-6, rule 7 applied from the same files):
  1. key disputed by any grader (Conventions also needs 3/3 key votes at insert)
  2. not exclusive by majority
  3. distractors weak by majority
  4. median of 2+ of 3 distractors free-strikable
  5. explanation path wrong per any grader
  6. a grader-majority resolving word within four words after the blank
  7. a grader majority marks `matches_open_mark: true`
- **Elimination (A23):** candidate minus matched live control eliminable-rate <= +20
  points, control <= 80%, n >= 12 per arm; else the stage does not pass. Run on all 16
  authored items (as v15) so n >= 12 regardless of drops.
- **Difficulty:** panel MEDIAN of the three graders, never the author's label.
  **Panel-median easy is DROPPED, not relabelled.**
- **Conservative extensions (as v9-v15):** any item a grader explicitly recommends
  dropping is dropped; any item a majority of graders call off the digital SAT SEC
  blueprint is HELD for Andy, not inserted. A frozen item flagged by sec-near-dup.mjs is
  HELD.
- **Cross-item tell:** a heuristic named by a majority of graders is recorded against the
  batch even if no item drops on it. Two kept items named as one template are both
  inserted and the pair recorded (nothing enforces one-per-form).
