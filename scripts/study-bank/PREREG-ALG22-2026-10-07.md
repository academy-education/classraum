## What changes from v19 (and what does not)

Same target definition, same graders' question. Changes:

1. **No pre-gate auditor and no repair round.** Four times a repair created
   the next tell (A51, A58/A59 twice, A70 B6/B7). Pipeline is author ->
   mechanical pre-flight -> gate. The author may fix a MECHANICAL pre-flight
   failure (sandbox mismatch, sign pair, hub, extremity) before freeze; no
   model reader sees any item before freeze.
2. **Construct quota.** No more than 2 items per construct family across the
   14, because A70 found the one reliably-hard shape (solution count with a
   trap candidate) becomes a template above about one per form.
3. **Author self-audit with the stem visible** for the five no-arithmetic
   eliminations the 2026-09-24 corollary names: range bound readable from a
   printed number, closure of the option set under the asked operation,
   adjacency (exactly one adjacent pair on a greatest/least-integer stem),
   sign pairs, monotonicity. This is a pre-flight aid only — per CLAUDE.md
   an author's declared constraints eliminate nothing and are NOT a check.
   The with-source graders decide.
4. **Near-duplicate check against ALL live SAT Math rows, paged** (new
   `check-math-near-dup.mjs`, break-tested on a live row before use).

## Pipeline

author (two halves, separate Claude subagents) -> `verify` (sandbox recomputes
every key and every `distractor_solve`) -> pre-flights -> FREEZE (sha recorded)
-> gate, both halves run in parallel on the frozen file:

- **blind half**: options-only render, three samples of one Claude solver;
- **with-source half**: three fresh Claude graders. Stage 1 on a COLD file
  (stem + options, no key, no explanation): solve, list free eliminations,
  exclusivity, difficulty, blueprint. Stage 1 is written to disk BEFORE
  stage 2 opens the keyed file (explanations + distractor paths).

No GPT model anywhere.

## Ceiling check

Non-`v2` hard Algebra controls have measured 21.4% (A68) and 33.3% (A70).
The +10 bar can fire while the control is at or below 90%. If the control
returns above 90% the blind half is NOT MEASURED, not passed. If the control
returns at its letter line the bar stands as written.

## Bars

- **Blind:** candidate minus matched control <= +10 points. Unanimity is a
  batch-level RATE against the control's rate (three samples of one solver),
  never a per-item drop by itself. Above +10, nothing is inserted.
- **Per-item drop**, any one (three graders):
  1. key disputed by any grader (stage-1 cold answer != key after re-check)
  2. not exclusive by majority
  3. distractors weak by majority
  4. median >= 2 of 3 distractors free-strikable without arithmetic
     (with stem visible), or the KEY free-strikable by any grader
  5. a `distractor_solve` / explanation path that does not produce its value
- **EXT-1:** any item a grader explicitly recommends dropping is dropped.
- **HOLD:** any item a grader majority calls off the published SAT Algebra
  blueprint is held for Andy (`.held.batch.json`), not inserted.
- **Difficulty:** banked label = three-grader PANEL MEDIAN, never the
  author's. Question to graders (verbatim from v18/v19): "hard" means the item
  belongs on the module-2 hard route because it has a decision point that
  routine procedure (including Desmos) does not bypass; "medium" means
  careful multi-step procedure reaches the key. Easy is rejected
  (BANK_BAND default hard), not relabelled.

  forms at 407/15). They do not count toward the hard route.
- **0 panel-median-hard survivors = FAILED HARD COMMISSION**, reported as such
  regardless of insert count.
- **1-5 hard: PARTIAL**, inserted; hard route stays 8.
- **>= 6 hard: form 9 bought**, confirmed only by re-running
  `verify-sat-hard-route.ts math 10` and `form-capacity.mjs`.
- A class of free elimination the graders find that the author self-audit
  named and still missed is recorded against the brief, not just the item.
- No repair after freeze. Nothing re-enters the gate.
