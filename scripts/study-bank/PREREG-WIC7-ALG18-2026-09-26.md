## Bars
- Blind: candidate minus matched control <= +10 points. Where both arms sit far
  above their letter line the instrument saturates and the with-source half
  decides (standing rule).
- Per-item drop, any one: key disputed by any grader; not exclusive by majority;
  distractors weak by majority; a grader-majority resolving word within four of
  the blank; explanation path that does not produce its value (maths). Unanimity
  is NOT a drop condition (A53). Median of three on strike counts.
- Conservative extensions, stated in advance as in v17: any item a grader
  explicitly recommends dropping is dropped; any item a majority of graders call
  off the published blueprint is HELD for Andy, not inserted.
- WIC specific: if a majority of graders find that "pick the plainest word"
  beats chance across the batch, that is a batch-level tell and is recorded
  against the batch even if no single item drops on it.
