## What is new (A91's lessons, each with its mechanism)

1. **FAR: a REAL distractor from the far side of every extreme key (A91's largest drop
   class: one-sided / single-bound on extreme keys, 9 of 13 drops; D06 B03 C09 A10 Q02 A07
   B08 S05 and A10).** mf28's rule 5 required a `counter_error` past the key, but it is
   not an option, so it changed nothing a student sees; A91 found every such drop's three
   distractors were "forgot a constraint" shortfalls. **An option cannot sit past an
   extreme key BY VALUE** (the key would stop being extreme and break its slot), so "far
   side" is defined by the ERROR: `mf29-checks.mjs` FAR, gating -
   - FAR0 every distractor declares `error_sense`: `omits` (leaves out a constraint, term,
     fee, condition or step; stops early) | `adds` (double-counts, applies a step twice, adds
     a term that does not belong, ignores a reducing cap) | `swaps` (sign, inversion, swapped
     quantity, wrong operation or base);
   - FAR1 on an S/L key the three distractors are not all `omits` (A91's set) and not all
     `adds` (its mirror);
   - FAR2 `far_distractor {option, natural_side, why}` names a REAL distractor built from an
     error of a kind that ordinarily lands on the FAR side of the key (`natural_side` "above"
     on L, "below" on S), whose declared direction is the near side, and `why` says how it
     lands there in this item.
   **This is a declaration, and a declaration is not a check** (CLAUDE.md, 2026-09-24: an
   author declares the constraints it has already satisfied). The check is (a) my hand read
   of every `(read) FAR` line at stage 0 - would a student who recognises that error sign it
   on the near side from the stem without computing? a yes is a return - and (b) after
   freeze, the graders' `one_sided` / `single_bound_kills_all` / EXT-1 on extreme keys,
   which is the measurement. FAR could not be calibrated on any prior batch (no prior item
   carries `error_sense`; it gates all 58 of mf28's frozen items for that reason alone).
   Plus A91's **cheapest-stem-bound read**: every extreme item states it in
   `cheap_bound_check` (printed as `(read) BOUND`), and I read each at stage 0: one bound
   readable from the stem that puts all three distractors on its far side is a return.
2. **DSUM: an option pair joined by a SUM of two printed numbers BLOCKS (A91 R01: key 24 =
   option 7 + (8 + 9)).** mf28's DPAIR gated only a RATIO equal to a derived number and
   listed differences as `(read)`; R01 passed stage 0 and fell to grader F's EXT-1.
   `mf29-checks.mjs` DSUM (gating): the key and an option differ by a + b for two
   different printed integers a, b >= 2 whose sum is not itself printed (a printed one is
   PAIR's). The sum of key and option equal to such a number stays `(read)`. Selftest 13/13
   (R01 gated by DSUM and not by DPAIR or PAIR; a clean item; a printed sum left to PAIR; ten
   FAR cases). **Calibration, recorded before use:** DSUM gates 9 of mf28's frozen 58 (R01,
   C09 and Q02 dropped at the gate; A04 A08 C08 C10 D01 Q04 kept), 14 of algf27's frozen 50
   and 13 of adv25's frozen 59. So it also refuses items the graders passed: an authoring
   constraint designed around with the part check, not a claim that every hit is a tell.
3. **Graders write `option_pair_tell: null`, never prose** (A91: 25 single-grader flags
   were read, most a "not a tell" sentence in the field). Brief only; the verdict still
   counts any truthy value.
4. **Scratch outside the repo.** Author parts, author scratch and the graders' private
   folders live in `WORK` = the session scratchpad's `sat-math-v29-full-work/` (constant
   `WORK` in `mf29-slots.mjs`); author batch files, renders, snapshots and every grader /
   solver output that ships are written to `scripts/study-bank/` as before.
5. **The insert file is written stripped** (A91 process note 4): `mf29-kept.mjs --write`
   writes `sat-math-v29-full.kept.batch.json` with the strip allow-list only; the gate files
   keep the full frozen items.

Everything else is mf28's, unchanged: PLUG (P1-P3, the key at try >= 3 on a substitutable
ask, the hand read of every `(read) PLUG` line), DPAIR, PAIR / RUN / DIR, one part file per
item with the per-item `mf29-part-check.sh` (sandbox, slot, mf29-checks, near-dup, interior
niceness), grader private folders with the stage-1 snapshot / chmod 444 / hash before the

## Stage 0 — pre-flight, per author file, BEFORE the one return

`mf29-assemble.mjs <x>` folds the parts; `mf29-preflight.sh <file>` gates: `math-bank-helper
verify` (N/N keys, 3N/3N distractors; mutation of one key and one distractor body refused, on
the first file in), `mf29-slots.mjs`, **`mf29-checks.mjs` (PAIR, RUN, DIR, DPAIR, DSUM,
PLUG, FAR)**, `check-sign-pair`, `check-key-is-composition`, `check-explanation-hygiene`,
`check-interior-niceness` (any parity / KEY-CLEANER / complement / denominators line is a
return), `stem-duplicates`, `check-math-near-dup`, `check-duplicate-option-sets`,
merge-halves CLOSURE on the single file. Reported and read: both hub checks,
`key-rank-position`, `check-key-magnitude`, `check-key-arith-class`, `math-mechanism-dup`
(FLAG and NEAR).

By hand, on EVERY author's file: (a) every extreme-key item: could a student read the
direction of the natural errors from the stem and strike all three without solving? (b)
every `(read) FAR` line: is the named far-side error really one a student would sign past
the key? (c) every `(read) BOUND` line: does one cheap stem bound strike all three? (d)
every `(read) PLUG` item: could a student check an option against the stem in one routine
step? (e) every `(read)` PAIR / DPAIR / DSUM line. A yes is a return. Plus my hand keyword
search per item (`mf29-kw.mjs`), all recorded in `mf29.stage0.json` / `mf29.dupsearch.json`.

**One return per author**, carrying every stage-0 finding at once; the returned items re-run
all of stage 0. An item still failing after its return is **dropped before freeze**.
Batch-level, per domain, on the merged set: `key-extremity-gate` PASS and key-position band
(commissioned ± 3, scaled to drops): **Algebra S 9-15 / I 13-19 / L 9-15; Advanced Math S 8-14
/ I 11-17 / L 7-13.** A batch-level failure in a domain after the returns **stops that
domain** (the other proceeds).

**Re-dump before freeze** (`mf29-dump.mjs`, prior set widened to `sat-math-v28-full*`): every
live maths row added since the authoring dump is read against all items; `math-mechanism-dup`
re-runs over the live table and its NEAR list is read. A duplicate found there drops the item.

## Stage 1 — options-only attack (blind half), PER DOMAIN

`make-mf29-oo.mjs --domain alg|adv` (= make-mf28-oo.mjs; EXCLUDE adds sat-math-v29-full;
seeds 20261052 / 20261053): one live control per candidate from the same domain in the
candidate's authored band, numeric only, keys dealt flat, interleaved. Eligible today:
Algebra hard 30 / medium 139; Advanced Math hard 48 / medium 118. Three samples of one
Claude solver per render. `score-oo.mjs`.

Per domain: **HOLD that domain** if candidate minus control > **+10** points, or the
candidate unanimous-correct rate exceeds the control's by > **15** points. Ceiling: both bars
fire only while the control <= 90%; above that the domain's blind half is NOT MEASURED and that
domain stops. Unanimity alone never drops an item. An options-only result is never reported as
"does not leak".

## Stage 2 — with-source graders (decides per item)

Three fresh Claude graders (D, E, F), each over all frozen items in three runs (thirds of the
seeded order, positions 1-25 / 26-50 / 51-75; `mf29-strip.mjs` + `make-grade-render.mjs`),
under the folder protocol (`mf29-grade-stage.mjs`, `mf29-coldcheck.mjs`). Fields as mf28
(MF29-GRADER-BRIEF.md). At most 2 graders at a time.

Drop rules (`mf29-verdict.mjs` = mf28-verdict.mjs, written before any grader runs): **1** key
disputed by any; **2** not exclusive by majority; **3** distractors weak by majority; **4**
median >= 2 free strikes; **6** plug-back <= 2 routine tries by majority; **11** template of an
EARLIER item (seeded order) by >= 2; **EXT-1** any `drop_recommend`. Confirmed by me from any
one grader: key free-strikable (4), incoherent path (5), arithmetic-class tell (7), option pair
incl. a derived multiplier or a difference equal to a sum of two printed numbers (7pair),
one-sided family on an extreme key (8), interior tell (9), single bound killing all three on
an extreme key (10), a one-grader template or live duplicate (11). Every decision in
`mf29.confirm.json`. **HOLD** off-blueprint by majority. Drop signals are read from the
stage-2 files only.

Difficulty banked = three-grader panel median. **Easy cap per domain at the live mix:
Algebra easy <= max(1, round(0.020 x kept_alg)); Advanced Math easy <= max(1, round(0.030 x
kept_adv))**, earliest in the frozen order first; the rest held in `.held.batch.json`.

## Stage 4 — kept set, gate per domain, insert

`mf29-verdict.mjs --write` writes the per-domain sets; each re-runs stage 0 as a set.
**`mf29-kept.mjs` runs `key-extremity-gate` on EACH domain's kept set separately (>= 40.0%,
refusing). A domain that fails - or has fewer than 10 scorable items, which is no measurement
and not a pass - is held whole**: nothing in it inserted, no item dropped, moved or edited to
pass, no override, no re-run under another rule. Passing domains go to
`sat-math-v29-full.kept.batch.json` and are inserted once:

    BANK_BAND=mixed BANK_COHORT=sat-math-v29-full \
      node scripts/study-bank/math-bank-helper.mjs insert \
      scripts/study-bank/sat-math-v29-full.kept.batch.json scripts/study-bank/mf29.qc.json

after this batch's ledger entry is written onto `ledger.json` re-read from HEAD (only this
entry staged; nobody else's uncommitted change to the file is discarded). Then
`form-capacity.mjs`, `next-form.mjs`, `verify-sat-hard-route.ts math`. REGISTER §5 and this
batch's own ledger entry in one commit. Commit locally; no push.

## Stop rule

Stop at the first failed gate, per domain: a batch-level stage-0 bar after the return, a
stage-1 hold or a control above the ceiling, or the stage-4 extremity gate. On a stop nothing
in that domain is inserted and nothing is repaired; the result is recorded with denominators.
