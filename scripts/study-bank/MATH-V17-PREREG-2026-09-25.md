# SAT Math v17 — pre-registration, and a PIPELINE change

Written before any item exists. Four consecutive maths batches have been held
(v15-adv 18, v15-alg 10, v16-alg 14, v16-adv 16 — 58 authored, 0 inserted).
Repeating the v16 recipe with a longer brief is not a plan.

## What actually failed, across all four

Not the keys: **all 58 keys were correct**, verified by graders who solved
before reading any explanation. Not the arithmetic: every sandbox and tolerance
check passed. What failed was always the same thing —

> a solver holding the STEM and the OPTIONS, doing no arithmetic, can strike
> two or three of the distractors.

and the same party always found it: **the gate's with-source grader, never the
author.** Five batches running, the author declared the constraints it had
already satisfied and missed the live ones. The v16 Algebra author declared 1
strike where a grader found 17; the Advanced Math author declared 2 where a
grader found 20.

## The pipeline change: move the audit before the gate

    v16 (failed)   author  ->  GATE (blind + with-source)  ->  held
    v17            author  ->  INDEPENDENT AUDITOR  ->  author repairs once
                           ->  GATE with FRESH solvers and FRESH graders

The auditor does exactly one job — the no-arithmetic audit — and is not the
author and not the gate. This is legitimate where a fourth round against the
same attackers is not: the repair answers a party that does not decide, and
the gate that does decide has never seen the batch.

**The risk, named in advance:** the author overfits to the auditor's particular
list and the gate finds a different class. That is what the gate is for, and if
it happens it is recorded as a failure of this pipeline, not smoothed over.

## Brief length is itself a variable

A44 records that a brief's constraints compete for the author's attention: the
v4 words-in-context author met every batch-level rule and had nothing left for
the option sets. The v16 briefs were the longest of the session and produced
the most thorough authors — who still missed the thing the brief was mostly
about. **v17 briefs are deliberately short.** The audit carries the load the
brief was failing to carry.

## Bars, fixed now

- **Need:** 7 Algebra and 8 Advanced Math. Form 26 requires BOTH; one half
  alone buys nothing.
- **Blind:** candidate must not exceed the matched live control by more than
  **10 points**. Ceiling check: the controls measured 35.7% (Algebra) and 16.7%
  (Advanced Math) on the same instrument this week, so a +10 bar sits well
  inside the attainable range and can fire in either direction.
- **Per-item drop** (any one):
  1. key disputed by any grader
  2. not exclusive by majority of graders
  3. distractors weak by majority
  4. 2+ of 3 distractors fall to a free elimination
  5. explanation describes a path that does not produce its printed value
- **Unanimity is NOT a drop condition.** Changed today: the three blind solvers
  agree 69-72% against 27-36% under independence, so unanimity is two thirds of
  all items and cannot carry a per-item verdict. It is reported as a batch-level
  RATE against the control's rate and nothing else. (REGISTER A53.)

## Discard conditions

- If either half returns fewer than its bar, **both** are held; there is no
  partial credit, because a form needs both.
- If the gate finds a class of strike the auditor missed entirely, the pipeline
  change is recorded as failed regardless of the item count.
- No second repair round. One repair, against the auditor, before the gate.
