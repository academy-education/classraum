## What changes (v21 result)
In v21, all three graders independently named two templates, and the gate dropped 13 of 24 items on median
struck >= 2.
1. **2x2 textual design: BANNED.** Its restated-pattern pair is struck on sight, and 7 of 7 such items dropped.
   Every textual item is "no-reverse": no two options state the same comparison in opposite directions. This
   design produced v21's strongest items (A-02, B-03, C-02, D-02, D-03).
2. **"Beat every member of group G" table design: at most ONE per author**, and never with a lure from outside
   the group that is ruled out by its label alone. Each author's other two table items use different designs.
   Allowed designs:
   - a change across columns, or a ratio;
   - a trend across rows;
   - an exception to a pattern;
   - a two-condition match (meets X and Y).

   These are recorded as `meta.table_design`, all different within an author.
3. **No option set where exactly one option crosses the relevant group label.** v21's A-05 and B-05 were struck 3
   of 3 that way.

  correct no-reverse items. It now reads `it.meta?.design !== 'no-reverse'`. Break-tested by planting `2x2` on one
  item: it fires, and passes again after the file was restored.
- **Name-range rule:** generic place words (Hills, Forest, Reserve, Spit, Point, Sands, Strand, Museum, Delta,
  Range) and the common noun "Conservator" were added to its skip list.
- Quantitative items were relabelled from "Command of Quantitative Evidence" to the live label "Command of
  Evidence", the same fix as II21 Amendment 2. The first freeze, sha 6f0553d6, was replaced before any reader saw
  it.
