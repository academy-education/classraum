# MAP: five clean tests per subject — why it stalled, and the plan

Written 2026-10-23/24. Read-only research: no DB writes, no git. Every bank number was measured that day with
`bank-state.mjs counts|sittings` and a read-only, paged census of every `family='map'` row (153 rows; server
count asserted). Form depth comes from `map-v2-work/map-form-depth.ts` (new, read-only): it replays the real
`drawByPassage`, `ITEMS_PER_PASSAGE` and `MAP_AREA_QUOTAS` with a copy of `drawMapAreas`, and it counts a form
as complete only when every instructional area fills its whole quota from unseen items. Break-test: the projection
below gives 5/5/5; taking one item (or one passage) away from ANY single area gives 4.

Source policy, unchanged: no NWEA item, live or reconstructed. The Herald PDFs are harvested live NWEA items and
were not opened. The citable spec is NWEA's public CCSS instructional-area PDF (structure only). Claude only.

## 1. Part 1 — why MAP stalled

### 1.1 How a MAP form is assembled (src/lib/study)

- `admission-tests.ts`: `AdmissionFamily` includes `'map'`; three untimed blocks (Reading 39, Language Usage 39,
  Math 40; `minutes: 0`, four choices). `MAP_AREA_QUOTAS`: Reading Literary 13 (by passage) / Informational 13 (by
  passage) / Vocabulary 13 (one per group); Language Writing / Grammar-Usage / Mechanics 13 each; Math 10 per area.
  `ITEMS_PER_PASSAGE.map = 3`.
- `assemble.ts` `assembleAdmissionSection` → `drawMapAreas`: every verified, unarchived `family='map'` row of the
  section, ranked unseen-first, each area drawn to its quota; a thin area backfills from the rest of the block with
  a console warning (so a short area produces a full-length but off-blueprint form, silently to the student).
- **No grade level, no RIT band, no difficulty in the draw.** Owner decision Q1 (2026-10-12): one launch level,
  grades 5-8, so the draw takes every MAP row; items carry `verify_meta.map.grade_target` and tier for later.
  No adaptivity (fixed forms; MAP's CAT cannot be honestly reproduced without calibrated difficulties).
- MAP is wired end to end but behind the coming-soon gate (`shipped-tests.ts`), so a released row reaches no
  student until the flip.

## 2. Part 2 — the route that works, per subject, with the evidence

### 2.1 Math: the SAT/ACT sandbox route (decidable), made general from map-math-g58-v5

**Why.** Maths is the one family where keys are exact: `math-bank-helper.mjs verify` recomputes every key and every
distractor in a sandbox, and the inserters refuse a batch whose keys sit at an extreme under 0.8 x chance. The
options-only attack *discriminates* on numeric maths (CLAUDE.md: "the instrument discriminates ... the maths
families"; MAP v1 control 15.6-28.9%, v3 matched ISEE 19-39%), so it is a valid half-gate, and the with-source read
supplies the half it cannot see (stem + options free eliminations, CLAUDE.md 2026-09-24). ACT Math batches on this
route keep 78-83% (v16 45/54, v18 36/46); MAP's own passing cells kept 63-80% (v1 38/60, v3 Oper/Geo 45/50).

**What changes from v1-v5 (each from the record):**
- One cell per author (area x grade), 11 in parallel, 221 slots in one batch — not 2-3 authors and 40-60 slots.
- LONE everywhere; **FORM and SLIP refusing only in Number Systems** (they took it from +19.4 to +1.8 and a pass);
  advisory elsewhere, because v3 Operations (+3.6) and Geometry (+0.0) passed without them and v5's yield fell
  under them.
- ~70% end-position slots (v5: 68% authored → 50% kept; v4 60% without the end-key recipe → 14%).
- The pre-freeze screen RETURNS once, never drops on one screener (v2's asymmetry).
- Freeze and every bar per area; no batch-wide stop; no n>=10 hold — the inserter's own extremity gate is per batch
  of >= 10 numeric items, so freeze each area's kept items as its own insert batch.
- Insert `verified=true` (MAP stays behind the coming-soon flag, so this is ready-for-launch, not live).

**Targets.** Areas and grades as in §1.2 (13 per grade per area). Difficulty maps by **tier within grade**
(low / mid / high = most / about half / stronger students of that grade), stored as `difficulty` easy / medium /
hard and `verify_meta.map.rit_band_target`: grade 5 190-219, grades 6-7 200-229, grade 8 210-239 (low/mid/high
bands of 10). These are authoring targets, never shown to students and never validated without response data.

### 2.2 Language Usage: the v6 method, extended

**What worked:** Mechanics constructions (2x2 grids) held across pilots 3-4 (9/10, 8/10) and passed in v3
Punctuation (11/12) and v6 Spelling (26/28, every bar). What made v6 pass where v5 stopped: the construction was
**assigned per slot and the freeze filled cells**, so no countable feature (length, vowels, capitals) could carry
the key. **What failed:** model difficulty bars (an invalid instrument, §1.3), per-strand statistics at n=10,
rule-of-the-week fixes that spawned the next tell, and the two vocabulary rules (now in Reading, with the rule the
human sittings accepted). Agents do well on mechanics, usage and vocabulary (`agent-reading-comprehension-limit`).

**Method:** seeded construction per slot — Writing: key length rank (all four lengths >= 2 characters apart) or
position-only options (placement, irrelevant sentence, which carry nothing without the paragraph); grids: the two
error **site kinds** (add / remove / sub, never add+add or remove+remove, which make the key the shortest or
longest — the v4 grammar tell); Capitalization: case pattern (half mixed, a quarter each both-over/both-under —
the v4 corner tell). `lu-check.mjs` refuses any slot built otherwise (break-tested: wrong site kinds, a
both-errors option that is not the union, wrong case pattern and wrong length rank each fail).

**Targets:** Writing 65 (organization: transitions, placement, irrelevant sentence, intro/conclusion/support;
language: precise word, combining, style/audience, concise revision), Grammar 65 (Parts of Speech; Phrases,
Clauses, Agreement, Sentences; L.5.1-L.8.1 by grade), Mechanics +28 (Punctuation L.5.2-L.8.2 at all grades,
Capitalization grades 5-6 only). Per grade about 16 Writing and 16 Grammar.

### 2.3 Reading: the SSAT/ISEE agent brief + grader v2 + spot runs

**Why:** it is the only reading route with human evidence of working. MAP draft-1 (in-session brief, the same
construction) went 24/24 "only defensible", blind 20.8%. ISEE and SSAT agent batches written to
`SSAT-READING-AGENT-BRIEF.md` with the co-founder's rules are clearing his sittings at spot level, and the
co-founder-standard grader v2 was promoted on 2026-10-23 (second_defensible recall validated; other checks are hold
signals only). **Adapted to MAP:** 3 items per passage (one theme/central-idea + two other kinds of the passage's
own area), grade-sized passages (170-380 words), four choices, no attitude-word table, and Vocabulary as
**standalone** items (V1 grades 5-6 / V2 grades 7-8, a-m / n-z word split; multiple-meaning distractors are the
other senses of the word, the rule the human sittings accepted).
