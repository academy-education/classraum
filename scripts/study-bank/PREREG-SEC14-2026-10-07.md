## Brief (what is new against v13)
12 items, two independent Claude authors (never GPT), 6 items each, disjoint
construction lists; every distractor grammatical in isolation and wrong only in
context; no two items on one template; key letters flat; explanations quote options.

Recorded v13 tells the brief must break, across the merged 12:
- **One template.** v13 used "a long fronted phrase delays the deciding clause"
  throughout. v14: **at most 3 of 12** items may delay the deciding clause with a long
  fronted phrase.
- **Boundaries only.** v14: **at least 3 of 12** items are Form, Structure, and Sense
  (agreement, pronoun, verb form/tense, modifier, possessive/plural), each with a
  second rule interacting (e.g. agreement under inversion or across a clause containing
  plural nouns). At most 9 boundary items.
- **"Match the mark already open."** v13 lost A-04, B-03, B-06 to it, v12 both dash
  keys. v14: **no item whose key is decided by the partner of a mark already in the
  sentence** (closing a dash/comma/parenthesis pair, or copying a mark used in a
  parallel slot elsewhere). Authors must not build it; graders are asked for it (rule 7).
- **Added-word tell.** v12: added word never the key; v13: added JOINING word always the
  key. v14: options that add a word appear in at least 6 items; an added-word option is
  the key in **2 to 4** items and WRONG in at least as many; at least 2 of the wrong
  added-word options are joining words (coordinator, subordinator or relative).
- **Mark family histogram (v11 tell, kept):** no single mark family is the key in more
  than 3 of 12; at least 5 different families appear as keys (comma, semicolon, colon,
  period, no mark, comma+coordinator, ", however," / transitional, dash, a form choice);
  a period option is WRONG in >= 3 items; every mark that is a key somewhere is a
  distractor somewhere. A dash key is allowed only where no open dash decides it; if
  none survives authoring, that is recorded, not forced.
- **v12 drops, kept:** no answer-resolving word within four words after the blank; no
  free-strike distractors (aim for <= 1 of 3 strikable without parsing); explanation
  path correct.
- **Near-duplicates.** Each author runs `sec-near-dup.mjs` on its file against ALL live
  SEC rows (paged, count-asserted: 363 today, passage-or-prompt text) and changes any
  item with passage trigram Jaccard >= 0.20 to a live row, or an option skeleton set
  identical to a LIVE HARD item or to any v13 item, BEFORE delivery. Topics must not
  repeat any v10-v13 topic.

## Pipeline
two authors -> merge -> **freeze** (sha recorded) -> structural pre-flight (bank-helper
check, key spread, length ranks, mark-family key histogram, added-word count, fronted-
phrase count, sec-near-dup.mjs re-run on the frozen file, read by hand) ->
options-only blind attack (3 fresh solver samples) + 3 fresh with-source graders
(ws-d/e/f) -> gate-verdict.mjs + rule 7 -> elimination-paired.mjs --batch (A23 bar) ->
mk-rw-qc.mjs -> ledger -> insert as rw-v14-sec-hard -> verify-sat-hard-route.ts +
form-capacity.mjs. **No audit-and-repair round, and no repair after freeze.** Anything a
stage after freeze catches (pre-flight included) is dropped or held, never repaired and
re-counted. Every agent is Claude.

**With-source render:** `BLIND=bare node bank-helper.mjs blind <frozen batch>`, and it is
asserted before any grader sees it that **no item's `subskill` string, and none of the
words "dash pair", "boundar", "agreement", "closing", "opening", appears in the render**
(v13's inherited render named the key in each header). A render that fails the
assertion is not used.

## Ceiling check
A "candidate exceeds control by more than 10" bar can fire only while the control is at
or below 90%. **If the control returns above 90% the blind half is reported NOT MEASURED,
not passed.** If the control returns at or below its own best-fixed-letter line + 5
points, the blind half is likewise reported NOT MEASURED.

## Bars
- **Blind (batch level):** candidate minus matched control <= **+10** points. Above that,
  the batch is HELD whole.
- **Unanimity** is a batch-level rate against the control's rate only (A53); never a
  per-item drop on its own.
- **Per-item drop** (gate-verdict.mjs rules 1-6, rule 7 applied from the same files):
  1. key disputed by any grader (Conventions also needs 3/3 key votes at insert)
  2. not exclusive by majority
  3. distractors weak by majority
  4. median of 2+ of 3 distractors free-strikable
  5. explanation path wrong per any grader
  6. a grader-majority resolving word within four words after the blank
  7. **NEW:** a grader majority marks `matches_open_mark: true` (the key is decided by
     matching a punctuation mark already in the sentence)
- **Elimination (A23, elimination-paired.mjs --batch):** candidate minus matched live
  control eliminable-rate <= +20 points, control <= 80%, n >= 12 per arm; else the stage
  does not pass. If fewer than 12 survive the with-source rule, the stage is run on all
  authored items before drops so n >= 12, and reported as such.
- **Difficulty:** panel MEDIAN of the three graders, never the author's label.
  **Panel-median easy is DROPPED, not relabelled.**
- **Conservative extensions (as v9-v13):** any item a grader explicitly recommends
  dropping is dropped; any item a majority of graders call off the digital SAT SEC
  blueprint is HELD for Andy, not inserted. A frozen item flagged by sec-near-dup.mjs
  (passage >= 0.20 or skeleton identical to a live hard item) is HELD.
- **Cross-item tell:** if a majority of graders name the same batch-level heuristic, it
  is recorded against the batch even if no item drops on it.
