## Item 1

Strand: Analyze Theme and Literary Elements; Summarize | Grade target: 6 | Target band: RIT 190-199

Passage:

Amara wanted fresh bread for dinner, and she wanted it fast. She mixed the flour, water, and yeast in a big bowl. Then she kneaded the dough hard, punching it again and again as if that would make it rise sooner. After only ten minutes, she shoved the pan into the hot oven. When the timer finally rang, the loaf came out flat and as heavy as a brick. Her grandmother looked at it carefully but did not scold her. "The dough needs time to grow," she said quietly. The next morning, Amara mixed a new batch and covered the bowl with a clean towel. She set it beside the sunny kitchen window, sat down with a book, and let a whole hour pass. That evening, the loaf was tall, golden, and soft. Amara cut the first slice for her grandmother.

Question: Which sentence best states a theme of the passage?

A. Hard work always pays off in the end.
B. Some things turn out best when given time.
C. Being kind matters more than being right.
D. Family members should share what they make.

Passage:

When a honeybee discovers a good patch of flowers, it flies back to the hive to share the news. A bee cannot talk, so instead it dances. The bee walks in a figure-eight pattern across the honeycomb, wiggling its body as it moves. The direction of the wiggle points the other bees toward the flowers. The length of the wiggle tells them how far away the flowers are, and a long wiggle means a long trip. Because the hive is dark inside, other bees crowd close to feel the dance. Then they fly out and find the flowers on their own.

D. Honeybees wiggle for a longer time when the flowers are far away.
