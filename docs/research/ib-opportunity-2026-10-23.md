# Should Classraum build IB prep? Decision memo

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Recommendation:** **No-go on an IB product for now.** At most, run a demand-gated micro-pilot: IB Biology (or Chemistry) Paper 1A multiple choice, English only, SL+HL. Start it only if a pre-commitment test clears a set bar (section 7).

Figures are dated. "Est." marks my own arithmetic or judgement, not a published number.

## 2. What IB assessment actually is

- **Internal assessment (IA) is 20–30% of every subject.** Teachers mark it and IB moderates. Self-practice cannot reach it.
- **Maths AA and AI (first exams 2021)**
  - SL: Paper 1 (no calculator) 40% and Paper 2 (GDC) 40%.
  - HL: Papers 1 and 2 at 30% each, plus Paper 3 at 20%, which is two long problem-solving questions.
  - The Exploration IA is 20% ([Save My Exams](https://www.savemyexams.com/learning-hub/exam-guides/how-many-ib-maths-aa-papers/); [Nord Anglia guide](https://img.nordangliaeducation.com/resources/asia/_filecache/e17/178/185784-mathematics-maa-and-mai-hl-sl.pdf)).
  - Each paper has Section A (short) and Section B (extended), marked for **method** as well as the answer.
  - **There is no multiple choice in IB Maths at all.**
- **Biology, Chemistry, Physics (new syllabus, first exams 2025)**
  - Paper 1 (36%) combines **1A multiple choice** and **1B data-based questions**. Paper 2 (44%) is short and extended response. The IA is 20% ([Save My Exams, Chemistry](https://www.savemyexams.com/learning-hub/exam-guides/how-many-ib-chemistry-papers/); [RevisionDojo, Physics](https://www.revisiondojo.com/ib/ib-physics-new)).
  - One source lists 30 MC on SL Paper 1A. HL item counts were not verified.
  - **1A is roughly half of Paper 1, so about 15–20% of the subject grade** (est.). It is the only real MC in the DP.
- **Essay and rubric subjects:** English A (guided analysis and comparative essay), History (source paper plus essays), TOK (1,600-word essay plus exhibition), the EE (4,000 words), and every IA.
- **MYP eAssessment** is optional and on-screen. In May 2026 more than 101,000 students at 359 schools in 82 countries took it ([IB](https://ibo.org/news/news-list/more-than-101000-middle-years-programme-myp-students-receive-their-results/); [subject list](https://ibo.org/programmes/middle-years-programme/assessment-and-exams/onscreen-examinations/available-subjects/)). Few Korean public MYP schools appear to use it (**not verified**).
- **Subject sizes worldwide, May 2026** (bulletin p.25):

  | Subject | HL | SL |

  | Maths AI | 8,500 | 44,196 |

## 3. Can our pipeline generate and auto-grade it?

| Component | Fit with our pipeline | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sciences Paper 1A MC | **Good**, mechanically | Same shape as ACT Science and SAT MC |
| Sciences Paper 1B data-based / Paper 2 short answer | **Partial** | Numeric answers can be key-checked. Explanation marks need rubric grading |
| Maths Section A short answer | **Partial** | Final answers can be key-checked in the SAT-math sandbox. Method marks, which make up much of the IB score, cannot be checked that way |
| Maths Section B / HL Paper 3 | **Poor** | Multi-part "show that" questions and proofs |
| English A, History, TOK, EE, IAs | **Poor today** | Needs a rubric grader. Ours fails calibration on ETS's own samples (mean −1.5 bands, see CLAUDE.md). IB criteria are IB copyright, and IB's academic-integrity rules make IA/EE "feedback" products sensitive |

Two lessons from our own bank argue against this content:

- **The blind attack does not work on science MC.** Our ACT Science run measured the model's science knowledge rather than item leakage: 88.9% model versus 28.6% human on the shipped bank. IB science MC is the same kind of content. The gate would therefore depend on the with-source grade and a **human sitting by someone who knows the IB syllabus**. We have no such reviewer; the co-founder reviews SAT, ACT and TOEFL.
- **Korean-language content is a second bank.** It would serve the public-school segment, but with IB command terms in Korean and terminology that must match IB's Korean papers, it is a second bank, not a translation pass.

## 4. IP and legal

These points come from IB's *Rules for use of IB Intellectual Property*, updated 2026-02-19 ([ibo.org](https://www.ibo.org/terms-and-conditions/intellectual-property/)).

- **Copyright:**
  - IB claims copyright over exam questions, question banks and markschemes.
  - It also claims copyright over guide content: syllabi, topics, command terms and assessment criteria.
  - Third parties with no relationship to IB may not reproduce any of it without a written licence (copyright@ibo.org).
  - Past papers bought from the Follett IB Store are for "strictly personal and non-commercial use".
- **Trademark:**
  - "IB" and "International Baccalaureate" may not appear in a product name, title, domain or logo.
  - Referential use in descriptive text is allowed, provided no endorsement is implied. IB recommends a set disclaimer ("developed independently from and is not endorsed by the International Baccalaureate Organization…").
  - A product called "Classraum IB" would not comply. Copy such as "practice for IB Diploma Biology" would.
- **What competitors do:** Revision Village, Save My Exams and RevisionDojo write **original** "exam-style" questions mapped to syllabus topics and carry the disclaimer. Endorsed publishers (Kognity, OUP, Pearson) hold IB "In Cooperation" licences.
- **For us:** original agent-written items are the right model, and we never ingest past papers or markschemes, which suits our pipeline. The same caution as our MAP rule applies: never author or validate against harvested IB papers.

## 6. Fit and effort

- **Different shape from our current tests:**
  - SAT, ACT and TOEFL are one blueprint each, with MC or grid-in answers, and the whole score is practisable.
  - IB is 6 subjects × SL/HL. A Biology student and an Economics student share nothing, and MC is 0% of maths and about 15–20% of each science.
  - Our "five clean tests per section" floor would apply per subject and level.
- **MVP options:**
  - **(a) Sciences 1A MC only:** Bio, Chem and Phys, with HL items forming a superset of SL.
  - **(b) Add Maths AA Section A** short answers, key-checked by the sandbox.
- **Authoring effort, est.:**
  - One subject-level at 5 forms × 30–40 MC is 150–200 items, plus topic coverage across about 20 subtopics. That is **about one SAT domain's worth per subject-level**.
  - Three sciences at SL+HL come to about 600–1,000 items. That is **close to the size of our entire SAT R&W bank (1,009 items)**, and the gate is weaker on it (section 3).
  - Adding Maths AA Section A adds about 300+ items and a method-marking gap.
  - The Korean-language track roughly doubles all of this.
- **Revenue, est.:**
  - Assume 15% of Korea's ~1,600 DP students pay ₩189,000 a year (our Premium Annual): **about ₩45M a year (about US$33k)**.
  - It only reaches ₩90M even at an aggressive 30%.
  - Selling into the roughly 18k-a-year Asian English-language market means competing head-on with Revision Village and Save My Exams on price and depth, with no distribution advantage. Plausible capture is small (not modelled).

## 7. Recommendation

**No-go on building an IB product now.**

**Why:**
1. **The Korean market is tiny.** About 810 exam-year students a year, or about 1,600 enrolled. The fast public-school growth is in PYP and MYP and will not reach DP volume for years.
2. **Our MC strength covers little of the grade.** It reaches about 15–20% of a science grade and 0% of maths, while 20–30% is IA and much of the rest is extended or essay response.
3. **Incumbents are cheap, deep and entrenched** ($249 one-time, about £4/mo).
4. **Our quality gate is weakest on this content.** The science blind attack saturates, and we have no IB-qualified human reviewer.
5. **Opportunity cost.** The same authoring effort is about one SAT R&W bank, which serves a much larger Korean test-prep market. That market was **not sized in this memo**; check it before using it as the comparator.

**If you want a cheap test instead (narrow, demand-gated):**
- **Step 0, no content:** ask partner hagwons and international-school contacts, and add an "IB Biology/Chemistry practice: notify me" toggle on the study page. **Bar: at least 50 Korean DP students or 3 academies pre-commit within 6 weeks.**
- **If the bar is met:** build **IB Biology Paper 1A MC, English, HL with SL as a subset**, 5 forms (about 200 items). Original items with the IB disclaimer, a with-source key grade, and one paid IB-teacher reviewer as the human sitting.
  - Biology is the largest HL science (33,585).
  - It also has Korean-language DP status, which keeps a Korean track open later.
  - Price it as an add-on to Premium. Do not build a separate product.
- **Don't build yet:** maths method-marking, IA/EE/TOK feedback (integrity and IP exposure, and our grader is uncalibrated), Korean-language items, MYP.

**Risks if we proceed:**
- **IP:** guide text or criteria copied into items or rubrics; any product name containing "IB".
- **Quality:** a model-generated science bank that passes our gate but a teacher rejects. ACT Science showed the gate can be fooled.
- **Mis-sizing:** the November Korean-language cohort is counted but cannot use English content.
- **Policy:** public-school IB depends on superintendent politics and the 수능 timing dispute. It could stall.
- **Distraction:** about one SAT-bank of effort for a market measured in hundreds of students.

**What to watch:** the Nov 2026 bulletin's Korea count (the public DP cohort); the DP school count on the IB Korea page (39 today); any IBO licensing route for third-party question banks.
