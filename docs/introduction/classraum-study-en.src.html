What Parents Should Know

If your child uses Classraum Study, or is about to start, here is what to know about payment and cancellation.

A Receipt for Every Charge

Each renewal and each credit or pass purchase is followed by an email receipt to the account’s address. Billing history and the card slip are also in the app.

Cancel Anytime

A plan can be cancelled in the app at any time, and stays active until the end of the period already paid.

Passes That Do Not Renew

The SAT and TOEFL 3‑month passes are a single payment. When one ends, the account moves to the free plan automatically, so nothing is ever charged without you knowing.

Credits Come Back

If a test fails to start, its credits are refunded automatically. There is nothing to request.
